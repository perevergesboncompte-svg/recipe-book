Empanada de Bacalao con Pasas

Yield
Filling for 1 large empanada
Active Time
20 min
Rest
30 min (chill before assembling)
Oven
190-200 °C, about 35 min
Source
Traditional bacalao empanada — no chef or publication credited
Energy Cost
$2.24 (35 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Salt cod, desalted and flaked: 400 g
  • Large onions: 3
  • Red pepper: 1
  • Garlic cloves: 2
  • Raisins (soaked): 50 g
  • Pine nuts: 30 g
  • Crushed tomato: 150 g
  • Olive oil, pimentón, salt: to taste

Method

  1. Soak the raisins in warm water. Lightly toast the pine nuts and set both aside.
  2. Sweat the onion, garlic and pepper in oil over low heat until meltingly soft.
  3. Add pimentón and tomato, and cook down well.
  4. Fold in the flaked cod and give it a few minutes. Adjust salt carefully — the cod brings its own.
  5. Off the heat, stir through the raisins and pine nuts. Cool.
  6. Fill, seal with a repulgue and bake at 190-200 °C for about 35 min.

Notes

  • Stovetop cook time is 25 min.
  • The sweetness of raisins and pine nuts against salt cod is the signature of this Mediterranean classic.
  • Desalt the cod properly, 24-36 h with changes of water, or buy it already desalted and flaked.
  • Taste before salting. The cod sets its own level.
  • Drain the sofrito well so it does not soak the dough.
  • Filling always goes on cold.
