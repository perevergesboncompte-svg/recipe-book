Empanada de Carne Argentina

Yield
Filling for 1 large empanada (or about 12 individual ones)
Active Time
15 min
Rest
Overnight (chill before assembling)
Oven
200 °C, about 25 min
Source
Traditional Argentine empanada criolla — no chef or publication credited
Energy Cost
$1.89 (25 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Minced beef: 600 g
  • Large onions, finely chopped: 3
  • Hard-boiled eggs, chopped: 2
  • Green olives, pitted and cut into pieces: 12
  • Ground cumin: 1 tsp
  • Sweet pimentón (plus a pinch of hot): 1 tsp
  • Oregano: 1 tsp
  • Salt and pepper: no quantity given
  • Oil (or fat): 3 tbsp
  • Stock: if needed

Method

  1. Sweat the onion in oil over medium heat until translucent and soft. Do not let it brown.
  2. Raise the heat, add the beef and break it up until it loses its red colour.
  3. Season with cumin, pimentón, oregano, salt and pepper. Add a little stock if it runs dry.
  4. Off the heat, cool completely — ideally overnight in the fridge.
  5. Fold in the chopped egg and olives just before assembling.
  6. Fill, seal with a repulgue and bake at 200 °C for about 25 min until golden.

Notes

  • Stovetop cook time is 20 min.
  • Resting the filling cold overnight is the Argentine trick. The flavour settles and it handles better.
  • Cumin is the aroma that defines an empanada criolla. Do not leave it out.
  • Lots of onion relative to the meat is what keeps it juicy.
  • Egg and olives go in at the end, not while cooking, so they stay whole.
  • Some cooks add raisins for the traditional sweet-savoury note.
  • Filling always goes on cold.
