Pasta / Doughs

Cocoa Dough drafted

Makes
954 g
Active Time
8-10 min kneading
Rest
30 min minimum, wrapped
Source
Pasta, Missy Robbins

Ingredients

  • Tipo 00 flour: 470 g
  • Natural cocoa powder, sifted: 30 g
  • Egg yolks: 454 g
  • Extra 00 flour for kneading: as needed
  • Room-temperature water: up to 1 tbsp, only if dry

Method

  1. Stir the sifted cocoa into the flour until evenly coloured.
  2. Mound on a wooden surface with a well 5 to 6 inches across.
  3. Mix the yolks lightly, add half to the well, drawing in flour from the inner wall with a fork.
  4. Add the rest the same way, scraping back any escapes.
  5. Knead 8 to 10 minutes until smooth and firm.
  6. Add water by the tablespoon only if it stays dry.
  7. Wrap and rest at least 30 minutes.

Notes

  • Egg dough with 30 g of the flour swapped for cocoa, so 6% of the dry weight. Same yolk quantity.
  • Natural cocoa, not Dutch-process.
  • Keeps 24 hours. Chilled overnight, pull it out 20 minutes before rolling.
