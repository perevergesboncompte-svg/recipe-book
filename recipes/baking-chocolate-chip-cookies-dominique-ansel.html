Dominique Ansel Chocolate Chunk

Yield
14 cookies
Rest
5-10 min

350F, 9-10 min

Ingredients

Batch
  • Unsalted butter, room temperature: 198 g
  • Granulated sugar: 130 g
  • Light brown sugar, packed: 130 g
  • Eggs, room temperature: 2
  • All-purpose flour: 330 g
  • Baking soda: 5 g
  • Salt: 6 g
  • Milk chocolate chunks: 100 g
  • Semi-sweet chunks: 100 g

Method

  1. Whisk the room-temperature butter with both sugars, vigorously, until smooth with no lumps. The air beaten in here is what makes them chewy.
  2. Whisk in the eggs about 30 seconds until creamy.
  3. Fold in flour, baking soda and salt with a spatula, stopping as soon as no dry flour shows.
  4. Fold in both chocolates.
  5. Chill 5 to 10 minutes, just enough to firm up.
  6. Form 6 cm balls, place 5 cm apart, flatten each to about 1.25 cm.
  7. Bake at 350F for 9 to 10 minutes. Light golden edges, centres still gooey.
  8. Cool on the sheet 5 to 10 minutes. They deflate, which is expected.

Notes

  • One bowl, a whisk and a spatula. No mixer.
  • The shortest chill of any recipe in the set, so it is the one to make on no notice.

Learnings

Flat 3.5 across appearance, texture and flavor. The easiest of the five made and by far the fastest, since the chill is minutes rather than hours, so it wins on convenience rather than result.
