Prawn and Smoked Salmon Ravioli

Filling
Prawn and Smoked Salmon Filling
Shape
Ravioli
Serves
2
Total Time
1 h 30 min
Source
Great British Chefs
Chef
Georgina Auterac
Filling Note
Prawn and smoked salmon
Pasta Colour
Yellow
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Batch
  • Pasta — 00 flour: 200 g
  • Pasta — eggs: 2
  • Pasta — olive oil: 0.5 tbsp
  • Filling — smoked salmon: 100 g
  • Filling — double cream: 50 ml
  • Filling — cooked prawns: 180 g
  • Filling — dill, chopped: 1 tsp
  • Filling — salt and black pepper: to taste
  • Filling — semolina: for dusting
  • To Serve — butter: to taste
  • To Serve — dill, chopped: 1 tbsp
  • To Serve — lemon (juice): 1

Method

  1. Make pasta dough: In a food processor combine the flour, eggs and olive oil and blend until a coarse crumb has been formed. Pour out onto the work surface and work it into a dough. Leave to rest for at least 30 minutes at room temperature.
  2. Prepare filling: Add half of the smoked salmon and cream to a blender and blend to form a paste. Roughly chop the remaining smoked salmon and add to a bowl with the prawns and dill. Season with salt and pepper, mix well and chill in the fridge for 20 minutes.
  3. Roll pasta: Divide the pasta dough into workable pieces. Using a pasta machine, roll out the pasta, beginning with the widest setting and finishing with the second narrowest. When not using dough, make sure that it remains covered to avoid drying out.
  4. Form ravioli: Spoon about ½ teaspoon of filling at intervals along the length of the pasta. Brush a little water around each filling and lay a second rolled piece of pasta on top. Seal the pasta tightly around the filling and using a stencil cut through the pasta. Make sure your stencil is lightly floured to prevent it from sticking.
  5. Cook: Add each ravioli to a baking tray which has been lightly coated with semolina. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil. Cook the ravioli for 4-5 minutes.
  6. Serve: Gently toss in melted butter, a squeeze of lemon juice, a sprinkle of dill and season with salt and pepper.

Notes

  • Georgina shares her ideas for a romantic meal and how to make the classic Italian dish of ravioli which she stuffs with prawn and smoked salmon. The extra effort put into a home-made delicious dinner is worth more than any over-priced flowers and elaborate gifts - the way to the heart is through the stomach!
  • Chef's Tips: Never overfill the ravioli - however nice the filling is! It isn't worth it when they burst when cooking. Another way to prevent the ravioli 'erupting' in the water is to make sure when sealing that there is no air bubbles between the pasta and filling.
