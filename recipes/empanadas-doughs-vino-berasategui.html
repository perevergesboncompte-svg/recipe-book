Baking Pasta Croquetas Empanadas

Empanadas / Doughs

Masa con Vino, Berasategui

Yield
1 large empanada, base + tapa
Active Time
15 min
Rest
45 to 60 min prove
Oven
180 °C, 50 to 60 min
Source
Martín Berasategui, El Diario Vasco

Ingredients

Batch
  • Wheat flour: 600 g
  • Lukewarm water: 200 ml
  • Fresh compressed yeast (levadura prensada): 25 g
  • Oil from the relleno sofrito: 100 ml
  • White wine: 50 ml
  • Salt: 1 tsp
  • Egg, for brushing: 1

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm water.
  2. Mix the flour with the salt in a bowl, then add the yeasted water, the sofrito oil and the white wine.
  3. Knead to a smooth, elastic dough that comes away from your hands.
  4. Cover and prove 45 to 60 min until doubled, somewhere warm and out of draughts.
  5. Divide in two and roll thin, base and tapa. Fill with cold relleno and seal the edges. Cut a central chimney for the steam.
  6. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 180 °C for 50 to 60 min, until golden.

Notes

  • A fermented dough that still gets rolled thin, so it lands between springy and crisp. That is the distinguishing feature against the other bread doughs here.
  • Water hydration is 42%, counting the 200 ml water plus 50 ml wine against 600 g flour, which matches the source. The fat is the 100 ml of sofrito oil, 17% of flour weight, far less than the enriched doughs.
  • Sofrito oil as the fat is the Galician signature: it carries the relleno's flavour into the dough.
  • The white wine adds flavour and helps both the browning and the crispness.
  • Martín Berasategui, 12 Michelin stars, Lasarte-Oria.
