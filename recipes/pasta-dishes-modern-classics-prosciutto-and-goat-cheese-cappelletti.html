Prosciutto and Goat Cheese-Filled Cappelletti with Pistachios

Filling
Prosciutto, Goat Cheese and Spinach Filling
Shape
Cappelletti
Serves
4-6
Total Time
2 h plus the dough rest
Source
Missy Robbins, Pasta

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Egg dough: 1 batch
  • Prosciutto, in a single piece: 128 g
  • Ricotta: 116 g
  • Spinach leaves, lightly packed: 114 g
  • Fresh goat cheese: 168 g
  • Parmigiano-reggiano, finely grated, for the filling: 22 g
  • Egg yolk: 18 g
  • Unsalted butter, cold and cubed: 45 g
  • Raw Sicilian pistachios, very finely chopped: 31 g
  • Parmigiano-reggiano, finely grated, to finish: 22 g
  • Salt: quantity not given

Method

  1. Chill the meat grinder and all its dies for at least an hour before you grind anything.
  2. Cut the prosciutto into cubes of ½ to 1 inch and give them about 15 minutes in the freezer. They want to be colder than a refrigerator gets them, without freezing.
  3. Fit the grinder with the larger of two dies, or the middle one of three, and run the prosciutto through twice. Set it aside.
  4. Pulse the ricotta in a food processor just to a glossy, frosting-smooth state and no further. Over-processing breaks it. Scrape it into a bowl.
  5. Salt a large pot of water heavily and bring it to the boil over high heat. Set up a salted ice bath with a colander standing in it.
  6. Boil the spinach about a minute, until tender but still bright green, then move it into the ice bath to stop it cooking. Drain it, turn it out onto a kitchen towel and squeeze the water out.
  7. Chop the dry spinach finely.
  8. Combine the whipped ricotta, goat cheese, ground prosciutto, spinach and parmigiano in a bowl and mix until everything is evenly integrated. Taste and adjust with salt. It wants to taste properly seasoned. Stir the egg yolk through and refrigerate until you are ready to pipe.
  9. Make 70 to 80 cappelletti with the egg dough and the filling.
  10. Salt a large pot of water heavily and bring it to the boil over high heat.
  11. Set a large saute pan over low heat, drop the butter in and pour in 115 to 170 g of pasta water, 2 to 3 ladles. Swirl to emulsify.
  12. Slide the cappelletti into the pot and pull the heat back to a gentle simmer. A rolling boil breaks this filling. Cook 2 to 4 minutes. The thickest closure should give tenderly.
  13. Lift them across with a spider, raise the heat to medium and swirl 30 seconds to a minute, turning them with a spoon so every side is coated. A splash more pasta water loosens the sauce if it tightens. It is ready when it clings with a gloss.
  14. Divide onto plates, drizzle the sauce left in the pan over the top and finish with the pistachios and parmigiano.

Notes

  • Grind the prosciutto cold. Chilled grinder, chilled dies, 15 minutes in the freezer for the cubes, or the fat smears instead of cutting cleanly.
  • Ricotta is doing structural work here. It covers for the chalkiness of goat cheese and tempers its tang.
  • Whip the ricotta only to a frosting-like sheen. Past that point it breaks.
  • Squeeze the blanched spinach properly dry before chopping it, otherwise it loosens the filling.
  • Nuts and bread crumbs over pasta are a southern Italian habit more than a northern one. The pistachios give texture and a faint sweetness.
