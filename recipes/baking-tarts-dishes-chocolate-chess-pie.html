Chocolate Chess Pie

Dough
Flaky Tart Dough
Makes
1 pie, 23 cm, 8 to 12 servings
Active Time
20 min
Rest
4 to 6 h refrigerated before serving
Total Time
6 to 8 h
Oven
350F for 1 h
Source
Tartine, A Classic Revisited

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Flaky tart dough, partially baked and cooled in a 23 cm pie pan: 1 batch
  • Unsalted butter: 60 g
  • Dark chocolate, coarsely chopped: 70 g
  • Eggs, large: 2
  • Evaporated milk: 160 ml
  • Vanilla extract: 1 tbsp
  • Sugar: 350 g
  • Cocoa powder: 50 g
  • Salt: 0.5 tsp

Method

  1. Make the flaky tart dough, line a 23 cm pie pan with it, and bake the shell only partway. Cool it before filling.
  2. Heat the oven to 350F [180C].
  3. Pour water about 5 cm deep into a small saucepan and bring it to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat.
  4. Set the butter and the chocolate in a small heatproof bowl, one that sits firmly on the pan's rim, and rest it above the water without letting it touch. Stir with a rubber spatula until melted and smooth, then lift it off the heat.
  5. Whisk the eggs, evaporated milk and vanilla into the melted chocolate and butter.
  6. Whisk the sugar, cocoa powder and salt together in a medium bowl.
  7. Add the chocolate mixture to the dry mixture and whisk until smooth.
  8. Pour the batter into the shell and bake about 1 hour, until a paring knife pushed into the middle of the pie comes out clean.
  9. Move the pie to a wire rack to cool, then refrigerate it 4 to 6 hours before cutting.

Notes

  • A chess pie sets on eggs, butter and sugar alone, with no starch and no fruit, which makes it both sweeter and firmer than a pudding pie. It is sturdy enough to travel.
  • The shell goes in partially baked, not fully baked, since the filling takes a full hour in the oven.
  • Any partially baked pie dough works under this filling. The book matches it with the einkorn version of the flaky tart dough.
  • The 4 to 6 hours in the fridge are part of the recipe, not a resting courtesy. Cut it warm and the filling smears.
  • Well wrapped, it keeps a week in the fridge. Unsweetened whipped cream is the usual accompaniment.
