Pasta / Fillings

Aubergine and Ricotta Filling

Source
Great British Chefs
Chef
Emily Roux and Diego Ferrari

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Aubergine: 1
  • Ricotta: 40 g
  • Parmesan: 40 g
  • Shallot: quantity not given
  • Basil: quantity not given
  • Rock salt, for roasting the aubergine: quantity not given
  • Vinegar, for the optional overnight marinade: quantity not given

Method

  1. Wrap the aubergine in foil with rock salt and roast it for 1 hour at 180 C.
  2. Dice the flesh and dry it out in a pan.
  3. Mix the dried aubergine with the ricotta, Parmesan, shallot and basil.
  4. Pipe onto the pasta.

Notes

  • Used in Aubergine Ravioli with Sicilian Red Prawns.
  • No sub-components.
  • Drying the aubergine out completely is the step the source flags. Wet flesh ruins the texture once the cheese goes in.
  • Shallot and basil carry no quantities, and the rock salt is a roasting bed rather than a measured seasoning.
  • The source's tip suggests marinating the aubergine in vinegar overnight for extra flavour, with no quantity and no place in the sequence. The recipe's stated "2 hours + overnight" prep is the dough rest.
  • The 150 g smoked scamorza is a siphoned sauce foam, the 12 red prawns a grilled garnish, and the 50 ml reduced prawn bisque a sauce. All three are outside this file.
