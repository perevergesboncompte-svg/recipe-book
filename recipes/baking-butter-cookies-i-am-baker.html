Baking / Butter cookies

I Am Baker Butter Cookies

Yield
16 cookies
Oven
350F, 13-15 min
Source
I Am Baker, iambaker.net

Ingredients

Batch
  • Unsalted butter, room temperature but still cool: 227 g
  • Granulated sugar: 133 g
  • Egg yolks, room temperature: 2
  • Vanilla extract: 5 ml
  • All-purpose flour: 240 g
  • Kosher salt: 1.5 g
  • Whole milk, room temperature: 30-45 ml

Method

  1. Cream the butter and sugar on medium until light and fluffy.
  2. Mix in the yolks and vanilla.
  3. Add the whisked flour and salt on low, scraping down.
  4. Add the milk a tablespoon at a time until the dough is sticky, thick and well combined.
  5. Pipe circles through a large star tip, 1 inch apart. The batter is thick enough to need a heavy bag or two bags nested.
  6. Bake at 350F for 13 to 15 minutes until golden.
  7. Cool 10 minutes on the sheet before moving.

Notes

  • No chill at all, like the Simple Pipeable recipe.
  • Sturdy enough to decorate with chocolate or royal icing once cool.
  • Dough keeps 3 days chilled or freezes. Pipe from frozen and add a couple of minutes.
