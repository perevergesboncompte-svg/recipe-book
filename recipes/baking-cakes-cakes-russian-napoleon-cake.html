Russian Napoleon Cake

Dough
Flaky Tart Dough
Filling
Pastry Cream
Makes
1 cake, 20 cm, 8 to 10 servings
Active Time
1 h 30 min
Rest
2 h chilled, up to overnight
Total Time
about 5 h
Oven
375F for 7-8 min per sheet
Source
Tartine, A Classic Revisited

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Flaky tart dough: 2 batches
  • Pastry cream: 2 batches
  • Heavy cream, very cold: 480 ml
  • Sugar, for the whipped cream: 4 tsp
  • Berries, for serving: 600 g
  • Sugar, for the berries: 1-2 tbsp
  • Lemon juice, fresh, for the berries: quantity not given

Method

  1. Make two batches of flaky tart dough and two batches of pastry cream. Both need to be cold before the cake goes together.
  2. Heat the oven to 375F. Line four baking sheets with parchment and have four more sheet pans standing by to weight the dough.
  3. Working in batches, roll the dough out to ten rounds of 20 cm, each one 2 mm thick.
  4. Lay two rounds on each lined sheet. Cover them with a second sheet of parchment and set a sheet pan on top as a weight.
  5. Bake 4 minutes. Pull the sheet out, press gently down on the weight and the dough, and turn the sheet round.
  6. Bake a further 3 to 4 minutes, to a light gold. If they are still too pale, take the weight and parchment off and keep baking.
  7. Cool the rounds completely on a baking rack.
  8. Crumble two of the cooled rounds and keep the crumbs aside for the outside of the cake.
  9. Set one round on a cardboard cake round or a plate. Spread pastry cream over it with an offset spatula, out to the edge and a shade under 6 mm thick.
  10. Carry on stacking and spreading until eight rounds are in place. Leave the eighth bare, because that is the top of the cake.
  11. Whisk the heavy cream with the sugar on medium speed in a stand mixer fitted with the whisk, to medium peaks.
  12. Cover the top of the cake with the whipped cream and spread it with a spatula. Push the surplus gently over the edges and work it evenly down the sides.
  13. Press handfuls of the crumbled rounds onto the cream until the whole cake is coated.
  14. Refrigerate at least 2 hours, and up to overnight, until it is cold and set right through.
  15. For the fruit, toss the berries with sugar to the sweetness you want and sharpen them with a squeeze of lemon.
  16. Cut the cake into slices and spoon the berries over, if you are serving them.

Notes

  • Ten rounds get baked. Eight go into the stack and two are crumbled for the coating.
  • The second sheet of parchment and the sheet pan on top are what hold the flaky dough flat instead of letting it puff.
  • Two batches of pastry cream come to roughly 1 L, which is what eight layers at just under 6 mm take.
  • An overnight chill softens and melds the layers into the traditional texture. Served within about three hours of assembly the layers instead stay crisp and separate.
  • The cake is at its best within 24 hours of baking.
