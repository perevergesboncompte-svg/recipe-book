Croquetas

Croquetas de Jamón Ibérico, Dani García

Yield
~25 croquetas
Active Time
40 min
Rest
Several hours
Source
Dani García, Tapas Magazine

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Milk: 700 g
  • Chicken stock (jamón stock is better): 300 g
  • Butter: 50 g
  • Flour: 110 g
  • Jamón ibérico, diced: to taste
  • Hard-boiled eggs, chopped fine: 2
  • Fine and coarse breadcrumbs: as needed
  • Beaten egg: as needed
  • Salt: to taste

Method

  1. Put the butter, jamón and flour in a pan and stir over medium-high heat until the flour is cooked, about 4 min.
  2. At around the 8 min mark add the stock, stirring, then the milk a little at a time.
  3. Cook the bechamel about 25 min. Correct the salt.
  4. Off the heat, fold in the chopped boiled egg. Transfer to a buttered dish, cover, and chill.
  5. Shape with oiled hands and coat in egg and breadcrumbs, fine and coarse mixed. Chill again.
  6. Fry in hot oil until evenly golden.

Notes

  • Roux is 110 g flour : 50 g butter to 1 kg of liquid (700 g milk, 300 g stock). Heavy on flour and light on fat next to a 1:1 roux, because the stock is carrying part of the body.
  • Milk plus jamón stock at 700+300 deepens the flavour without drying the mass out.
  • Fine and coarse breadcrumbs mixed give a crisper, more irregular crust.
  • Chopped boiled egg adds body and the classic flavour.
  • Two separate chills, once for the mass and again after coating, make shaping easier and stop them splitting.
  • Oil your hands to roll so the mass does not stick.
  • Marbella, three Michelin stars in 2018.
  • Header calls for hot oil.
