Baking / Butter cookies

Old-Fashioned Butter Cookies tested

Yield
50 cookies
Rest
1 h chill after pressing
Oven
350F, 13-16 min
Source
Divas Can Cook, divascancook.com

Scorecard

4/5Appearance
3.5/5Texture
4/5Flavor
easyTechnique
4/5Overall

Ingredients

  • Unsalted butter, softened: 113 g
  • Butter-flavored shortening: 113 g
  • Granulated sugar: 150 g
  • Sea salt: 2 g
  • Egg yolks: 2
  • Vanilla extract: 7.5 ml
  • All-purpose flour: 250 g

Method

  1. Cream the butter and shortening smooth.
  2. Add sugar and salt, beat about 2 minutes until fluffy and uniform.
  3. Mix in the yolks and vanilla, scraping down.
  4. Stir in the flour a little at a time with a spatula to a soft uniform dough.
  5. Knead lightly by hand to pick up the last of the flour.
  6. Press through a cookie press, or pipe with an open star tip, packed close since the tray is going in the fridge.
  7. Chill 1 hour, then space them 1 to 2 inches apart on a fresh lined sheet.
  8. Bake at 350F for 13 to 16 minutes until the edges are golden. Watch them from 13.
  9. Leave on the pan until cool. They are fragile warm and crisp up as they cool.

Notes

  • Half the fat is shortening, which is what holds the pressed shape.
  • Highest yield of the butter cookies at 50, so it is the one for volume.
  • Overbaking dries them out. Golden edges means stop.
  • Keeps for weeks airtight.

Learnings

4 overall. Appearance and flavor both 4 but texture only 3.5, the weakest texture of the five made, which is the shortening trade-off for holding shape. Easy, and the 50-cookie yield is the real argument for it.
