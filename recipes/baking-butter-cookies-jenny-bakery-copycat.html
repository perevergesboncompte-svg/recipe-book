Baking / Butter cookies

Jenny Bakery Copycat Butter Cookies tested

Yield
15 cookies
Oven
300F, 25-30 min
Source
TheZongHan, thezonghan.com

easyTechnique
3.75/5Overall

Ingredients

  • Salted butter, softened: 150 g
  • Icing sugar: 30 g
  • Plain flour: 95 g
  • Cornstarch: 75 g

Method

  1. Cream the softened salted butter with the icing sugar until light.
  2. Sift in the flour and cornstarch.
  3. Fold gently until just combined. The dough should be soft but hold shape.
  4. Pipe through a star tip.
  5. Bake at 300F for 25 to 30 minutes, until lightly golden but still pale.
  6. Cool completely on the sheet before moving.

Notes

  • Four ingredients, the shortest list in the whole collection.
  • Cornstarch is 44 percent of the flour weight, which is what makes it melt in the mouth.
  • The lowest oven and longest bake of any butter cookie here, which is the mechanism for the soft texture.
  • Butter quality carries this one, since there is nothing else to hide behind.
  • The source gives 300F and 130C fan-forced, but 130C fan is roughly 266F, so those two do not agree. Pick one and record which worked.
  • Only 15 cookies, so scale up.

Learnings

3.75 overall. Appearance and texture both 4, but flavor 3.75 pulls it down, which is a fair result for a formula with no vanilla and nothing but butter for flavour. Lowest of the five made.
