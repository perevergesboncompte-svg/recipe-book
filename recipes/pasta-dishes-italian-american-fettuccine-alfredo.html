Pasta / Dishes

Fettuccine Alfredo

Shape
Fettuccine
Serves
4-6
Total Time
1 h plus the dough rest
Source
Missy Robbins, Pasta

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Egg dough: 1 batch
  • Unsalted buffalo's milk butter, cold and cubed: 104 g
  • Unsalted cow's milk butter, cold and cubed: 52 g
  • Black pepper: 50 grinds
  • 2-year parmigiano-reggiano, finely grated: 231 g
  • 5-year parmigiano-reggiano, coarsely grated: 120 g

Method

  1. Roll and cut 624 g of fettuccine from the egg dough.
  2. Fill a large pot with water, salt it heavily and bring it to the boil over high heat.
  3. Set a large sauté pan over low heat, then ladle in 2 to 3 ladles of the pot water, 115 to 170 g. Add both butters and swirl the pan until they emulsify. Grind in the pepper and stir it through.
  4. Boil the fettuccine 1 to 2 minutes. It wants to be tender, not soft.
  5. Move the pasta across with tongs or a pasta basket, raise the heat to medium and toss for 1 to 2 minutes. Add another 2 to 3 ladles of pasta water, 115 to 170 g, and keep tossing to marry.
  6. Pull the pan off the heat and add the finely grated 2-year parmigiano a little at a time, tossing as it goes. If the sauce tightens, a splash of pasta water loosens it.
  7. Divide between bowls, then finish with the 5-year parmigiano, grated coarsely, and more pepper.

Notes

  • The two butters do separate jobs. Buffalo's milk brings a tang, cow's milk carries the emulsion. All cow's milk butter still makes a good dish.
  • The 5-year parmigiano is the cutting note that plain Alfredo lacks. Any wheel aged at least two years will stand in.
  • Grate the finishing cheese coarsely, so the crystals land as crunch instead of melting away.
  • Pasta water goes into the pan before the pasta does, so the butter has something to emulsify into from the start.
