Baking Pasta Croquetas Empanadas

Pasta / Shapes

Tajarin

Dough
Egg Dough
Rest
20-30 min at room temperature
Source
Pasta, Missy Robbins
Formed
ribbon
Region
Piedmont, particularly the Langhe hills south of Turin

Method

  1. Make an egg-based dough, then roll and sheet it.
  2. Give a wooden work surface a light dusting of 00 flour. Line a sheet pan with parchment, then dust the parchment with semolina.
  3. Knife the sheets into 18-inch lengths and trim the ragged edges off. Save the trim for soup. Keep the sheets under plastic or a towel.
  4. Fold one sheet in half, then in thirds, until you have a packet about 4 1/2 by 6 inches. Turn it so the long side faces you and trim the ends. Cut 1/10-inch strands, working inward from one edge.
  5. Dust the strands generously with 00 flour and shake them apart. Coil the batch into a nest on the pan.
  6. Repeat with the rest of the sheets.
  7. Dry 20 to 30 minutes at room temperature.
  8. To hold, cover the pan with plastic and refrigerate up to 24 hours.

Notes

  • Cut width is 1/10 inch, by hand off a folded packet. No cutter attachment.
  • Traditionally very yolk-heavy, anywhere from 20 to 40 yolks per kilo of 00 flour. Most cooks keep a separate dough for it. Robbins does not, because her standard egg dough is already yolk-only.
  • Same family as tagliolini and maccheroncini di Campofilone. All three are thin, rich egg ribbons that differ mainly in thickness and cutting method.
  • Coiled into a nest, not a horseshoe.
  • Refrigerated shelf life is 24 hours.
  • Alba white truffle is the famous pairing. It also takes butter and sage, a ragu, chicken-liver sauce, or the juices from roast meat, and is eaten year round.
