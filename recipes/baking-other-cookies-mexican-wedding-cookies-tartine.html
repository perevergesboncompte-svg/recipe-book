Mexican Wedding Cookies from Tartine

Makes
24 small cookies
Active Time
20 min
Total Time
about 1 h 30 min
Oven
325F for 25 min
Source
Tartine, A Classic Revisited

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Walnuts: 150 g
  • All-purpose flour: 285 g
  • Unsalted butter, at cool room temperature: 225 g
  • Confectioners' sugar, for the dough: 40 g
  • Salt: 0.5 tsp
  • Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
  • Water: 1 tsp
  • Confectioners' sugar, for coating: quantity not given

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 325F and line a baking sheet with parchment or a nonstick liner.
  2. Run the walnuts in a food processor until they are finely ground, about 30 seconds.
  3. Sift the flour into a small bowl and set it aside.
  4. Fit a stand mixer with the paddle and cream the butter with the confectioners' sugar and the salt, on medium, for a minute.
  5. Still on medium, add the vanilla and the water.
  6. Scrape the sides and the base of the bowl down with a rubber spatula.
  7. Drop to low speed, add the ground walnuts and mix about 30 seconds, then add the sifted flour. Stop the mixer the moment everything is evenly combined.
  8. Drop mounds of dough onto the lined sheet with a small cookie scoop, leaving 2.5 cm between them.
  9. Bake 25 minutes, to golden brown.
  10. Move the cookies to a wire rack and let them cool.
  11. Sift confectioners' sugar into a medium bowl and toss the cooled cookies through it until coated.
  12. Wait half an hour and toss them a second time for a whiter, cleaner finish.

Notes

  • Grind the walnuts to a fine meal rather than a paste. Around 30 seconds in the processor is enough.
  • The butter goes in at cool room temperature, not fully softened.
  • Leave 2.5 cm between the scooped mounds on the sheet.
  • The second toss in sugar, after the cookies have sat half an hour, is what produces the clean white coating. No weight is given for this sugar.
  • Sealed from the air, a week is fine in a cool kitchen and a fortnight chilled.
