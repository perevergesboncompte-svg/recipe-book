Butternut Squash and Marjoram Filling

Source
Great British Chefs
Chef
Theo Randall

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Small butternut squash: 1
  • Garlic clove, sliced: 1
  • Fresh marjoram, chopped: 1 tbsp
  • Unsalted butter: 125 g
  • Nutmeg, grated: 1/4
  • Mascarpone: 100 g
  • Salt and pepper: to taste

Method

  1. Peel and deseed the squash, cut it into small dice, and boil in salted water until cooked through. Drain in a colander and cool.
  2. Foam half the butter in a pan and soften the garlic in it.
  3. Add the squash and marjoram and cook about 10 minutes, mashing gently with a wooden spoon as the liquid evaporates.
  4. Stir in the nutmeg, mascarpone, salt and pepper and leave to cool.

Notes

  • Used in Butternut Squash and Marjoram Ravioli.
  • No sub-components.
  • The 125 g of butter is shared with the sauce. Half foams the garlic here, and the source browns the rest for the sage and Gorgonzola sauce.
  • Step 3 is a drying step as much as a cooking one. The mix has to lose its liquid or the ravioli explode.
  • A heaped teaspoon per portion, spaced 3 cm apart along one side of the sheet, with water brushed between the piles to seal.
  • The mascarpone is what makes it rich, and the source pairs that against the tang of the Gorgonzola sauce.
