Oxtail and Celeriac Filling

Source
Great British Chefs
Chef
Andy McLeish

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

  • Oxtail: quantity not given
  • Wine: quantity not given
  • Stock: quantity not given
  • Vegetables: quantity not given
  • Braised celeriac: quantity not given

Method

  1. Braise the oxtail at 120 C for 4 to 5 hours with wine, stock and vegetables until tender.
  2. Pick the meat off the bones.
  3. Mix the picked meat with braised celeriac.
  4. Form into 6 balls and chill overnight.

Notes

  • Used in Oxtail Ravioli.
  • No sub-components are separated out, but the celeriac arrives already braised and the source gives no method for braising it.
  • The source's entry for this recipe is a five-line method summary with no ingredient list at all, so not one quantity here is known. Nothing has been rounded or dropped: the numbers are simply absent from the source.
  • Has to be started a day ahead. The source says the filling needs the overnight chill to set firm enough to handle.
  • 6 balls for 6 servings, sealed into 8 cm rounds and boiled 6 minutes.
  • The braising liquid becomes the oxtail jus, outside this file.
