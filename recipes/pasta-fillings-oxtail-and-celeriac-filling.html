Oxtail and Celeriac Filling

Source
Great British Chefs
Chef
Andy McLeish

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Oxtail, cut into pieces: 1 small
  • Red wine: 375 ml
  • Brown chicken stock: 1 l
  • Carrot, roughly chopped: 1
  • Onion, roughly chopped: 1
  • Garlic bulb, cut in half: 1
  • Thyme: 1 sprig
  • Tomato purée: 1 tbsp
  • Celeriac, peeled and diced: 1 small
  • Olive oil: quantity not given
  • Salt and pepper: quantity not given

Method

  1. Braise the oxtail at 120 C for 4 to 5 hours with the wine, stock, carrot, onion, garlic, thyme and tomato purée until tender.
  2. Pick the meat off the bones.
  3. Simmer the diced celeriac gently in enough of the reduced oxtail sauce to cover it, until it is soft.
  4. Mix the picked meat with the braised celeriac.
  5. Form into 6 balls the size of golf balls, about 4 cm across, and chill overnight.

Notes

  • Used in Oxtail Ravioli.
  • No sub-components are separated out. The celeriac is braised here, in enough of the reduced oxtail sauce to cover it.
  • The source gives a quantity for everything except the olive oil, the salt and the pepper, which it lists bare. Nothing has been rounded or dropped: those three are simply absent.
  • Has to be started a day ahead. The source says the filling needs the overnight chill to set firm enough to handle.
  • 6 balls for 6 servings, sealed into 8 cm rounds and boiled 6 minutes.
  • The braising liquid becomes the oxtail jus, outside this file.
