Ingredients

Batch
  • Key Components — 00 flour: 500 g
  • Key Components — egg (whole): 200 g
  • Key Components — Désirée potatoes (for 400g cooked): 500 g
  • Key Components — butter: 100 g
  • Key Components — smoked water: 15 g
  • Key Components — shallots (for reduction): 250 g
  • Key Components — white wine: 400 ml
  • Key Components — butter (for sauce): 250 g
  • Key Components — egg yolks (St. Ewe's preferred): 4
  • Key Components — truffle paste: 80 g
  • Key Components — fresh summer truffle: to serve

Method

  1. Pasta: Flour, eggs, salt, water, oil - knead 10 min, rest 2 hours.
  2. Potatoes: Boil until tender, drain, rice - need 400g. Melt butter, add salt, smoked water, milk. Mix with potato, pipe into nests.
  3. Shallot reduction: Shallots, wine, peppercorns - simmer 30 min, reduce.
  4. Beurre blanc: Cream + reduction, boil, gradually add butter. Add vinegar, salt, strain.
  5. Form: Roll thin, 15cm squares. Pipe potato nest, place yolk inside, seal with second square. Cut 12cm rounds with ring.
  6. Serve: Boil 3 min. Warm beurre blanc with truffle paste. Coat ravioli, plate, top with sauce, shave fresh truffle.

Notes

  • This smoked potato ravioli from Bancone is a delicious and dramatic pasta dish. A single, huge raviolo filled with a gooey St. Ewe's egg yolk is served in a rich, creamy truffle-infused beurre blanc sauce and covered in fresh summer truffle.
  • Chef's Tips: The key is removing all air from around the egg yolk before sealing - any air will cause the yolk to float during cooking. Use a 120mm ring for the perfect size. The smoked potato flavor is subtle but distinctive.
