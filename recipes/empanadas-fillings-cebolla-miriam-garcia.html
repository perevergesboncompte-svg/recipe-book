Empanadas / Fillings

Relleno de Cebolla, Miriam García

Yield
filling for 1 large empanada
Active Time
15 min
Rest
30 min to cool
Source
Miriam García, "El invitado de invierno"
Cook
up to 3 h, slow

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Onions: 1 kg
  • Extra virgin olive oil (some of it for the dough): 100 ml
  • Salt: to taste
  • Bay leaf (optional): 1
  • Italian green pepper (optional): 1
  • Red pepper (optional): ½
  • Sweet pimentón (optional): 1 tsp
  • Tinned atún or bonito, ~200 g drained (optional): 2 tins

Method

  1. Cut the onion into fine julienne, and the peppers into strips if using them.
  2. Sweat it covered over low heat with the oil and a pinch of salt until the onion is translucent. With patience this takes up to 3 h, and it is better made ahead.
  3. Uncover and caramelise over gentle heat, stirring, until browned and toasted to taste.
  4. Add the pimentón, and the bay leaf, off the heat so they do not burn.
  5. For a filling with protein, fold the drained tuna into the warm sofrito and check the salt.
  6. Drain for 10 min and cool before spreading it thinly over the base. Keep the oil for the dough.

Notes

  • Drain it for 10 min and cool it before assembly, and spread it thin so the empanada closes and bakes through.
  • Miriam García's original is only caramelised onion, oil and salt. The pepper, pimentón and tuna are NOT from the source — the sheet flags them as additions for a more classic profile.
  • Make it ahead. The sofrito needs hours and improves overnight.
  • Low heat and covered first, then uncovered to caramelise without burning.
  • Reuse the flavoured oil in the masa gramada.
  • Vegan version: leave it as onion only and do not brush the dough with egg.
  • Written for gramada-miriam-garcia.
