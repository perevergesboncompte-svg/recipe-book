Baking / Butter cookies

Brazilian Butter Cookies drafted

Yield
100 cookies
Unit Weight
11 g
Oven
350F, 18-20 min
Source
Reddit / YouTube

Ingredients

  • Butter, room temperature: 300 g
  • Granulated sugar: 170 g
  • All-purpose flour: 490 g
  • Egg yolks: 2
  • Powdered milk: 50 g
  • Vanilla extract: 10 ml

Method

  1. Beat the butter and sugar 3 to 4 minutes until smooth and creamy.
  2. Add the yolks one at a time, then the vanilla.
  3. Mix in the powdered milk until evenly spread.
  4. Add the flour in batches until the dough is cohesive and stops sticking to your hands.
  5. Roll into 11 g balls, optionally rolled in extra sugar.
  6. Stamp each one, or flatten with a fork.
  7. Bake at 350F for 18 to 20 minutes until the edges are golden.
  8. Cool 5 minutes on the sheet, then move to a rack.

Notes

  • Same powdered milk trick as the Milk Powder cookies, which scored highest of the butter cookies, so this one is worth trying on that basis alone.
  • 100 cookies at 11 g each, by far the largest yield in the collection. No chill step.
  • Half the dough takes cinnamon or cocoa if you want two kinds from one batch.
  • Keeps 2 weeks airtight.
