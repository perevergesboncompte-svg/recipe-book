Pasta / Dishes

Balanzoni Burro e Salvia

Filling
Mortadella and Ricotta Filling
Shape
Balanzoni

North

4-6
Total Time
2 h plus the dough rest
Source
Missy Robbins, Pasta

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Mortadella, in one piece: 120 g
  • Ricotta: 604 g
  • Parmigiano-reggiano for the filling, finely grated: 30 g
  • Nutmeg, whole: quantity not given
  • Salt: 0.75 tsp
  • Green dough: 1 batch
  • Unsalted butter, cold and cubed: 150 g
  • Sage leaves, small: 15
  • Parmigiano-reggiano to finish, finely grated: 22 g
  • Black pepper: quantity not given

Method

  1. Chill the meat grinder and its small die in the refrigerator at least 1 hour, or 30 minutes in the freezer.
  2. Fit the small die and run the mortadella through twice.
  3. Push the ricotta through a tamis or a fine-mesh strainer into a small bowl. Add the mortadella and the 30 g of parmigiano and stir until evenly blended.
  4. Grate in 5 or 6 passes of nutmeg, stir, and taste. It should sit in the background at this stage, because it blooms once the pasta is cooking.
  5. Add the 3/4 tsp salt and taste again. Mortadellas differ in salt and spice, so more may be needed. Aim for properly seasoned. Cover it and refrigerate until you need it.
  6. Make 50 to 60 balanzoni from the green dough and the filling.
  7. Salt a large pot of water heavily and bring it to a boil over high heat.
  8. While the water heats, set a small saucepan over medium heat and cook half the butter 3 to 4 minutes, to just past golden brown. Drop in 5 of the sage leaves and take the pan off the heat. This gets drizzled over the plated pasta.
  9. Set a large saute pan over low heat, add 2 ladles of pasta water (115 g) and the rest of the butter, and swirl to emulsify. Take it off the heat.
  10. Drop the balanzoni in and bring the water down to a gentle simmer rather than a rolling boil. Cook 1 to 2 minutes. They are done when the thickest seal has gone tender.
  11. Return the saute pan to low heat. Lift the pasta across with a spider or pasta basket and raise the heat to medium. Swirl 30 seconds to 1 minute, turning the pieces with a spoon so every side gets coated. A splash of pasta water loosens the sauce if it tightens.
  12. Divide between bowls. Finish with the 22 g of parmigiano, grated nutmeg and black pepper, spoon the sage butter over, and scatter the remaining sage leaves.

Notes

  • Simmer, do not boil. That filling is fragile and comes apart if the water is moving hard.
  • The 150 g of butter is split in two. Half browns with 5 sage leaves and goes over the plate at the end. Half emulsifies with pasta water into the pan sauce.
  • Doneness is judged at the thickest closure point, not at the body of the pasta.
  • Ricotta salata and smoked ricotta salata are both given as alternatives to the parmigiano finish.
  • Balanzoni are said to have come out of tortellini, the tortellini filling cut with ricotta.
