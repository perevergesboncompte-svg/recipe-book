Baking / Butter cookies

Mexican Wedding Cookies drafted

Yield
48 cookies
Unit Weight
15 g
Oven
325°F, 15-20 min

Ingredients

Batch
  • 227 g butter
  • 57 g confectioners' sugar
  • 7 ml vanilla extract
  • 5 ml almond extract (optional)
  • 5 g salt
  • 72 g almond flour
  • 270 g flour

Method

Before starting:

  • Preheat oven to 325°F.
  • Lightly grease or line two baking sheets with parchment.
  1. In a large bowl, beat together butter, sugar, vanilla, almond extract, and salt until smooth.
  2. Beat in almond flour, then flour, until just combined.
  3. Scoop chestnut-sized (1") pieces of dough and roll into balls. A level teaspoon cookie scoop works perfectly.
  4. Place balls on prepared baking sheets, leaving 1" between them.
  5. Bake for 15-20 minutes until set on top. Some may start to barely brown around edges.
  6. While baking, put coating sugar in a shallow bowl.
  7. Remove from oven, cool on pan for a few minutes.
  8. Transfer 5-6 cookies at a time to bowl with sugar. Roll to coat evenly.
  9. Transfer to rack to cool completely.
  10. Roll in sugar again when cool for thorough snowy-white coating.
  11. Store airtight at room temperature for several days; freeze for longer storage.

Notes

  • If using salted butter, reduce added salt to 1/2 teaspoon.
  • Also known as Russian Teacakes, or Vanilla Snowballs.
  • For extra tender texture, use almond flour or finely ground blanched almonds.
  • Roll twice in sugar - once warm, once cool - for the classic snowy appearance.
