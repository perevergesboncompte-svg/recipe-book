Pate Brisee

Yield
1 x 23 cm tart base, serves 6
Active Time
10 min
Rest
30 min to 1 h
Source
French Patisserie, Ferrandi Paris

Ingredients

Batch
  • All-purpose flour, sifted: 125 g
  • Unsalted butter, diced and well chilled: 75 g
  • Egg, lightly beaten: 40 g
  • Sugar: 6 g
  • Salt: 2.5 g

Method

  1. Paddle the sifted flour, diced butter, sugar and salt on low until it looks like coarse sand, 30 to 45 seconds. The butter should be in small pieces, all coated in flour.
  2. Scrape the bowl down.
  3. With the mixer low, pour the beaten egg in steadily. It comes together within 15 to 20 seconds.
  4. Stop the moment it is smooth. Overworking turns it elastic and tough.
  5. Shape, wrap, and chill 30 minutes to an hour.

Notes

  • Only 6 g of sugar, so this is the savoury-leaning shortcrust. Use it for anything that is not a dessert tart.
  • The butter needs to be well chilled but not frozen, unlike pate sablee.
  • Keeps 3 days refrigerated, well covered.
