Croquetas

Croquetas de Cocido

Yield
4 people (~20 croquetas)
Active Time
35 min
Rest
2 h (overnight is better)
Source
Directo al Paladar (Pakus)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Leftover cocido (meats, chickpeas, vegetables): 200 g
  • Cocido broth: 200 ml
  • Whole milk: 400 ml
  • Butter: 50 g
  • Wheat flour: 50 g
  • Onion: 1
  • Hard-boiled egg (optional): 1
  • Extra virgin olive oil: 1 tbsp
  • Salt, pepper, nutmeg: quantity not given
  • Eggs (for coating): 2
  • Breadcrumbs (for coating): quantity not given

Method

  1. Chop the cocido meats and the onion very fine. Sweat the onion in the butter and oil. Heat the milk together with the broth. Stir the meats into the pan, along with the chopped boiled egg if using.
  2. Add the flour, stir, then pour in the hot milk and broth gradually, stirring for 20-25 min until the mass pulls away from the pan.
  3. Season and add the nutmeg. Pour into a tray, cool, and refrigerate at least 2 h. Overnight is better.
  4. Shape and coat in beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  5. Fry in plenty of hot oil and drain on kitchen paper.

Notes

  • Bechamel ratio given by the source as roughly 50 g flour : 50 g fat : 600 ml liquid, which here is 400 ml milk plus 200 ml broth. Creamy but still firm enough to roll.
  • Using cocido broth for part of the liquid, 200 ml of the 600, is what gives this its character.
  • The leftovers croqueta par excellence, born from reusing the meats of a cocido.
  • Boiled egg is the traditional reinforcement, optional but recommended.
  • Unfried they keep 2-3 days in the fridge, or freeze them.
  • Header calls for deep oil.
