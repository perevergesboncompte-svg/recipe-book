Relleno de Rabo de Toro

Yield
filling for 1 large empanada
Active Time
25 min
Rest
overnight to cool
Source
Directo al Paladar (Pakus), following the method of the Cofradía del Rabo de Toro Cordobés
Cook
over 3 h, slow stew

Ingredients

Batch
  • Rabo de toro, or beef tail: 1,5 kg
  • Onions: 2
  • Carrots: 2
  • Ripe tomatoes: 3
  • Garlic cloves: 5-6
  • Italian green pepper: 1
  • Extra virgin olive oil: 75 ml
  • Montilla-Moriles wine (or dry red): 350 ml
  • Water, just enough to cover: 1 l
  • Peppercorns: 8-10
  • Cloves: 4
  • Saffron: quantity not given
  • Salt: ~20 g

Method

  1. Trim the fat off the tail. Sweat the onion in the AOVE, add the garlic, carrot and pepper for 5 to 6 min, then the tomato in wedges.
  2. Add the tail and stir. Add the saffron and the wine, let the alcohol evaporate, then cover with the water. Add the cloves and peppercorns.
  3. Once it boils, skim, drop to the lowest heat and stew over 3 h until the meat comes away from the bone. Better done the day before.
  4. Bone and shred the meat. Blend the strained sauce with the vegetables.
  5. Mix the meat with only enough sauce to bind it, 4 to 6 tbsp, and reduce it in a pan until juicy with no loose liquid.
  6. Drain and cool completely before assembling. Keep the rest of the sauce to serve.

Notes

  • Reduce until juicy but with no loose liquid, then drain and cool completely. The sheet gives overnight as the cooling time, and cooking it the day before improves it a lot anyway.
  • The cofradía method from Córdoba: slow cooking rather than pressure, to get the collagen out of the tail.
  • 1,5 kg of tail yields roughly 450 to 550 g of clean meat, which is right for a large empanada.
  • Keep the leftover sauce separate to serve alongside rather than putting it in the filling.
  • The same source suggests this meat for croquetas, albóndigas or empanadas.
