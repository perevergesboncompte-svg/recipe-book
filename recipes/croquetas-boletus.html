Croquetas

Croquetas de Boletus

Yield
4 people (~20 croquetas)
Active Time
35 min
Rest
8 h (overnight is better)
Source
Carmen Tía Alia, Directo al Paladar

Ingredients

Batch
  • Dried boletus: 20 g
  • Water to rehydrate (100 ml of it gets reserved): 200 ml
  • Onion: 1
  • Butter: 60 g
  • Wheat flour: 80 g
  • Whole milk: 350 ml
  • Reserved soaking water: 100 ml
  • Salt, white pepper, egg and breadcrumbs: to taste

Method

  1. Soak the boletus in water at least 30 min. Drain and keep 100 ml of the soaking water.
  2. Dice the onion fine and sweat it. Dry and chop the boletus, add them, and cook a couple of minutes.
  3. Add the butter. Once it melts, stir in the flour and cook it a couple of minutes.
  4. Add the 100 ml of boletus water, the milk, salt and pepper. Cook 20-30 min to a creamy mass.
  5. Transfer to a tray, cover to the surface, and refrigerate at least 8 h.
  6. Shape, coat in egg and breadcrumbs, and fry at 180 °C in batches. Drain.

Notes

  • Roux is 80 g flour : 60 g butter to 450 ml liquid (350 ml milk plus 100 ml boletus water). Not a 1:1 roux, and far and away the stiffest mix in this collection: 17.8 g of flour per 100 ml of liquid against 6.7 to 11 g for every other recipe here. Worth checking against a second source before scaling up.
  • The boletus soaking water is what gives the depth, with no need for truffle.
  • Long rest, 8 h, for creamy croquetas that roll easily.
  • Dry the boletus well before frying them off or they water the mass down.
  • Strain the soaking water in case it carries grit.
  • Mixing boletus with fresh button mushroom brings the cost down.
  • Header gives the frying temperature as 180 °C.
