Crab Ravioli with Crab Sauce

Ingredients

Batch
  • Pasta Dough — 00 flour: 250 g
  • Pasta Dough — egg (whole): 1
  • Pasta Dough — egg yolks (reserve 2 for egg wash): 6
  • Pasta Dough — olive oil: 1 tbsp
  • Pasta Dough — cold water: 1 tbsp
  • Pasta Dough — salt: 1 pinch
  • Crab Meat — large cock crabs (or 1kg white + 300g brown): 3
  • Crab Meat — water: 6 l
  • Crab Meat — salt: 100 g
  • Crab Meat — lemons, juiced: 3
  • Crab Meat — thyme: 1 sprig
  • Crab Meat — bay leaf: 1
  • Salmon Mousse — smoked salmon: 200 g
  • Salmon Mousse — sole (or other white fish): 200 g
  • Salmon Mousse — egg white: 1
  • Salmon Mousse — double cream: 600 ml
  • Salmon Mousse — salt and pepper: to taste
  • Crab Sauce — crab shells (from cooked crabs): 3 kg
  • Crab Sauce — brandy: 250 ml
  • Crab Sauce — onion, carrots, fennel, leek, celery (sliced): 50 g each
  • Crab Sauce — tomatoes: 100 g
  • Crab Sauce — garlic: 20 g
  • Crab Sauce — saffron threads: 1 g
  • Crab Sauce — Pernod: 100 ml
  • Crab Sauce — white wine: 500 ml
  • Crab Sauce — thyme, rosemary: 10 g each
  • Crab Sauce — parsley: 20 g
  • Crab Sauce — double cream: 500 ml
  • Crab Sauce — butter: 150 g

Method

  1. Prepare the crabs: Combine water, lemon juice, salt, thyme and bay leaf in a large pan. Bring to boil, add crabs and simmer for 10 minutes. Cooking time depends on crab size. Once cooked, cool and pick all meat - keep white and brown separate. Reserve shells for stock.
  2. Make pasta dough: Place flour in a bowl, make a well in centre. Pour in eggs, olive oil, water and salt. Gradually bring flour in from edges until dough forms. Turn onto table and knead 4-5 minutes until smooth. Cover and rest in fridge for minimum 1 hour.
  3. Prepare salmon mousse: Remove skin and bone from fish, cut into chunks. Blend with egg white until smooth paste. Pass through fine sieve. Place over ice, slowly stir in cream, salt and pepper. Check consistency by poaching a spoonful - don't let water boil. Continue adding cream until right texture.
  4. Make filling: Combine 1kg white crab meat with 300g brown meat and 200g salmon mousse. Add herbs, lemon juice and seasoning. Mix well and chill.
  5. Prepare crab sauce: Break shells into small pieces, roast at 180°C for 10 minutes. Deglaze with brandy (careful - it will ignite). Sauté vegetables, add shells, tomatoes, wine, Pernod and aromatics. Cover with water and simmer 1 hour. Strain, reduce, finish with cream, butter, lemon juice, Tabasco. Season to taste.
  6. Form ravioli: Roll pasta to second thinnest setting on machine. Weigh filling into 50g balls. Place on pasta sheet, egg wash around filling, cover with another sheet. Cut with 6cm pastry cutter. Press around filling to seal, removing all air. Store on tray.
  7. Cook and serve: Bring 2 litres salted water to boil. Cook ravioli for 4 minutes. Meanwhile, bring sauce to boil, add butter and brown crab meat, blend to achieve bubbles. Blanch samphire 30 seconds. Place samphire in bowls, arrange ravioli on top, add tomato concasse, spoon sauce over. Finish with brown crab meat, olive oil, fleur de sel and chervil leaves.

Notes

  • This elaborate crab ravioli recipe makes a tremendous main course, especially apt for spring and summer months. The dish features a complex crab filling with salmon mousse, enclosed in delicate homemade pasta, and served with an intensely flavorful crab sauce made from roasted shells.
  • Chef's Tips: The key to this dish is timing - prepare all components in advance and cook the ravioli to order. The brown crab meat adds intense flavor to both the filling and the sauce. Don't skip the fresh herbs - they brighten the rich crab flavors.
