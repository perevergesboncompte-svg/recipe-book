Ingredients

Batch
  • All-purpose flour: 250 g
  • Cake flour: 155 g
  • Baking powder: 10 g
  • Baking soda: 2 g
  • Kosher salt: 2 g
  • Cold butter, cubed: 225 g
  • Brown sugar, packed: 145 g
  • Granulated sugar: 135 g
  • Dark chocolate chips: 340 g
  • Walnuts, toasted and chopped: 120 g
  • Eggs, beaten: 2

Method

  1. Whisk both flours with the baking powder, baking soda and salt.
  2. Cream the cold cubed butter on low 30 to 45 seconds. Stop while small butter pieces are still visible, since those make the dense crumb.
  3. Add both sugars, cream another 30 to 60 seconds.
  4. Beat in the chocolate chips and toasted walnuts.
  5. Add the flour mixture all at once, beat low until crumbly. Break up any large butter lumps by hand.
  6. Add the beaten eggs, mix until the dough holds together when pressed.
  7. Form 170 g balls, 4 per sheet, and freeze at least 90 minutes. This is what keeps them thick.
  8. Preheat with a second sheet upside down in the oven.
  9. Bake one sheet at a time on top of the hot inverted sheet, 375F for about 26 minutes, until edges and top spots are golden and centres still look doughy.
  10. Cool on the pan. Moving them early leaves the centres raw.

Notes

  • Cold butter, not softened, is the mechanism here.
  • Cake flour softens the interior while the butter pieces keep the crumb textured.
  • Double-stacking the pans stops the bottoms burning before the centres cook.
  • Only 8 cookies, so the per-cookie effort is high.

Learnings

Joint top overall at 4.5, tied with Broma. Flavor 4.5 and texture 4, but appearance only 3, the weakest look of the five made. The 90 minute freeze plus 26 minute bake makes this a plan-ahead cookie.
