Pasta / Fillings

Goat's Cheese and Cream Cheese Filling

Source
Great British Chefs
Chef
Andy Waters

Ingredients

Batch
  • Goat's cheese: 500 g
  • Cream cheese: 100 g
  • Coriander, chopped: 1 tbsp
  • Flat-leaf parsley, chopped: 1 tbsp
  • Chives, chopped: 1 tbsp

Method

  1. Beat the goat's cheese, cream cheese and herbs together until evenly combined.
  2. Divide into four balls and chill.

Notes

  • Used in Goat's Cheese Ravioli.
  • No sub-components, and nothing is cooked. The mixture goes in raw and stays raw.
  • That ravioli is an open fazzoletti style, so the filling is not sealed inside. Pasta squares are boiled separately and the cheese is spooned between them at the plate, a teaspoon-sized ball at a time.
  • Bring it back to lukewarm before serving, so the four chilled balls get broken down again at plating.
  • Assemble at the last moment. The pasta keeps drawing moisture out of the cheese.
