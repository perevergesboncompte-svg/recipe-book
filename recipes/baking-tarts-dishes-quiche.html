Quiche

Dough
Flaky Tart Dough
Makes
1 quiche, 25 cm, 6 to 8 servings
Active Time
20 min
Rest
at least 20 min on a rack before slicing
Total Time
1 h 20 min
Oven
375F for 10 min, then 325F for 30 min
Source
Tartine, A Classic Revisited

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Flaky tart dough, baked out fully and cooled in a 25 cm deep tart pan or pie pan: 1 batch
  • Eggs, large: 5
  • All-purpose flour: 30 g
  • Crème fraîche: 240 g
  • Whole milk: 240 ml
  • Salt: 1 tsp
  • Black pepper, ground fresh: 0.5 tsp
  • Thyme, fresh, chopped fine: 1 tbsp
  • Sharp cheese, Gruyère or Cheddar, grated: 115 g

Method

  1. Make the flaky tart dough, line a 25 cm deep tart pan or pie pan with it, and bake the shell through. Let it cool before you fill it.
  2. Heat the oven to 375F [190C].
  3. Beat one egg with all the flour until smooth. Use a stand mixer on high with the paddle fitted, or a large bowl and a whisk.
  4. Add the other four eggs and mix only until the custard is even.
  5. Whisk the crème fraîche in a medium bowl until it is perfectly smooth, then whisk the milk into it.
  6. Hold a fine-mesh sieve over the milk mixture and pour the egg mixture through it.
  7. Whisk the salt, the pepper and the thyme in, then stir the grated cheese through.
  8. Pour the custard into the cooled shell.
  9. Bake 10 minutes, then drop the oven to 325F [160C] and give it about 30 minutes more, until the filling is just set. Touched at the centre it should feel slightly firm rather than liquid.
  10. Leave it on a wire rack a good 20 minutes at least, so the custard finishes setting and the slices hold their edge. Serve warm or at room temperature.

Notes

  • Two things set this filling apart from most. Crème fraîche stands in for some of the dairy, adding richness and a faint tartness, and there is 30 g of flour in the custard.
  • The custard can be mixed up to 4 days ahead and kept covered in the fridge. The flour settles out, so whisk it hard before pouring.
  • Up to 85 g of raw coarsely chopped chard or spinach can go straight into the egg mixture. At that weight the liquid the greens give off does not spoil the set. Anything more has to be cooked and wrung dry first.
  • A brim-full shell is hard to carry. Fill it three-quarters, bake until the surface has barely set, then pour in the rest and carry on.
  • To warm a fully cooled quiche, cover it with foil and put it in a 325F [160C] oven for about 15 minutes. Tightly wrapped, it keeps 4 days in the fridge and is good cold as well.
