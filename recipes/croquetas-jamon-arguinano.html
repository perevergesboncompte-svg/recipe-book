Croquetas

Croquetas de Jamón, Arguiñano

Yield
~25-30 croquetas
Active Time
30 min
Rest
Overnight
Source
Karlos Arguiñano, Hogarmania

Ingredients

Batch
  • Jamón serrano, chopped: 200 g
  • Cebolleta (spring onion): 1
  • Garlic clove: 1
  • Flour: 100 g
  • Butter: 100 g
  • Milk: 1 l
  • Chopped parsley: to taste
  • Beaten eggs (for coating): 3
  • Flour and breadcrumbs (for coating): as needed
  • Extra virgin olive oil for frying: as needed

Method

  1. Melt the butter, sweat the cebolleta and garlic 5-6 min, add the jamón and cook briefly.
  2. Stir in the flour, cook 2 min, then add the milk gradually, stirring constantly.
  3. Cook the bechamel about 20 min until thick. Stir in the parsley.
  4. Spread the mass out, press film onto the surface so it cannot skin over, and chill completely. A day ahead is better.
  5. Shape, then coat in flour, egg and breadcrumbs.
  6. Fry in plenty of hot oil until golden. Drain and serve.

Notes

  • Roux 1:1 (100 g flour : 100 g butter : 1 l milk). The source calls this the canonical ratio for a creamy croqueta.
  • The cold rest, preferably overnight, is what makes the mass shapeable and coatable.
  • Add the milk a little at a time and never stop stirring, or it goes lumpy.
  • Film pressed onto the surface while it cools prevents a skin.
  • Fry in small batches so the oil temperature does not drop.
  • Header calls for deep oil for frying.
  • Arguiñano holds one Michelin star, Zarautz.
