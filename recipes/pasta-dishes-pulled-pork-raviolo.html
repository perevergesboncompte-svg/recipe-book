Method

  1. Braise pork: 120°C oven, 4 hours with stock, aromatics. Shred meat.
  2. Mix filling: Combine pork, chorizo, reduced braising liquid, coriander.
  3. Form: 50g balls, place on gyoza wrapper, top with another, seal.
  4. Cook: Steam 6 minutes, ice bath, re-steam 5 minutes to serve.
  5. Sauce: Green chilli, peanut, sesame, fish sauce.
  6. Serve: Cucumber pickle base, raviolo, sauce, cress garnish.

Notes

  • This stunning starter combines flavours from across continents for a harmonious dish, with pulled pork belly concealed inside gyoza wrappers to make a 'raviolo'. Topped with a Thai-inspired chilli, peanut and sesame sauce, this is a must-try dish for dinner parties.
  • Chef's Tips: Uses gyoza wrappers instead of traditional pasta - a clever fusion approach. The Asian-influenced sauce perfectly complements the Spanish chorizo.
