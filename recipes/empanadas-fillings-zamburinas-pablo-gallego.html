Empanadas / Fillings

Relleno de Zamburiñas, Pablo Gallego

Yield
filling for 1 large empanada
Active Time
15 min
Rest
30 min to cool
Source
El Comidista style, Pablo Gallego, A Coruña, elpais.com/gastronomia
Cook
25 min

Ingredients

Batch
  • Zamburiñas, 20/30 count, or vieiras: 1 bag
  • Onions: 2-3
  • Green pepper: 1
  • Garlic cloves: 2
  • Large tomato: 1
  • White wine: a splash
  • Parsley: to taste
  • Salt: to taste
  • Olive oil: plenty

Method

  1. Thaw the zamburiñas overnight if frozen and drain them very well. Halve the large ones. If they are fresh, soak them in cold salted water for about 2 h.
  2. Finely chop the onion, garlic and green pepper, and chop the tomato.
  3. Heat plenty of olive oil, add the garlic and, as it colours, the onion.
  4. Soften the onion, add the green pepper, and cook slowly until the onion turns translucent.
  5. Add the chopped tomato. Once it releases its water, add the white wine and let the alcohol cook off.
  6. Keep cooking until the zaragallada is compact and juicy with no loose liquid.
  7. Stir in the zamburiñas and parsley, season, give them a couple of minutes and take the pan off the heat.
  8. Cool completely and drain off the excess oil before assembling.

Notes

  • Has to be cooked down and cooled: the source says to cook until compact with no excess liquid, then "deja enfriar del todo", because a warm filling softens the dough and excess liquid softens the base and splits the empanada.
  • The zaragallada, pochado onion and pepper, is the classic base. The zamburiñas go in at the end so they do not dry out.
  • Do not overcook the zamburiñas. About 2 minutes is enough and they finish in the oven.
  • Keep some of the frying oil back for brushing the dough or enriching it.
  • This is the filling half of the El Comidista zamburiñas empanada. Its dough half is leche-pablo-gallego.
