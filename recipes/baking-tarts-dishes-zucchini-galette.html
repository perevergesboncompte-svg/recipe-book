Zucchini Galette

Dough
Whole Wheat Galette Crust
Serves
6
Source
Claire Saffitz, Dessert Person

Ingredients

Batch
  • Zucchini: 1 0.5 lb, about 3 medium-large
  • Unsalted butter: 3 tbsp
  • Yellow onion, thinly sliced: 1 medium
  • Fresh thyme: 3 sprigs
  • Gruyere, grated on the large holes: 4 oz
  • Parmesan, finely grated: 2 oz, about 0.5 cup
  • Egg: 1, divided
  • Ground nutmeg: a pinch
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper: to taste

Method

  1. Make and chill the whole wheat crust.
  2. Caramelise the sliced onion in the butter with the thyme sprigs.
  3. Prepare the zucchini and draw its water out before it goes near the pastry. This is the step the whole recipe turns on.
  4. Roll the crust out, layer on the cheeses, the caramelised onion and the zucchini, seasoning with salt, pepper and the nutmeg.
  5. Fold the border over the filling and brush the pastry with egg.
  6. Bake until the crust is crisp and golden.

Notes

  • A wet filling ruins a galette, and zucchini at 1 1/2 lb is mostly water. Salting and draining it first is what keeps the base crisp.
  • Gruyere on the large holes but parmesan finely grated, so they behave differently: one melts into pockets, the other seasons throughout.
  • The egg is listed as divided, so part goes in the filling and part is the wash.
  • The source is a video and its description gives the ingredient list but no oven temperature, no bake time and no quantities for the seasoning. Those are absent here rather than guessed. A galette of this size normally wants a hot oven, around 400F, for 45 minutes or so, but that figure is not from the source.
