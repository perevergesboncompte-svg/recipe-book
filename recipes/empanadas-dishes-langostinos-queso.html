Empanada de Langostinos y Queso

Yield
Filling for 1 large empanada
Active Time
20 min
Oven
180 °C, 25-30 min
Source
Langostino and leek empanada, based on Beatriz Córdoba (oipacyl)
Energy Cost
$2.06 (30 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Langostinos, peeled and cut up: 250 g
  • Large leeks, julienned: 2
  • Small onion, chopped: 1
  • Garlic cloves: 2
  • Red pepper, diced: 1
  • Grated cheese: 100 g
  • Puff pastry sheet (plus a lid): 1
  • Egg (for glazing): 1
  • Oil, salt, pepper: no quantity given

Method

  1. Sweat the onion, leeks and pepper in oil with salt for 15-20 min until meltingly soft, without browning.
  2. Add the garlic and, once it colours, the langostinos. Sauté 1-2 min.
  3. Adjust salt and pepper and cool completely.
  4. Roll out the puff pastry, spread over the filling and the grated cheese, and cover with the lid.
  5. Seal the edges and brush with beaten egg.
  6. Bake at 180 °C for about 25-30 min until the pastry is golden.

Notes

  • Stovetop cook time is 20 min.
  • Built on a bought sheet of hojaldre (puff pastry) rather than an empanada dough.
  • The sweet richness of sweated leek is the ideal base for shellfish.
  • Do not overcook the langostinos. One to two minutes is enough, and they finish in the oven.
  • The grated cheese binds the filling and adds creaminess as it bakes.
  • Drain and cool well. Shellfish releases water and would soften the pastry.
  • Puff pastry browns fast. Watch the last few minutes.
