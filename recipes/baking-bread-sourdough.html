High Hydration Sourdough

Makes
1 loaf
Active Time
30 min
Rest
8-10 h bulk, then 1-6 h cold
Oven
450F, 60 min total
Price
$8
Tags
vegan
Source
mine, kitchen notebook

Ingredients

Batch
  • Active starter: 50 g
  • Warm water: 375 g
  • Bread flour: 500 g
  • Fine sea salt: 9 g

Method

  1. Thursday to Friday, feed the starter.
  2. Saturday evening, make the dough. Sunday morning, bake.
  3. Mix the water with the starter. Form a rough dough and rest 1 hour.
  4. Work it into a ball, 15 to 20 seconds.
  5. Bulk rise 8 to 10 hours at room temperature.
  6. Dimple the dough all over with floured fingertips.
  7. Gently shape into a round and rest 5 to 10 minutes.
  8. Flip the dough and shape again, then flip it over. Shape once more and flip back.
  9. Into the bread basket, seam side up.
  10. Cover and refrigerate 1 hour, up to 6 if needed.
  11. Let it sit at room temperature while the oven heats. Preheat to 450F.
  12. Bake 20 minutes covered, 30 minutes with the lid off, then 10 minutes directly.

Notes

  • The schedule is built around a weekend: feed Thursday and Friday, mix Saturday night, bake Sunday morning.
  • Seam side up in the basket, so it goes seam down into the pot.
  • The three-stage bake is 60 minutes total, longer than most recipes, and the final 10 minutes out of the pot is what sets the crust.

Learnings

375 g of water against 500 g of flour is 75% hydration before the starter, which is where the name comes from. Counting the starter as half flour and half water, total hydration lands near 76%.

The preheat line in the notebook has one temperature struck through with 450F written after it, so 450F is what is recorded here. Worth settling whether the struck-out figure was a hotter preheat that then drops for the bake.
