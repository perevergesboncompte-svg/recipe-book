Sourdough Concha

Yield
14 conchas
Unit Weight
about 85 g each
Rest
4-5 h levain, 5 h bulk, overnight to 2 days chilled, then 4-5 h final proof
Oven
350F / 177C, 18 min
Source
Sourdough Brandon, sourdoughbrandon.com/sourdough-conchas
Energy Cost
$1.65 (18 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Sourdough starter, for the levain: 50 g
  • Bread flour, for the levain: 50 g
  • Water, for the levain: 50 g
  • Bread flour: 570 g
  • Granulated sugar: 62 g
  • Sea salt: 11 g
  • Eggs: 3
  • Whole milk, room temperature: 125 g
  • Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
  • Unsalted butter, softened: 113 g
  • Orange zest: from 1 orange, optional
  • All-purpose flour, for the topping: 120 g
  • Powdered sugar, sifted, for the topping: 113 g
  • Unsalted butter, softened, for the topping: 113 g
  • Kosher salt, for the topping: a pinch
  • Flavouring or colouring, for the topping: as needed

Method

  1. Levain: stir starter, flour and water together, cover, and leave 4 to 5 hours at 75-80F until doubled and bubbly. Or skip it and use 150 g of active starter.
  2. If using orange zest, rub it into the granulated sugar until it smells of orange and looks like wet sand.
  3. Mix the flour, salt, sugar, eggs, milk, vanilla and all the levain until the flour is hydrated, a couple of minutes.
  4. Work the softened butter in.
  5. Bulk ferment in a covered bowl at 75-80F for about 5 hours, until doubled, airy and domed.
  6. Refrigerate overnight, up to 2 days.
  7. Punch down, weigh the dough, divide by 14, and roll each portion into a ball. About 85 g each.
  8. Topping: bring the flour, sifted powdered sugar, butter and salt together to a Play-Doh consistency. Divide and colour or flavour it now if you want more than one.
  9. Press the topping into rounds and drape one over each ball.
  10. Score the shell pattern with a lame, sharp knife or concha cutter.
  11. Cover and proof 4 to 5 hours somewhere warm, until doubled, airy, and wobbling when you shake the tray.
  12. Bake one tray at a time at 350F for 18 minutes, until lightly golden. Cool completely.

Notes

  • Sourdough rather than commercial yeast, so the timeline runs across two days and the flavour is the point.
  • The topping is equal butter and powdered sugar at 113 g each against 120 g flour, which is why it stays pliable enough to drape and score.
  • Bulk at 75-80F. Cooler and the 5 hour figure stops applying.
  • Wobble when you shake the pan is the proof test, not the clock.
  • Divide the actual dough weight by 14 rather than trusting 85 g, since egg sizes vary.
