Langoustine Filling

Source
Great British Chefs
Chef
Martin Wishart

Ingredients

Batch
  • Langoustines, heads removed and reserved for stock: 1.5 kg

Method

  1. Boil the langoustines for 10 seconds.
  2. Shell them and chop the tail meat.

Notes

  • Used in Langoustine Ravioli.
  • No sub-components. Chopped tail meat and nothing else.
  • The source's entry for this recipe is compressed to six one-line steps, so there is no binder, no seasoning and no portion weight for the filling. Only what is stated is recorded here.
  • The source insists on fresh, still-alive langoustines and warns against overcooking the tails. Ten seconds is a blanch to release the shells, not a cook.
  • Heads go to the langoustine stock, which also takes a further 1 kg of heads. That stock and the braised endive are outside this file.
  • Sealed into 8-10 cm rounds and boiled 90 seconds.

Used in

  • Langoustine Ravioli
