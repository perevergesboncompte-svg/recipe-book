Gluten-Free Crumb Crust

Base
Oat Digestive Biscuits
Makes
1 pie shell at 23 cm, or 1 cheesecake base at 25 cm
Active Time
10 min
Total Time
about 30 min plus cooling
Oven
350F / 180C for 5 to 10 min
Source
Tartine, A Classic Revisited

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Oat digestive biscuits, baked and cooled: 150 g
  • Unsalted butter, melted: 4 tbsp
  • Brown sugar: 1 tbsp + 2 tsp
  • Salt: 0.5 tsp
  • Cinnamon, optional: 1 tsp

Method

  1. Bake a batch of oat digestive biscuits ahead and let them cool completely.
  2. Heat the oven to 350F.
  3. Grind the biscuits to a fine crumb in a food processor, about 30 seconds.
  4. Pour the melted butter over the crumbs in an even stream, then add the brown sugar, the salt and the cinnamon if you are using it.
  5. Run the processor about 10 seconds more. Stop once a handful of the mixture clumps when you squeeze it in your palm.
  6. Tip the crumbs into a pie pan or a springform and press them evenly across the bottom and up the sides.
  7. Pack them down firmly with the flat base of a cup.
  8. Bake 5 to 10 minutes, until golden brown.
  9. Cool the shell completely before any filling goes in.

Notes

  • 150 g of biscuit works out to roughly 10 of them.
  • Butter, sugar, salt and cinnamon are all given by volume, with no weights alongside.
  • The crust is gluten-free because the biscuits are. They are built on rolled oats and oat flour with no wheat, so use certified oats if that matters.
  • The squeeze test in step 5 is the one that decides the outcome. Crumbs that will not clump bake into a shell that collapses when you cut it.

Preserving

A pressed and baked shell is sturdier than it looks, but it draws moisture from the air and from any filling.

  • The baked shell keeps covered in the fridge up to 2 days before filling.
  • Keep it cold rather than at room temperature, because the butter is what holds the crumb together.
  • Fill it only once it is cold all the way through.
  • The biscuits themselves hold a week airtight, so make them well in advance.
