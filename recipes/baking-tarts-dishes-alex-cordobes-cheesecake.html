Baking / Tarts / Dish

Alex Cordobés Cheesecake drafted

Base
Maria Cookie Base
Base
Shortbread Tart Crust
Yield
1 x 20cm cake, 8 servings
Active Time
20 min, or 30 min with the shortbread crust
Rest
8 h chilling
Oven
320F for 35 min, then 355F for 5 min
Source
Alex Cordobés, Madrid, alexcordobes.es

Ingredients

Batch
  • Cream cheese, Philadelphia style, room temperature: 400 g
  • Heavy cream, 35% fat: 200 ml
  • Eggs: 4
  • Sugar: 150 g
  • All-purpose flour: 8 g
  • Salt: a pinch

Method

  1. Bring the cream cheese to room temperature, at least an hour out. Cold cheese leaves lumps.
  2. Line a 20cm springform with a large sheet of crumpled parchment standing well above the rim, since it rises a lot.
  3. Prepare the base, either the Maria cookie one or the blind-baked shortbread crust.
  4. Whisk the eggs and cream smooth, without overbeating.
  5. Add the cream cheese and stir gently with a spatula. Stir, do not whisk, since air bubbles crack the top.
  6. Sift the flour over and fold until no streaks remain.
  7. Add the sugar and salt last, mixing to a smooth homogeneous batter. The order matters for the texture.
  8. Pour over the base and tap the tin to release trapped air.
  9. Bake 35 minutes at 320F. Edges set, centre still wobbling.
  10. Raise to 355F for 5 minutes for the dark caramelised top.
  11. Chill 8 hours before cutting.

Notes

  • Basque style, so the dark top is the point rather than a mistake.
  • Only 8 g of flour in the whole cake, which is why it stays this creamy.
  • Two base options are recorded as separate parts, since the shortbread version adds a blind bake and 10 minutes.
  • The wobble in the centre coming out of the oven is correct. It sets over the 8 hour chill.
