Croquetas

Croquetas de Carrilleras

Yield
~25 croquetas
Active Time
30 min
Rest
Several hours in the fridge
Source
Sergio Señor Galán, Recetas de Cocina de El Mundo

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Butter: 70 g
  • Flour: 70 g
  • Milk: 750 ml
  • Braising liquid from the carrilleras: 150 ml
  • Carrilleras de ternera, braised, boned and chopped: to taste
  • Salt, pepper, nutmeg: to taste
  • Flour, egg, breadcrumbs plus panko (for coating): as needed

Method

  1. Melt the butter and add the sifted flour, cooking it about 5 min to lose the raw taste.
  2. Add the milk gradually until it thickens, around 15 min.
  3. Stir in the chopped carrillera meat and the braising liquid you reserved.
  4. Season with nutmeg and go light on the salt. The braising sauce is already savoury.
  5. Pour into a tray, cover to the surface, and chill a few hours in the fridge.
  6. Shape, coat in flour, egg, and breadcrumbs mixed with panko. A second coat is optional. Fry in plenty of oil.

Notes

  • Roux 1:1 (70 g flour : 70 g butter) to 750 ml milk plus 150 ml braising liquid, stated by the source.
  • A leftovers croqueta off the braise. The meat and its liquid give an unusually unctuous bechamel.
  • Panko mixed into the breadcrumbs plus a double coat stops them bursting in the fryer.
  • Salt carefully. The braising liquid brings both flavour and salt.
  • Chill the mass properly before rolling.
  • The meat quantity is given as "to taste" with no weight, so the meat-to-bechamel ratio is yours to set.
  • Header calls for deep oil.
