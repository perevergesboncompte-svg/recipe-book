Butternut Squash Ravioli

Ingredients

Batch
  • Ravioli Filling — butternut squash, peeled and diced: 1
  • Ravioli Filling — black truffle, grated: 1
  • Ravioli Filling — pecorino cheese: 50 g
  • Ravioli Filling — salt: to taste
  • Saffron Pasta Dough — pasta flour: 400 g
  • Saffron Pasta Dough — egg yolks: 5
  • Saffron Pasta Dough — saffron threads: 5 g
  • Saffron Pasta Dough — salt: 10 g
  • Saffron Pasta Dough — egg (for egg wash): 1
  • Sage Butter Sauce — butter: 20 g
  • Sage Butter Sauce — sage leaves, chopped: 2
  • Sage Butter Sauce — lemon juice: 15 ml
  • Sage Butter Sauce — salt: to taste

Method

  1. Prepare the squash: Cook the diced butternut squash in a pan with a little butter until very soft. Blend in a food processor and pass through a sieve. Hang in muslin cloth in fridge to dry out overnight - this is crucial for proper filling texture.
  2. Make saffron pasta: Add saffron threads to 200ml of water in a pan and bring to a simmer. Reduce until almost dry with approximately 20ml liquid remaining. Mix saffron liquid with egg yolks. Combine salt and flour in food processor, pulse in egg yolk mixture slowly until just combined - it should resemble large breadcrumbs. If too dry, add 1/2 egg yolk. Turn out and knead until smooth. Wrap and rest in fridge for 2 hours.
  3. Prepare filling: Once thoroughly strained, mix the butternut squash with grated truffle, pecorino and salt to taste.
  4. Roll pasta: Remove dough from fridge and leave at room temperature. Roll through pasta machine, putting through 4 times on each setting until down to number 1 thickness. Lightly flour to prevent sticking.
  5. Form ravioli: Cut at least 36, 20cm circles (cut extra in case of breakage). Lightly brush bottom half with egg wash. Place heaped tablespoon of filling in middle. Top with dry circle, seal edges, removing all air. Create neat flying saucer shape. Place on lightly flour tray in fridge for 30 minutes.
  6. Cook and serve: Bring large pot salted water to boil. For sauce, melt butter over medium heat, make beurre noisette - let foam and brown. Add chopped sage, then lemon juice and salt. Drop ravioli into boiling water for 3 minutes, add to sauce and gently toss. Garnish with fresh chopped sage.

Notes

  • In this gorgeous ravioli recipe, the pasta parcels are filled with a sweet butternut squash centre, resulting in a gorgeous vegetarian dish. The sauce is simply prepared with butter and chopped sage, but the pasta dough and ravioli filling do require some preparation ahead of time. The result is well worth the effort.
  • Chef's Tips: The overnight drying of the squash is essential - wet filling will make the ravioli burst. The saffron adds a beautiful golden color and subtle floral notes. Use the best quality truffle you can afford - it's the star of this dish.
