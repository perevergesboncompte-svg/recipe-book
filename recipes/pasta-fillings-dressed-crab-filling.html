Method

  1. Whisk the honey with the vinegar, then whisk in the oil to make the dressing.
  2. For the cocktail sauce, whisk the egg yolk with the mustard and vinegar, then whisk in the oil. Season it with ketchup, cognac, Worcestershire, Tabasco and cayenne.
  3. Combine the crab with lemon juice, tarragon and cayenne.
  4. Fold 20 g of the dressing and 25 g of the cocktail sauce through the crab.

Notes

  • Used in Crab with Turnip, where 0.5 mm turnip slices blanched and cut into 3 cm discs stand in for pasta. The mix is layered between two discs rather than sealed inside dough.
  • Three sub-components: the crab mix and the two dressings that go into it. Only 20 g and 25 g of them are filling. The rest of the dressing seasons the turnip discs and is drizzled round the plate, which is plating, not filling.
  • The source's entry for this recipe is a five-line method summary with no ingredient list. Those 20 g and 25 g are the only quantities it states anywhere, and it never says how much crab.
  • Nothing is cooked after assembly. This is a cold filling.
  • The source's own tip: make extra cocktail sauce, it keeps a week.
  • The dish intro calls the vinegar sherry vinegar. The method just says vinegar, and the same word covers both the dressing and the cocktail sauce.
