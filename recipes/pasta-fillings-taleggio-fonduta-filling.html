Taleggio Fonduta Filling

Source
Missy Robbins, Pasta

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Heavy cream: 474 g
  • Taleggio with rind: 340 g
  • Egg yolk: 10 g
  • Salt: quantity not given

Method

  1. Bring the cream to a bare simmer in a saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Drop the heat to low and hold it there until the cream has reduced by a third, 1 to 1½ hours. Watch the bottom of the pan, it scorches easily.
  3. Let the cream stand 5 minutes.
  4. While the cream reduces, cut the rind off the Taleggio and keep only the creamy interior. Chop it into 1-inch pieces.
  5. Pour the cream into a high-speed blender. With it running on low to medium, feed the cheese in slowly until the mixture emulsifies. Taste before you salt, because Taleggio brings plenty of salinity on its own.
  6. Still blending, add the egg yolk.
  7. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour before piping.

Notes

  • Cream and cheese with a little yolk, no wine. The cream is there to lengthen the cheese, which is what makes the texture loose enough to ooze.
  • Only the creamy interior goes in. Cut the rind away and discard it.
  • Filling held several hours or overnight needs 30 minutes out of the refrigerator before it will pipe.
  • The same method works with other cheeses, which is the point of treating fonduta as a technique rather than a single recipe.
  • 340 g of Taleggio against 474 g of cream fills 35 to 40 ravioli.
