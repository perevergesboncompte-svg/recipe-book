No-Knead Focaccia

Makes
1 rimmed sheet pan
Active Time
20 min
Rest
12-18 h bulk, then 2 h in the pan
Oven
425F, 25-30 min
Tags
vegetarian
Source
mine, kitchen notebook

$2.06 (30 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Active starter: 50 g
  • Cool water: 375 g
  • Honey: 20 g
  • All-purpose flour: 600 g
  • Fine sea salt: 9 g
  • Olive oil, for coating the pan: 45 g

Method

  1. Whisk the starter, cool water and honey together with a fork in a large bowl.
  2. Add the flour and salt. The dough will be very sticky, which is correct.
  3. Cover the bowl and bulk rise 12 to 18 hours.
  4. Pour the olive oil onto a rimmed sheet pan. Lay the dough in without stretching it.
  5. Cover and rest 2 hours.
  6. Preheat the oven to 425F.
  7. Gently stretch the dough out, dimpling it with your fingertips.
  8. Add toppings.
  9. Bake 25 to 30 minutes.

Notes

  • Cool water, not warm, which is the note written at the top of the page. The 12 to 18 hour bulk does the work instead of temperature.
  • No kneading at any point. Fork, bowl, time.
  • 375 g water to 600 g flour is 62.5% hydration before the starter, much stiffer than the high hydration sourdough at 75%.
  • Honey is the only sweetener and it is small, 20 g against 600 g of flour.
  • The 45 g of oil goes in the pan rather than the dough, so it fries the base as it bakes.
  • Do not stretch when the dough first goes in the pan. That happens two hours later, after it relaxes.
