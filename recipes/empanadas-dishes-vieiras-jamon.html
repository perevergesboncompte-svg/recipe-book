Empanada de Vieiras y Jamón

Yield
Filling for 1 large empanada
Active Time
25 min
Rest
30 min (chill before assembling)
Oven
180 °C, 30-35 min
Source
Traditional Galician cooking, Rías Baixas — lomejordegalicia.com
Energy Cost
$2.24 (35 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)

Print card (PDF)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Vieiras, cleaned (muscle and coral), cut up: 12
  • Medium onions, finely chopped: 5
  • Green peppers, chopped: 2
  • Garlic cloves, chopped: 3
  • Jamón, diced: 100 g
  • Extra virgin olive oil: 1 glass
  • Fresh parsley, chopped: 1 tbsp
  • Saffron: a few threads
  • Salt and pepper: no quantity given

Method

  1. Cut up and season the vieiras, and set aside.
  2. For the zaragallada, slowly sweat the onion, green pepper, garlic and salt until tender and lightly golden.
  3. Add the parsley, then the vieiras and jamón, and season with pepper and saffron.
  4. Cook until the vieiras are just done, then cool, draining off excess oil.
  5. Spread the cold filling over the dough base and cover.
  6. Seal, cut a central chimney, brush with egg and bake at 180 °C for 30-35 min.

Notes

  • Vieiras are the most luxurious shellfish of the Rías Baixas. Jamón and saffron give an elegant salty, smoky counterpoint.
  • Do not overcook the vieiras or they go rubbery. They finish cooking in the oven.
  • Drain and cool well. Shellfish releases water and would soak the dough.
  • Saffron gives colour and aroma. Dissolve it in a little warm water before adding.
  • Filling always goes on cold.
