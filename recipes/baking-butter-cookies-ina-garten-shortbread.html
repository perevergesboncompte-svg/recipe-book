Baking / Butter cookies

Ina Garten's Shortbread

Yield
24 cookies
Rest
2 h minimum
Oven
350F, 12-15 min
Source
Ina Garten, via The Kitchn

Ingredients

Batch
  • Unsalted butter, room temperature: 340 g
  • Granulated sugar: 100 g
  • All-purpose flour: 360 g
  • Salt: 0.25 tsp
  • Vanilla extract: 5 ml

Method

  1. Cream the butter and sugar on medium-high about 3 minutes until light and fluffy.
  2. Mix in the vanilla and salt.
  3. Add the flour gradually on low, just until combined.
  4. Halve the dough, flatten each into a disk, wrap and chill at least 2 hours.
  5. Roll to about 1/4 inch on a floured surface and cut shapes.
  6. Bake on ungreased sheets at 350F for 12 to 15 minutes, edges lightly golden, centres still pale.
  7. Cool 5 minutes on the sheet. They crisp as they cool.

Notes

  • Won The Kitchn's shortbread comparison outright, which is why it was collected.
  • More front-loaded work than the pan shortbreads, since it wants chilling, rolling and cutting.
  • Hotter than most shortbread recipes, which is what gives the crisp.
  • Best within a few days for crispness, though the flavour keeps improving.
