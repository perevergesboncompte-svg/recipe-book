Green Dough drafted

Makes
998 g
Active Time
8-10 min kneading, plus blanching
Rest
30 min minimum, wrapped
Source
Pasta, Missy Robbins

Ingredients

  • Spinach leaves: 454 g, to yield 250 g puree
  • Tipo 00 flour: 630 g
  • Egg yolks: 118 g
  • Extra 00 flour for kneading: as needed

Method

  1. Blanch the spinach 30 seconds to 1 minute, until tender but still bright green, then straight into ice water.
  2. Drain, wrap in a towel and wring out every bit of water. This matters, since leftover water throws the hydration.
  3. Blend to a smooth puree, loosening with a tablespoon of water at a time if it will not move.
  4. Measure out 250 g of puree. Freeze the excess.
  5. Mix the yolks lightly, then stir the puree through them.
  6. Mound the flour with a well 5 to 6 inches across, add half the green mixture, drawing in flour with a fork.
  7. Add the rest the same way, scraping back any escapes.
  8. Knead 8 to 10 minutes until smooth, firm, evenly bright green, with a slight spring back.
  9. Wrap and rest at least 30 minutes.

Notes

  • 454 g of leaves cooks down to more than 250 g, so you will have overage. Weigh the puree rather than using all of it.
  • 118 g of yolks is about 6 to 7 eggs, far fewer than plain egg dough, because the puree carries much of the liquid.
  • For ramp dough, swap half the spinach for leafy ramp tops.
  • Keeps 24 hours. Chilled overnight, pull it out 20 minutes before rolling.
