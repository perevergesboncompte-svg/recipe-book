Empanada de Bonito con Tomate

Yield
Filling for 1 large empanada
Active Time
15 min
Rest
30 min (chill before assembling)
Oven
190-200 °C, about 35 min
Source
Traditional northern Spanish bonito empanada — no chef or publication credited

Ingredients

Batch
  • Canned bonito or tuna, drained: 350-400 g
  • Large onions: 2
  • Red pepper: 1
  • Green pepper: 1
  • Garlic cloves: 2
  • Crushed tomato: 300 g
  • Hard-boiled eggs (optional): 2
  • Olive oil, salt, pimentón: to taste

Method

  1. Sweat the chopped onion, garlic and peppers in oil over low heat until soft.
  2. Stir in the pimentón off the heat for a second, then add the crushed tomato.
  3. Cook until the sofrito is compact and dry. Season.
  4. Turn off the heat and fold the drained, flaked bonito through the residual warmth.
  5. Cool, and add chopped hard-boiled egg when assembling if using.
  6. Fill, seal with a repulgue and bake at 190-200 °C for about 35 min.

Notes

  • Stovetop cook time is 25 min.
  • The bonito goes in last and uncooked — it is already cooked, and cooking it again dries it out.
  • Reduce the tomato sofrito properly. Excess liquid softens the base.
  • Use the oil from the bonito tin for the sofrito for more flavour.
  • Hard-boiled egg is a classic addition that gives body.
  • Filling always goes on cold.
