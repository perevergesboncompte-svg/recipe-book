Empanadas / Dishes

Empanada de Pollo

Yield
Filling for 1 large empanada
Active Time
20 min
Rest
30 min (chill before assembling)
Oven
190-200 °C, about 35 min
Source
Traditional chicken empanada filling — no chef or publication credited
Energy Cost
$2.24 (35 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)

Ingredients

Batch
  • Chicken (breast and/or thigh), in small dice: 500 g
  • Onions: 2
  • Red pepper: 1
  • Green pepper: 1
  • Garlic cloves: 2
  • Crushed tomato: 200 g
  • Hard-boiled eggs (optional): 2
  • Oil, salt, pepper, pimentón, white wine: to taste

Method

  1. Brown the diced chicken in oil, then lift it out and set aside.
  2. In the same oil, sweat the onion, garlic and peppers until soft.
  3. Add pimentón and crushed tomato, and cook down.
  4. Return the chicken, splash in white wine, season and cook until juicy.
  5. Cool, then fold in chopped hard-boiled egg if using.
  6. Fill, seal with a repulgue and bake at 190-200 °C for about 35 min.

Notes

  • Stovetop cook time is 25 min.
  • Thigh stays juicier than breast. A mix of both works.
  • Shredded leftover roast chicken makes this an ideal use-it-up recipe.
  • Drain well. The filling should be juicy but not wet.
  • Hard-boiled egg adds body and is very traditional here.
  • Filling always goes on cold.
