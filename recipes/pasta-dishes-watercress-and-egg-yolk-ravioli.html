Watercress and Egg Yolk Ravioli

Filling
Watercress and Egg Yolk Filling
Shape
Ravioli
Serves
2
Total Time
2 h
Source
Great British Chefs
Chef
Ruth Hansom
Filling Note
Watercress with a raw egg yolk
Pasta Colour
Yellow
Difficulty
Medium

Ingredients

Batch
  • Pasta Dough — 00 flour: 100 g
  • Pasta Dough — egg: 1
  • Pasta Dough — egg yolk: 1
  • Pasta Dough — rapeseed oil: 1 dash
  • Pasta Dough — salt: 1 pinch
  • Pasta Filling — watercress (leaves picked, stalks reserved): 6 handfuls
  • Pasta Filling — onion: 1
  • Pasta Filling — lime, zested: 1
  • Pasta Filling — pecorino (or Lord of The Hundreds): 50 g
  • Pasta Filling — mascarpone: 2 tbsp
  • Pasta Filling — salt and pepper: to taste
  • Pasta Filling — egg yolks (large, raw - for filling): 3
  • Celeriac Purée — celeriac, peeled and diced: 1/2
  • Celeriac Purée — whole milk: 50 ml
  • Celeriac Purée — unsalted butter, chilled and diced: 25 g
  • Celeriac Purée — salt: to taste
  • Sauce — button mushrooms, sliced: 100 g
  • Sauce — garlic clove, halved: 1
  • Sauce — Madeira: 50 g
  • Sauce — vegetable stock: 125 ml
  • Sauce — double cream: 125 ml
  • Sauce — salt and sugar: to taste
  • Garnish — Jerusalem artichoke: 1
  • Garnish — vegetable oil: for frying
  • Garnish — mixed wild mushrooms: 1 handful
  • Garnish — tenderstem broccoli: 3 sprigs
  • Garnish — rapeseed oil: 1 dash
  • Garnish — butter: 2 knobs
  • Garnish — salt and pepper: to taste

Method

  1. Make pasta dough: Place flour in food processor with egg and yolk, blend until dough comes together. Tip out, knead 5 minutes until smooth and elastic. Wrap in cling film, rest in fridge at least 30 minutes (up to 24 hours).
  2. Prepare filling: Pick watercress leaves, set stalks aside. Reserve few small leaves for garnish, blanch rest in boiling water 30 seconds. Chill quickly in iced water, squeeze out all moisture (use tea towel or muslin). Chop blanched leaves. Finely chop half onion, roughly chop other half.
  3. Shape filling: Add finely chopped onion to watercress with mascarpone and lime zest. Finely grate in pecorino, season to taste. Use 8cm round cutter to shape filling into 2 pucks (1.5cm high). Chill in fridge.
  4. Make sauce: Add roughly chopped onion, watercress stalks, button mushrooms and garlic to pan with dash oil and season. Cook 5 minutes until softened. Deglaze with Madeira, simmer until reduced by half. Add stock and cream, bring to boil. Remove from heat, cover with clingfilm, leave to infuse 1 hour.
  5. Make celeriac purée: Boil or steam celeriac until tender (around 20 minutes). Drain, transfer to blender. Blitz with milk and cold butter, season. Pass through fine sieve into clean pan.
  6. Roll pasta: Lightly flour work surface, unwrap dough. Roll until thin enough for machine, then roll through machine twice at widest setting. Continue making setting thinner by 1 each time until lowest setting. Cut into 4 equal 12cm squares.
  7. Form ravioli: Place discs of filling on 2 squares. Make small well in each, carefully nestle in egg yolk. Beat remaining yolk, brush around filling. Top with another square, seal carefully around edges - don't leave air bubbles. Use dull side of 9cm round cutter to press around filling, flip and use sharp side to trim. Rest in fridge 20 minutes on semolina-dusted tray.
  8. Prepare artichoke crisps: Slice artichokes finely on mandoline. Deep-fry at 140°C until lightly golden, drain on kitchen paper. Season with salt.
  9. Strain sauce: After infusing 1 hour, strain through fine sieve into clean pan. Season to taste with salt and sugar.
  10. Cook and assemble: Set up large pan salted boiling water and small frying pan over medium heat with rapeseed oil. Cook ravioli 3-4 minutes, add broccoli for last minute. Meanwhile gently reheat celeriac purée and sauce. Add wild mushrooms to frying pan, cook until coloring, add butter and remaining onion. Season.
  11. Plate: Drain ravioli, transfer to frying pan with butter and splash pasta water. Start with swipe of celeriac puree. Top with mushrooms, broccoli, artichoke crisps. Add cheese shavings and small watercress leaves. Place ravioli next to mushrooms. Use hand blender to whip sauce into foam, spoon over ravioli to finish.

Notes

  • A large, single raviolo filled with cheese, watercress and a runny egg yolk takes centre stage in this beautiful starter. The creamy celeriac purée is topped with wild mushrooms, broccoli and Jerusalem artichoke crisps, while the foamy watercress and mushroom sauce adds bags of earthy autumnal flavour.
  • Chef's Tips: The raw egg yolk is the star of this dish - it creates a wonderful rich sauce when the raviolo is cut. The trick is handling the delicate yolk without breaking it. This is an autumn/winter dish perfect for dinner parties.
