Baking / Tarts / Dough

Pâte Sablée, Rubbing-In Method drafted

Yield
1 x 23cm tart base, serves 6
Active Time
15 min
Rest
2 h
Source
French Pâtisserie, Ferrandi Paris (Flammarion, 2017)

Ingredients

Batch
  • All-purpose flour: 125 g
  • Confectioners' sugar: 65 g
  • Unsalted butter, diced and frozen 10-15 min: 65 g
  • Egg, lightly beaten: 25 g
  • Salt: 0.5 g

Method

  1. Sift the flour and confectioners' sugar together.
  2. Rub the cold diced butter in with your fingertips, lifting and dropping rather than pressing, so it stays cold and aerated.
  3. Keep going until it is uniformly like coarse sand with no visible butter pieces. Getting this right before the egg goes in is the whole technique.
  4. Make a well, pour in the beaten egg and salt.
  5. Draw the dough inward with a scraper, cutting and folding rather than stirring.
  6. Once the egg is mostly in, knead with the palm 1 to 2 minutes until smooth. Work fast and light.
  7. Flatten into a disk, wrap, chill at least 2 hours to relax the gluten.

Notes

  • Butter goes in the freezer first here, unlike pâte brisée where chilled is enough.
  • 65 g sugar against 125 g flour, so this is the sweet one, more than ten times the sugar of the brisée.
  • No mixer. Hands only, and quickly.
  • Keeps 3 days refrigerated, well wrapped.
