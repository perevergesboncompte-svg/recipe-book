Baking / Tarts / Dough

Pate Sablee, Crumbling Method

Yield
1 x 23 cm tart base, serves 6
Active Time
15 min
Rest
2 h
Source
French Patisserie, Ferrandi Paris

Ingredients

Batch
  • All-purpose flour: 125 g
  • Confectioners' sugar: 65 g
  • Unsalted butter, diced and well chilled: 65 g
  • Egg, lightly beaten: 25 g
  • Salt: 0.5 g

Method

  1. Freeze the diced butter 10 to 15 minutes first.
  2. Sift the flour and confectioners' sugar together.
  3. Rub the cold butter in with your fingertips, lifting and dropping rather than pressing, working fast so it stays cold.
  4. Keep going until it is uniformly like coarse sand with no visible butter. Getting this fully even before the egg goes in is the whole trick.
  5. Make a well, add the beaten egg and salt, and draw it together with a scraper.
  6. Wrap and chill 2 hours.

Notes

  • 65 g of sugar against 125 g of flour, so this is the sweet one. Half the flour weight in sugar.
  • Frozen butter, not merely chilled, which is what separates this from pate brisee.
  • Keeps 3 days refrigerated, well wrapped.
