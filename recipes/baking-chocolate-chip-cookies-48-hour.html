Baking / Chocolate chip cookies

48-Hour Chocolate Chip drafted

Yield
18 cookies
Rest
48 h minimum
Oven
375F, 12-14 min
Source
Alvin Zhou

Ingredients

  • Unsalted butter, for the toffee: 113 g
  • Light brown sugar, for the toffee: 220 g
  • Kosher salt, for the toffee: 5 g
  • Unsalted butter, to brown: 226 g
  • Ice cubes: 2, to stop the browning
  • Granulated sugar: 100 g
  • Dark brown sugar: 330 g
  • Espresso powder: 6 g
  • Kosher salt: 12 g
  • Baking soda: 3 g
  • Eggs: 2
  • Vanilla extract: 10 ml
  • All-purpose flour: 313 g
  • 70% cacao chocolate, chopped: 170 g
  • Maldon sea salt: to top

Method

  1. Make the toffee: melt the butter, add brown sugar and salt, cook until thick, cool completely.
  2. Brown the other butter, drop in the ice cubes to halt it, then cool until solid.
  3. Cream the solid brown butter with both sugars until light.
  4. Mix in espresso powder, salt and baking soda.
  5. Add eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
  6. Add flour, mix to just combined.
  7. Fold in the chopped chocolate, then the cooled toffee pieces.
  8. Chill 48 hours. That wait is the entire premise.
  9. Scoop 7.5 cm apart, top with Maldon, bake at 375F for 12 to 14 minutes.

Notes

  • Two sub-preparations before the dough even starts, toffee and brown butter.
  • Espresso powder and 12 g salt are both doing flavour work against 430 g of sugar.
  • Longest lead time in the set by far.
