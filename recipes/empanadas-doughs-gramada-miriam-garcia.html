Baking Pasta Croquetas Empanadas

Empanadas / Doughs

Masa Gramada, Miriam García

Yield
1 large empanada, base + tapa
Active Time
20 min
Rest
1 to 1½ h ferment
Oven
190 °C fan, or 210 °C without fan, about 30 min
Source
Miriam García, "El invitado de invierno", contributor to El Comidista

Ingredients

Batch
  • Strong wheat flour (harina de fuerza, not de mucha fuerza): 600 g
  • Water: 300 g
  • Salt: 10 g
  • Instant yeast (or 10 g fresh): 5 g
  • Oil from the sofrito, added after fermenting: 40 g

Method

  1. Mix the flour, water, salt and yeast and knead to a smooth dough.
  2. Work it until it passes the windowpane test, stretching without tearing.
  3. Ferment 1 to 1½ h until doubled.
  4. Knead the sofrito oil in after the first rise, not before.
  5. Divide in two, roll base and tapa, fill with cold relleno and seal the edges.
  6. Bake at 190 °C with fan, or 210 °C without, about 30 min.

Notes

  • Hydration is a clean 50%, 300 g water to 600 g flour, which the source calls "de libro de panadería". The fat is small and late: 40 g of oil, 6.7% of flour weight, the lowest of the six doughs here.
  • The technique that defines it: the sofrito oil goes in after fermentation, not before, so the yeast works better.
  • Use harina de fuerza and not de mucha fuerza. It gives structure without going leathery.
  • Produces a juicy, bread-like empanada with a springy crumb.
  • A fan oven browns more evenly, so watch the last few minutes.
  • The clearest taught of the gramada doughs, good for learning the technique.
