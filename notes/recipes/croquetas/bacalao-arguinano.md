# Croquetas de Bacalao, Arguiñano

- Section: croquetas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: ~25-30 croquetas
- Active time: 30 min
- Rest: Chill completely
- Source: Karlos Arguiñano, Hogarmania

## Ingredients

- Bacalao, flaked and desalted: 200 g
- Cebolleta (spring onion): 1
- Garlic cloves: 2
- Flour: 100 g
- Milk: 1 l
- Extra virgin olive oil (or 100 g butter): 100 ml
- Salt and parsley: to taste
- Flour and breadcrumbs (for coating): as needed
- Beaten eggs (for coating): 3
- Extra virgin olive oil for frying: as needed

## Method

1. Sweat the cebolleta in the oil, add the garlic and the flaked bacalao, and cook 2 min.
2. Add the flour and cook it out for 2 min.
3. Pour in the milk gradually, whisking, and cook the bechamel about 20 min until thick. Season and add the parsley.
4. Transfer to a tray, cover to the surface, and chill completely.
5. Shape, flour, then egg and breadcrumbs.
6. Fry 3-4 min until golden and drain.

## Notes

- Roux 1:1 by weight (100 g flour : 100 g fat) to 1 l milk, described by the source as the canonical thick bechamel. The fat is either 100 ml extra virgin olive oil or 100 g butter.
- Desalt the bacalao properly, 24-36 h with water changes, or buy flaked cod already desalted.
- Taste before you salt. The cod brings its own.
- Cover to the surface while it cools so it does not skin over.
- Fry in plenty of hot oil, in batches.
- No rest time given, only the instruction to chill completely.
