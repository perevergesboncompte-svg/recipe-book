# Croquetas de Pollo, Arguiñano Style

- Section: croquetas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: ~25 croquetas
- Active time: 30 min
- Rest: 4 h (overnight is better)
- Source: In the style of Karlos Arguiñano, okdiario

## Ingredients

- Cooked or roast chicken, shredded: 250 g
- Medium onion, chopped: 1
- Butter: 60 g
- Flour: 60 g
- Whole milk: 600 ml
- Extra virgin olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Salt, white pepper, nutmeg (optional): to taste
- Eggs (for coating): 2
- Flour and breadcrumbs (for coating): as needed
- Mild oil for frying: as needed

## Method

1. Sweat the onion in the butter and oil until translucent, then add the chicken and cook briefly.
2. Dust in the flour, stir 2 min, and add the milk gradually until the bechamel is thick enough to pull away from the pan.
3. Transfer to a tray, cover to the surface, cool, and refrigerate at least 4 h. Overnight is better.
4. Shape and coat in flour, egg and breadcrumbs. Coat twice for a heavier crust.
5. Fry at about 175 °C in small batches, roughly 3 min a side, until golden. Drain.

## Notes

- Roux 1:1 (60 g flour : 60 g butter : 600 ml milk), stated by the source as thick enough to pull away from the pan.
- This is the canonical leftovers croqueta. It works with roast chicken or with chicken pulled out of a cocido.
- Double coating for a thicker, crunchier crust.
- 175 °C in small batches so the oil temperature holds.
- Chill the mass properly before shaping.
- Credited as "in the style of" Arguiñano rather than his own published recipe.
