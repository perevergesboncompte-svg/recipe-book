# Croquetas de Queso Azul

- Section: croquetas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 6 people (~24 croquetas)
- Active time: 30 min
- Rest: 2 h (plus 30 min in the freezer if the mass is loose)
- Source: Directo al Paladar (Pakus)

## Ingredients

- Wheat flour: 75 g
- Butter: 75 g
- Milk: 750 ml
- Salt: to taste
- Roquefort (or Cabrales), crumbled: 50 g
- Eggs (for coating): 2
- Breadcrumbs: 50 g
- Plenty of oil for frying: as needed

## Method

1. Infuse the milk with the crumbled blue cheese, bringing it to the boil so the cheese dissolves. Strain out the residue and let it cool a little.
2. Melt the butter, add the flour, and make a lightly toasted roux.
3. Stir the infused milk into the roux and work the bechamel to the thickness you want. Correct the salt.
4. Spread in a tray, cover to the surface, and chill. If it is still too loose, give it 30 min in the freezer.
5. Shape and coat in egg and breadcrumbs. The source recommends coating twice because the mass is so creamy.
6. Fry in plenty of hot oil. Drain.

## Notes

- Roux 1:1 (75 g flour : 75 g butter : 750 ml milk), which the source picks out as easy to reproduce.
- Infusing and straining the milk with the cheese gives even, intense flavour with no green lumps in the mass.
- Double coating, because the mass stays very creamy and close to liquid.
- It barely needs salt. The blue cheese brings plenty.
- Freezes well in large batches once coated.
- Header calls for deep oil.
