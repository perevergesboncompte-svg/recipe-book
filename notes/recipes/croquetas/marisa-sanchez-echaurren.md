# Croquetas de Marisa Sánchez, Echaurren

- Section: croquetas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: ~30 croquetas
- Active time: 40 min
- Rest: Overnight
- Source: Marisa Sánchez / Francis Paniego, Echaurren, Ezcaray

## Ingredients

- Whole milk, very hot: 1 l
- Flour: 90 g
- Butter: 80 g
- Chopped onion: 50 g
- Olive oil: 10 g
- Diced chicken breast: 50 g
- Jamón serrano, chopped: 30 g
- Meat stock (or a stock cube): 40 g
- Hard-boiled eggs, chopped very fine: 2
- Breadcrumbs: 250 g
- Beaten eggs: 2-3
- Salt: to taste
- Oil for frying: quantity not given

## Method

1. Melt the butter and cook the jamón in it. Separately sweat the chicken with the onion in the olive oil, blitz, and combine with the buttered jamón.
2. Add the flour and cook the roux properly so it does not taste raw.
3. Pour in the very hot milk a little at a time, stirring. Add the stock and cook to a creamy mass.
4. Correct the salt and fold in the very finely chopped hard-boiled egg.
5. Spread out, cover to the surface, chill.
6. Shape, coat breadcrumb, egg, breadcrumb, and fry in very hot oil until golden.

## Notes

- Roux is 90 g flour : 80 g butter : 1 l milk, so slightly flour-heavy rather than a true 1:1.
- Marisa Sánchez's 1957 recipe, Premio Nacional de Gastronomía, later taken on by her son Francis Paniego.
- Very hot milk onto the roux avoids lumps and gives a finer bechamel.
- The very finely chopped hard-boiled egg is the signature of this version.
- Double coating (breadcrumb, egg, breadcrumb) gives a crust that does not split in the fryer.
- Chill completely before rolling.
- Echaurren, Ezcaray, two Michelin stars.
- Header calls for very hot oil.
