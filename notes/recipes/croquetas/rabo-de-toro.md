# Croquetas de Rabo de Toro

- Section: croquetas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 6 people (~24 croquetas)
- Active time: 30 min
- Rest: 5 h (overnight is better)
- Source: Directo al Paladar (Pakus)

## Ingredients

- Wheat flour: 50 g
- Butter: 50 g
- Whole milk: 750 ml
- Sauce from the rabo de toro: 2 tbsp
- Rabo de toro, braised and boned: 100 g
- Onion (optional): 1
- Eggs (for coating): 2
- Breadcrumbs: as needed

## Method

1. Bone the rabo de toro and chop the meat fine. Sweat the onion if you are using it.
2. Melt the butter, fry the meat briefly, and stir in the flour until it forms a paste.
3. Add the milk gradually, cooking the bechamel about 30 min.
4. Stir in 2 tbsp of the braising sauce. Transfer to a tray and rest in the fridge, 5 h minimum, overnight better.
5. Shape into quenelles and coat in egg and breadcrumbs.
6. Fry in hot olive oil, turning them so they colour evenly.

## Notes

- Roux 1:1 (50 g flour : 50 g butter) to 750 ml milk, stated by the source. At 6.7 g of flour per 100 ml of milk this is the loosest mix in the collection, which matches the 30 min cooking time.
- Uses up leftover rabo de toro stew, and the braising sauce reinforces the bechamel.
- Served plain, with no sauce, so nothing masks the rabo de toro.
- Long rest, overnight preferably, to roll them properly.
- Not the same recipe as the TATEL rabo croqueta.
- Shaped as quenelles rather than rolled balls.
- Header specifies olive oil for frying.
