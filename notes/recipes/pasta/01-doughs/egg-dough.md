# Egg Dough

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Makes: 954 g
- Active time: 8-10 min kneading
- Rest: 30 min minimum, wrapped
- Source: Pasta, Missy Robbins

## Ingredients

- Tipo 00 flour: 500 g
- Egg yolks: 454 g
- Extra 00 flour for kneading: as needed
- Room-temperature water: up to 1 tbsp, only if dry

## Method

1. Mix the yolks lightly, without beating them.
2. Mound the flour on a wooden surface and open a well 5 to 6 inches across. Wider than that and the walls collapse.
3. Add half the yolks, drawing in flour from the inner wall with a fork, working round the circumference.
4. Add the rest the same way. If the wall breaks, scrape the escape back into the flour until the mass holds itself.
5. Knead 8 to 10 minutes: press down and away with the heel of your hand, rotate 180 degrees, fold, repeat. Scrape your hands and the board as you go.
6. Add water a tablespoon at a time only if it stays dry and refuses to come together.
7. Stop when it is smooth, firm, and springs back slightly when poked. The texture to aim for is Play-Doh.
8. Wrap and rest at least 30 minutes before rolling.

## Notes

- 454 g of yolks is roughly 24 to 26 eggs, so plan what the whites are for.
- Yolks only, no whites and no whole eggs, which is what makes this rich and tender rather than elastic.
- Dry winter air is the usual reason it will not come together. Flour the board sparingly, since excess dries it out.
- Keeps 24 hours. Chilled overnight, pull it out 20 minutes before rolling.
