# Cocoa Dough

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Makes: 954 g
- Active time: 8-10 min kneading
- Rest: 30 min minimum, wrapped
- Source: Pasta, Missy Robbins

## Ingredients

- Tipo 00 flour: 470 g
- Natural cocoa powder, sifted: 30 g
- Egg yolks: 454 g
- Extra 00 flour for kneading: as needed
- Room-temperature water: up to 1 tbsp, only if dry

## Method

1. Stir the sifted cocoa into the flour until evenly coloured.
2. Mound on a wooden surface with a well 5 to 6 inches across.
3. Mix the yolks lightly, add half to the well, drawing in flour from the inner wall with a fork.
4. Add the rest the same way, scraping back any escapes.
5. Knead 8 to 10 minutes until smooth and firm.
6. Add water by the tablespoon only if it stays dry.
7. Wrap and rest at least 30 minutes.

## Notes

- Egg dough with 30 g of the flour swapped for cocoa, so 6% of the dry weight. Same yolk quantity.
- Natural cocoa, not Dutch-process.
- Keeps 24 hours. Chilled overnight, pull it out 20 minutes before rolling.

## Preserving

Make the full batch, then freeze whatever you will not roll out. Refrigerated dough
is only good for about 24 hours, so freezing is the answer past that rather than
trying to stretch it.

- Divide into portions before freezing, sized to one meal, so you thaw only what you need.
- Flatten each portion into a disc rather than a ball. It freezes faster and thaws evenly.
- Wrap tight in plastic, then bag it. Exposed dough dries and picks up freezer burn.
- Thaw overnight in the fridge, then leave it out 20 minutes to come back to room temperature before rolling.
- Do not refreeze thawed dough.
