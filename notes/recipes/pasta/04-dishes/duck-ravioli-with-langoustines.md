# Duck Ravioli with Langoustines

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: ravioli
- Chef: Mark Jordan
- Difficulty: Challenging
- Total time: 14h 20min
- Serves: 4
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Duck Confit — duck legs: 4
- Duck Confit — salt: 1000 g
- Duck Confit — duck fat: 1000 g
- Duck Confit — bulb garlic: 1
- Duck Confit — fresh thyme: 2 sprigs
- Ravioli & Langoustines — wonton wrappers: 1 packet
- Ravioli & Langoustines — egg yolk (beaten): 1
- Ravioli & Langoustines — langoustine tails: 4
- Veal Jus & Components — veal bones: 5 kg
- Veal Jus & Components — onion, leek, carrot: 1 each
- Veal Jus & Components — red wine: 750 ml
- Veal Jus & Components — langoustine jus: 475 ml
- Veal Jus & Components — double cream: 1.5 l
- Veal Jus & Components — lemon (juiced): 1

## Method

1. Cure duck: Sprinkle legs with salt, cure 4 hours. Wash, cover with duck fat, garlic, thyme. Oven at 95°C for 5 hours. Cool in fat.
2. Veal jus: Roast veal bones at 180°C until golden. Add vegetables, tomato purée, wine. Cover with water, simmer 8 hours. Strain, reduce to gravy consistency.
3. Langoustine bisque: Roast langoustine shells with veg 30-40 min. Add tomato purée, brandy, stock. Reduce to sauce.
4. Nantoise bubbles: Boil langoustine jus with cream, lemon, water. Season.
5. Form ravioli: Remove meat from 1 confit leg, shred finely. Season with veal jus and sherry vinegar. Place on wonton wrappers, egg wash, top with another wrapper, seal, cut rounds.
6. Cook: Boil ravioli 3 min. Sauté langoustines 1 min each side, finish with butter, lemon. Add spinach to wilt.
7. Serve: Spinach base, ravioli, langoustine on top. Veal jus around, nantoise foam, Brazil nuts.

## Notes

- This duck ravioli pairs rich duck confit with meaty seared langoustines and earthy spinach - a remarkable "surf and turf" dish. Allow plenty of time to cure the duck legs and confit them before starting.
- Chef's Tips: This is a project dish - start 2 days ahead. The duck confit can be made in bulk and freezes well. The nantoise bubbles (foam) add a dramatic finishing touch.
