# Polenta Ravioli
- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: ravioli
- Filling: polenta-filling
- Chef: Luke Holder
- Filling note: Polenta, artichoke and truffle
- Pasta colour: Yellow
- Difficulty: Challenging
- Total time: 5 h
- Serves: 6
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Key Components — water: 830 ml
- Key Components — polenta: 100 g
- Key Components — Parmesan: 100 g
- Key Components — butter: 30 g
- Key Components — artichokes: 9
- Key Components — pasta flour: 600 g
- Key Components — egg yolks: 12
- Key Components — truffle (for serving): 100 g

## Method

1. Polenta: Boil water, rain in polenta, cook 1 hour stirring. Blend with Parmesan, butter. Set in tray to cool.
2. Artichokes: Clean, vacuum bag with aromatics, steam 23 mins, cool.
3. Pasta: Flour + yolks in processor, rest 20 minutes.
4. Form: Roll thin, cut polenta circles, seal, cut triangles.
5. Serve: Stock + butter + truffle + tomato + artichoke. Blanch ravioli 2 min, serve with glazing sauce and fresh truffle.

## Notes

- The rootsy artichoke and rich, earthy truffle elevate the delicate polenta ravioli to an exciting new level. This challenging recipe is certain to impress dinner party guests. Make vegetarian by using vegetable stock and omitting Parmesan.
- Chef's Tips: The polenta must cook for a full hour for proper texture. Use a pasta machine for consistent results. This is a showstopper dish for special occasions.
