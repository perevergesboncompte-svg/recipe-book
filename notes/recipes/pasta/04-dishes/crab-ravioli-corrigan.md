# Crab Ravioli

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: ravioli
- Chef: Richard Corrigan
- Difficulty: Medium
- Total time: 1h 30min
- Serves: 4
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Crab Filling — white crab meat: 300 g
- Crab Filling — brown crab meat: 60 g
- Crab Filling — salt and pepper: to taste
- Saffron Pasta — pasta flour (00): 250 g
- Saffron Pasta — eggs (1 for egg wash): 3
- Saffron Pasta — saffron strands: 1 pinch
- Saffron Pasta — olive oil: 20 ml
- Saffron Pasta — water: 100 ml
- To Serve — samphire: 100 g
- To Serve — lobster bisque: 250 ml
- To Serve — olive oil: to taste

## Method

1. Saffron stock: Combine 100ml water with saffron in saucepan. Simmer until almost dry, remove heat, cool. Add 2 eggs and whisk.
2. Pasta: Combine flour, saffron eggs, olive oil. Mix until breadcrumbs, knead to dough, rest 30 min.
3. Filling: Mix white and brown crab meat, season with salt and pepper.
4. Roll: Roll pasta through machine to setting between 0-1.
5. Form: Cut 50cm sheets, brush with egg wash. Place 8 balls of crab filling in two rows. Cover with another sheet, press around each to dome. Cut with 7cm cutter, seal edges.
6. Cook: Boil 2-3 minutes until pasta cooked.
7. Serve: Blanch samphire, dress with olive oil. Heat lobster bisque. Serve ravioli with samphire, drizzle bisque over.

## Notes

- This exquisite crab ravioli beautifully recalls the sea, with its crab-filled ravioli and use of samphire and lobster bisque. Samphire is best enjoyed at its peak in the summer months between June and September.
- Chef's Tips: The saffron adds a beautiful golden color and subtle floral note. Use fresh crab for the best flavor. Samphire is at its best in summer.
