- Status: drafted

- Source: Great British Chefs

Not recorded in the source.

## Method

1. Turnip sheets: Slice turnips 0.5mm thick on mandoline. Blanch 1 min, ice bath, drain. Cut 3cm discs.
2. Honey dressing: Mix honey and vinegar, whisk in oil.
3. Cocktail sauce: Egg yolk, mustard, vinegar - whisk in oil. Add ketchup, cognac, Worcestershire, Tabasco, cayenne.
4. Crab mix: Combine crab, lemon juice, tarragon, cayenne. Mix with 20g dressing + 25g cocktail sauce.
5. Plate: Season turnip with dressing, rosemary, pink pepper. Layer: turnip, crab mix, turnip. Add radish, chives, flowers. Drizzle dressing around.

## Notes

- This light, refreshing starter is simple to make yet refined enough for an elegant dinner party. Fresh crab is complemented by the sweetness of honey and acidity of sherry vinegar, with delicate turnip "pasta" sheets.
- Chef's Tips: The turnip replaces traditional pasta - it's lighter and allows the crab to shine. Make extra cocktail sauce - it keeps for a week and works with other fish dishes.
