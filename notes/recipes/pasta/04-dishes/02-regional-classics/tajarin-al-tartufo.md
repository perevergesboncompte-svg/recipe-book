# Tajarin al Tartufo

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: tajarin
- Serves: 4-6
- Total time: 45 min plus the dough rest
- Region: North
- Source: Missy Robbins, Pasta

## Ingredients

- Egg dough: 1 batch
- Unsalted butter, cold and cubed: 85 g
- Parmigiano-reggiano, finely grated: 68 g
- White truffle: quantity not given

## Method

1. Cut 624 g of tajarin from the egg dough.
2. Put a large pot of water on to boil over high heat and salt it heavily.
3. Set a large saute pan over low heat and put in the butter with about two ladles of the pasta water, 115 g. Swirl the pan until it emulsifies, then take it off the heat.
4. Cook the tajarin 15 to 20 seconds. The strands are fine and go from raw to done almost at once.
5. Return the pan to low heat. Lift the pasta over with tongs or a pasta basket and raise the heat to medium. Toss it for 30 seconds to a minute so the pasta and sauce marry. A splash more pasta water loosens it if it tightens. It is ready when it clings with a gloss.
6. Off the heat, toss 45 g of the parmigiano through.
7. Divide into bowls, scatter the rest of the parmigiano over and shave truffle across each serving.

## Notes

- Allow at least 5 g of white truffle per person, and nearer twice that if you have it to spare.
- The shavings need to be paper thin. A mandoline on its narrowest setting does the job without a truffle shaver.
- Tajarin is Piedmont's egg-rich take on tagliolini. Watch the pot — 20 seconds is the outer limit.
- Butter and pasta water are the whole sauce, so the emulsion in step 3 has to hold. Swirl until it looks creamy rather than split.
