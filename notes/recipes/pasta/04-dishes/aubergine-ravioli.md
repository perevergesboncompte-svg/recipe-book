# Aubergine Ravioli
- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: ravioli
- Filling: aubergine-and-ricotta-filling
- Chef: Emily Roux and Diego Ferrari
- Filling note: Aubergine, ricotta and red prawns
- Pasta colour: Yellow
- Difficulty: Medium
- Total time: 2 h +
- Serves: 4
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Key Components — 00 flour + 75g semolina: 265 g
- Key Components — egg yolk: 250 g
- Key Components — aubergine (for filling): 1
- Key Components — ricotta, Parmesan: 40 g each
- Key Components — smoked scamorza cheese: 150 g
- Key Components — large red prawns: 12
- Key Components — prawn bisque (reduced): 50 ml

## Method

1. Pasta: Flour, semolina, yolks, oil - mix 3 min, knead 5 min, rest overnight.
2. Aubergine: Wrap in foil with rock salt, roast 1 hour at 180°C. Dice flesh, dry in pan.
3. Scamorza sauce: Vac bag cheese, milk, cream, sous vide 30 min at 85°C. Blend, sieve, siphon.
4. Filling: Mix aubergine with ricotta, Parmesan, shallot, basil. Pipe onto pasta.
5. Form: Roll thin, place filling, seal, cut into rounds.
6. Serve: Boil 1 min, grilled prawns, scamorza foam, bisque, samphire, micro basil.

## Notes

- This stunning ravioli recipe showcases beautiful Italian flavours and techniques. The pasta is filled with silky ricotta and aubergine stuffing, served with rich prawn bisque and smoky scamorza sauce. Sicilian red prawns are lightly grilled to garnish.
- Chef's Tips: The aubergine needs to be completely dried out before mixing with cheese. The scamorza sauce is made using a siphon for a light texture. Marinate aubergine in vinegar overnight for extra flavor.
