# Rigatoni Diavola

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Sauce: diavola-sauce
- Serves: 4-6
- Total time: 30 min plus the sauce and the rigatoni
- Source: Missy Robbins, Pasta

## Ingredients

- Extruded rigatoni: 624 g
- Diavola sauce: 585 g
- Pecorino romano, finely grated: 22 g
- Marjoram, leaves picked: 3 sprigs
- Olive oil: 14 g
- Black pepper: quantity not given

## Method

1. Extrude 624 g of rigatoni.
2. Salt a large pot of water heavily and bring it to the boil over high heat.
3. Cook the rigatoni 5 to 8 minutes, to al dente.
4. While it boils, set a large saute pan over low heat, spoon in the diavola sauce and stir one ladle of pasta water through it, 55 g.
5. Lift the pasta over with a spider or pasta basket, raise the heat to medium and toss 1 to 2 minutes. A splash more pasta water loosens the sauce if it tightens. It is married when the pasta has drunk the sauce, glides as you toss it, and almost nothing is left pooling in the pan.
6. Divide into bowls and finish with the pecorino, the marjoram, the olive oil and pepper to taste.

## Notes

- The diavola sauce is a separate recipe, and 585 g is what this weight of pasta takes.
- One ladle of pasta water, 55 g, goes in with the sauce at the start. Anything more goes in only if the pan tightens while you toss.
- Sauce still pooling under the rigatoni means the tossing is not finished. A properly married pan is nearly dry underneath.
