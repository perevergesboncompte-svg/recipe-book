# Eggplant-Filled Mezzelune with Simple Sauce and Ricotta Salata

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: mezzelune
- Filling: charred-eggplant-and-ricotta-filling
- Sauce: simple-red-sauce
- Serves: 6-8
- Total time: 1 h 30 plus the 2 h drain and the dough rest
- Source: Missy Robbins, Pasta

## Ingredients

- Egg dough: 1 batch
- Japanese eggplants, 10: 1.6 to 1.8 kg
- Ricotta: 174 g
- Salt: 5.5 g
- Simple red sauce: 500 g
- Ground red chile: quantity not given
- Ricotta salata: 120 g
- Basil leaves, small: 20 to 25

## Method

1. For the filling, heat a grill to medium, or heat the broiler with an oven rack set as close to the element as it goes.
2. Lay the ten eggplants over the grill, or set them under the broiler. Under the broiler they want a broiler pan or a wire rack in a sheet pan so air moves all around them. Turn and flip them every 5 to 10 minutes until the skins are charred all over and the centers are collapsing soft, 30 to 45 minutes.
3. Leave them at room temperature until they are cool enough to touch.
4. Split each one open and scrape the flesh out with a spoon. It should come away from the skin without a fight. Discard the skins.
5. Line a chinois with cheesecloth, or stand a fine-mesh strainer over a bowl. Pile the flesh in, weigh it down with a plate and refrigerate 2 to 24 hours so the excess liquid seeps out. Skip this and the filling is watery and loose.
6. Puree the drained flesh in a food processor until smooth, stopping once or twice to scrape the sides down.
7. Pass the ricotta through a tamis or a fine-mesh strainer into a bowl. Fold the eggplant into it until fully integrated, then mix the 5.5 g of salt through. Taste and add more. It wants to taste properly seasoned. Cover and refrigerate until you are ready to pipe.
8. Make 80 to 100 mezzelune with the egg dough and the filling.
9. Salt a large pot of water heavily and bring it to the boil over high heat.
10. Set a large saute pan over low heat with the red sauce and stir 1 to 2 ladles of pasta water through it, 55 to 115 g.
11. Slide the mezzelune into the pot and pull the heat back to a gentle simmer. A rolling boil breaks this filling. Cook 1 to 2 minutes. The thickest closure should give tenderly.
12. Lift them across with a spider, raise the heat to medium and swirl 30 seconds to a minute, turning them with a spoon so both faces are coated. A splash more pasta water loosens the sauce if it tightens.
13. Move them to a platter or divide onto plates and dust with ground chile to taste. Grate the ricotta salata coarsely over the top and scatter the basil.

## Notes

- The drain is not optional. Two hours minimum under a weighted plate, up to 24, or the filling will not hold inside the pasta.
- Char the eggplants right through to collapse, 30 to 45 minutes, turning every 5 to 10. The smoke is the flavor of the filling.
- The ricotta goes through a tamis before it meets the eggplant, which is what keeps the filling smooth.
- Pasta alla Norma is the Sicilian original, tomato sauce with eggplant and ricotta salata, usually on rigatoni. Here the eggplant moves inside the pasta instead of sitting beside it.
- The simple red sauce is a separate recipe, and 500 g is what this quantity of pasta takes.
