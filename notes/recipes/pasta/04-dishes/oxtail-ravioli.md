# Oxtail Ravioli
- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: ravioli
- Filling: oxtail-and-celeriac-filling
- Chef: Andy McLeish
- Filling note: Oxtail and celeriac
- Pasta colour: Yellow
- Difficulty: Challenging
- Total time: 2 h 30 min
- Serves: 6
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — oxtail, cut into pieces: 1 small
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — carrot, roughly chopped: 1
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — onion, roughly chopped: 1
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — garlic bulb, halved: 1
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — thyme: 1 sprig
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — tomato purée: 1 tbsp
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — red wine: 375 ml
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — brown chicken stock: 1 l
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — celeriac, peeled and diced: 1 small
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — olive oil: quantity not given
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — salt: quantity not given
- Oxtail and Celeriac Filling — pepper: quantity not given
- Pasta — fine semolina: 125 g
- Pasta — 00 flour: 125 g
- Pasta — egg: 125 g
- Spinach — spinach: quantity not given
- Spinach — butter: 1 knob
- Spinach — salt: quantity not given
- Spinach — pepper: quantity not given

## Method

1. Braise oxtail: 120°C oven, 4-5 hours with wine, stock, vegetables until tender.
2. Make filling: Pick meat, mix with braised celeriac, form into 6 balls. Chill overnight.
3. Pasta: 125g flour + 125g semolina + 125g egg. Rest overnight.
4. Form: Roll thin, place ball, seal, cut 8cm rounds.
5. Serve: Boil 6 min, spinach base, oxtail jus.

## Notes

- This sumptuous oxtail ravioli demonstrates perfectly how cheaper cuts of meat can be transformed to a thing of beauty with time and patience. Each raviolo is served on a bed of spinach, drizzled with jus made from the braising liquid.
- Chef's Tips: Must be done a day ahead - the filling needs time to set. The oxtail jus is the key - reduce until intensely flavored.
