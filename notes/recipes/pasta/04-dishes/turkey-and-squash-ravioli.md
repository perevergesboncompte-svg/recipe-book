# Turkey and Squash Ravioli
- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: ravioli
- Filling: turkey-and-butternut-squash-filling
- Chef: Dominic Chapman
- Filling note: Turkey and butternut squash
- Pasta colour: Yellow
- Difficulty: Medium
- Total time: 2 h
- Serves: 6
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Turkey and Squash Filling — turkey leg: 1
- Turkey and Squash Filling — butternut squash: 1
- Turkey and Squash Filling — onion, finely chopped: 50 g
- Turkey and Squash Filling — garlic cloves, minced: 2
- Turkey and Squash Filling — Parmesan, grated: 50 g
- Turkey and Squash Filling — parsley, finely chopped: 5 g
- Turkey and Squash Filling — olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Turkey and Squash Filling — salt and pepper: to taste
- Poaching Liquor — thyme: 10 g
- Poaching Liquor — bay leaves: 2
- Poaching Liquor — carrot, onion, celery stick, leek (large dice): 1 each
- Poaching Liquor — bulb of garlic, halved: 1
- Pasta & Serving — 00 flour: 250 g
- Pasta & Serving — egg yolks (+ 1 whole egg): 6
- Pasta & Serving — olive oil, cold water: 1 tbsp each
- Pasta & Serving — unsalted butter: 250 g
- Pasta & Serving — sage leaves: 18
- Pasta & Serving — pine nuts: 20 g
- Pasta & Serving — Parmesan, grated: to serve

## Method

1. Make pasta dough: Place flour in large bowl, make well in middle. Pour in 4 egg yolks, whole egg, olive oil, cold water and salt. Slowly fold flour in from edges. When dough forms, tip onto table and knead 4-5 minutes until smooth. Cover with cling film.
2. Roast squash: Preheat oven to 200°C/gas mark 6. Cut squash in half, score with knife, brush with olive oil. Place cut-side up in baking tray, cover with foil, roast until tender. Cool, mash flesh until smooth, set aside.
3. Poach turkey: Add turkey to pot of cold water with thyme, bay leaves, carrot, onion, celery, leek and garlic bulb. Bring to boil, reduce heat, simmer 10 minutes. Turn off heat, leave to cool in liquor. Once cool, remove skin, shred meat into bite-size pieces, removing all bone and sinew.
4. Make filling: Heat olive oil in large pan, fry onion and garlic until softened but not colored. Remove from heat, add turkey, squash, parmesan, parsley, salt and pepper. Mix until combined. Cool, then weigh into 50g balls.
5. Roll pasta: Roll dough through pasta machine, starting from widest setting, going down to between 0 and 1. Roll twice on last setting for consistent thickness. Pasta should be dry but with no cracks.
6. Form ravioli: Cut rolled pasta into 50cm lengths. Place sheet on floured surface, brush lightly with egg wash (2 egg yolks). Place turkey balls in two rows with 10cm gaps. Cover with another pasta sheet, stretch to meet edges, press around each ball to form dome. Cut out with 6-8cm ravioli stamp. Pick up individually, squeeze out air, seal edges. Store on tea towel-lined tray.
7. Toast pine nuts: Toast until golden brown, set aside.
8. Cook and serve: Bring large pot salted water to boil. Add ravioli, cook about 4 minutes until tender and they rise to top. Meanwhile, chop half the sage, heat butter until browned, add sage leaves. Fry remaining sage in hot olive oil until crisp. Drain on kitchen towel. Add ravioli to brown butter, toss gently. Transfer to warm plates, finish with more butter, sage leaves, pine nuts and grated Parmesan. Serve with green salad.

## Notes

- Making your own ravioli can be time-consuming but it pays dividends in terms of flavour and quality. This turkey and butternut squash recipe is something of a revelation - perfect for using leftover turkey, especially at Christmas. The combination of tender turkey meat with sweet squash and aromatic sage butter is simply wonderful.
- Chef's Tips: This is the perfect post-Christmas recipe for using leftover turkey. The turkey leg gives more flavor than breast. Make sure to dry the ravioli well before sealing - any moisture will prevent proper sealing.
