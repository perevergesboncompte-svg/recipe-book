# Pink Prawn Ravioli
- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: ravioli
- Filling: king-prawn-filling
- Chef: Great British Chefs Kitchen
- Filling note: King prawns
- Pasta colour: Pink, from beetroot
- Difficulty: Easy
- Total time: 1 h 40 min
- Serves: 2
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Pink Pasta — 00 flour: 215 g
- Pink Pasta — egg yolks (Freshlay Farms Golden Yolker preferred): 4
- Pink Pasta — cooked beetroot: 65 g
- Pink Pasta — olive oil (+ extra for frying): 1 tsp
- Prawn Filling — raw king prawns, peeled (+ 6-12 more to serve): 150 g
- Prawn Filling — double cream: 60 ml
- Prawn Filling — lemon zest (+ squeeze to serve): 1 tsp
- Prawn Filling — chives, finely chopped: 1 tbsp
- Prawn Filling — salt and pepper: to taste
- Champagne Beurre Blanc — Champagne: 150 ml
- Champagne Beurre Blanc — shallot, finely chopped: 1
- Champagne Beurre Blanc — white wine vinegar: 1 tbsp
- Champagne Beurre Blanc — unsalted butter, chilled and diced: 200 g
- Champagne Beurre Blanc — ground white pepper: 1 pinch
- Champagne Beurre Blanc — trout roe: 40 g

## Method

1. Make pasta: Mix flour and generous pinch of salt in bowl or on work surface. Blend egg yolks, beetroot and olive oil until smooth. Make well in flour, pour in mixture. Gradually incorporate flour until dough forms. Knead firmly 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic (or stand mixer with dough hook). Wrap tightly, rest in fridge at least 30 minutes (or up to 2 days).
2. Prepare filling: Put 150g prawns in food processor, pulse a few times until finely chopped (not puréed). Add cream, lemon zest, salt and pepper. Pulse a few more times to combine. Transfer to bowl, fold in chives. Cover, chill 20 minutes.
3. Roll pasta: Unwrap rested pasta, roll through machine starting on widest setting, gradually working down to around setting 6. Cut into shorter sheets if needed for easier handling.
4. Form ravioli: Take sheet of pasta, use teaspoon or piping bag to add small mounds of prawn filling to bottom half. Fold pasta over top, pressing gently around each mound to remove air bubbles. Use pasta cutter (fluted if possible) to cut square around each mound. Set aside on tray dusted with semolina or flour. Cover with tea towel until ready to cook.
5. Make sauce: Bring large pan salted water to boil (for ravioli). Add Champagne, shallot and vinegar to small saucepan, bring to simmer. Reduce until around 2 tbsp liquid left. Lower heat, start whisking in butter a few cubes at a time until glossy emulsified sauce forms. Season gently with salt and white pepper. Pass through fine sieve into jug. Keep warm but don't boil.
6. Cook and plate: Drop ravioli into boiling salted water, cook 2-3 minutes. Meanwhile, heat frying pan over high heat with dash olive oil, cook prawns 1-2 minutes each side until pink. Lift ravioli out with slotted spoon, divide among plates. Season prawns with salt and squeeze lemon juice. Stir trout roe into warm sauce. Spoon generously over pasta. Arrange prawns on top, finish with dill fronds and twist of black pepper.

## Notes

- Nothing says 'I love you' quite like making fresh pasta for your significant other - which is why this pretty-in-pink ravioli recipe is the ultimate Valentine's Day dish. Filled with lemony king prawns and doused in a deliciously rich champagne beurre blanc sauce, it's a real taste of luxury that's easy to recreate at home.
- Chef's Tips: The pink pasta color comes from beetroot - it's natural and adds a subtle earthy sweetness. The key to beurre blanc is keeping the heat low and adding butter gradually while whisking constantly. Don't let it boil or it will separate.
