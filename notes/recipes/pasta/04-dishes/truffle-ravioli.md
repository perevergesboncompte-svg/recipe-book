# Truffle Ravioli
- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: ravioli
- Filling: truffle-filling
- Chef: Steve Drake
- Filling note: Truffle gel
- Pasta colour: White
- Difficulty: Challenging
- Total time: 2 h
- Serves: 8
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Pasta Dough — pasta flour: 300 g
- Pasta Dough — eggs (+ 1 for egg wash): 3
- Pasta Dough — olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Truffle Filling — ruby port: 100 ml
- Truffle Filling — Madeira: 50 ml
- Truffle Filling — brandy: 25 ml
- Truffle Filling — brown chicken stock: 400 ml
- Truffle Filling — truffle juice: 400 ml
- Truffle Filling — butter: 240 g
- Truffle Filling — truffle oil (extra for garnish): 40 ml
- Truffle Filling — salt: 10 g
- Truffle Filling — gelatine (soaked): 16 g
- Truffle Filling — agar agar: 6 g
- Garnish — white truffle (8 large shavings): 1
- Garnish — pea shoots: 1 handful
- Garnish — Parmesan shavings: 1 handful
- Garnish — sea salt: 1 pinch

## Method

1. Start the dough: Place eggs, olive oil and flour in food processor. Pulse until combined. Knead to form smooth dough, wrap in cling film, rest 30 minutes in refrigerator.
2. Prepare truffle filling: Combine port, Madeira and brandy in small pan, reduce to 15ml. Add stock and truffle juice, bring to boil, skim off any fat or scum.
3. Finish filling: Remove from heat, whisk in butter, olive oil and salt. Place in thermomix with soaked gelatine and agar agar. Take up to 90°C on full speed for 30 seconds.
4. Set filling: Remove top from mixer, leave to set in refrigerator. Once set, blend again and place into small 2cm round silicone or rubber moulds.
5. Roll pasta: Roll through pasta machine, starting from widest setting, going down to between 0 and 1. Fold dough in half before rolling on settings 10, 7, 4, and 2 to develop gluten. Repeat on last setting for consistent finish. Pasta should be dry but elastic.
6. Form ravioli: Fold final piece in half, cut thin strip at folded edge to create 2 equal sheets. Place 1 sheet on floured surface, apply light even coat of egg wash. Remove truffle filling from moulds, place in 2 even rows of 4 along pasta. Cover with second sheet, press gently around each truffle ball to form dome. Cut out with 3cm pastry cutter. Pick up individually, squeeze out air, seal edges. Store on tea towel-lined tray.
7. Cook and serve: Cook in salted boiling water for 2-3 minutes until pasta is just cooked. Remove, toss with truffle oil and sea salt. Place on spoons or small serving dishes. Garnish with pea shoots, Parmesan shavings and slice of fresh truffle. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- The combination of fresh truffle and warm truffle ravioli provides a rich explosion of flavour. This sophisticated recipe makes elegant canapés or a luxurious starter. To make it vegetarian, use vegetable stock instead of chicken stock, replace gelatine with extra agar agar and omit the Parmesan.
- Chef's Tips: The truffle filling needs to be properly set before forming ravioli - don't rush the chilling time. Fresh truffle shavings are essential for the full experience. This is an impressive dinner party starter or elegant canapé.
