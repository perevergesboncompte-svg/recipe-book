# Langoustine Ravioli
- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: ravioli
- Filling: langoustine-filling
- Chef: Martin Wishart
- Filling note: Langoustine
- Pasta colour: Yellow
- Difficulty: Medium
- Total time: 2 h 30 min
- Serves: 4
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Key Ingredients — langoustines (heads removed, reserved for stock): 1.5 kg
- Key Ingredients — langoustine heads (for stock): 1 kg
- Key Ingredients — onion, carrots, celery (2cm dice): 1 each
- Key Ingredients — fennel seeds, coriander seeds: 1 tsp each
- Key Ingredients — star anise: 3
- Key Ingredients — white wine: 200 ml
- Key Ingredients — fish stock: 2 l
- Key Ingredients — endive, finely sliced: 300 g
- Key Ingredients — caster sugar: 35 g
- Key Ingredients — sherry vinegar: 1 tbsp
- Key Ingredients — orange, juiced: 1

## Method

1. Pasta: 240g flour + 2 yolks + 1/2 egg + oil + water. Rest 1 hour.
2. Stock: Brown heads in hot oil, sauté veg, add spices, wine, stock. Reduce 2/3, infuse 1 hour, strain.
3. Langoustines: Boil 10 seconds, shell, chop tail meat.
4. Braised endive: Butter, sugar, caramelize, add vinegar + orange, reduce.
5. Form: Roll thin, place filling, seal, cut 8-10cm rounds.
6. Serve: Boil 90 seconds. Serve with reduced stock + braised endive.

## Notes

- Sumptuous langoustine ravioli is complemented with endive braised in orange juice and a rich langoustine jus. Use fresh, still-alive langoustines for the best results - the sweet meat is incomparable.
- Chef's Tips: Fresh langoustines are essential - the sweet meat is the star. Don't overcook the tails. The braised endive provides a wonderful bitter contrast to the sweet langoustine.
