# Goat's Cheese Ravioli
- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Shape: ravioli
- Filling: goat-s-cheese-and-cream-cheese-filling
- Chef: Andy Waters
- Filling note: Goat's cheese and cream cheese
- Pasta colour: White
- Difficulty: Easy
- Total time: 30 min
- Serves: 4
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Filling Ingredients — goat's cheese: 500 g
- Filling Ingredients — cream cheese: 100 g
- Filling Ingredients — coriander, chopped: 1 tbsp
- Filling Ingredients — flat-leaf parsley, chopped: 1 tbsp
- Filling Ingredients — chives, chopped: 1 tbsp
- Pasta Dough — plain flour: 500 g
- Pasta Dough — warm water: 300 ml
- Pasta Dough — salt: 1 pinch
- Pasta Dough — sugar: 1 pinch
- Pasta Dough — egg, beaten (for egg wash): 1

## Method

1. Make the filling: Mix the goat's cheese, cream cheese and herbs together in a bowl until well combined. Divide the mixture into four balls and place in the fridge to chill.
2. Prepare the dough: Mix the plain flour, salt, sugar and water together to create a dough mixture.
3. Roll the pasta: Roll out the pasta dough using a pasta machine (or rolling pin) to the thinnest setting possible. Put the dough through the machine twice on the thinnest setting to ensure consistency in thickness.
4. Cut and shape: Divide the sheet into 2 rectangles and keep one covered while working with the other. Using 10cm as your measure, cut 24 x 10cm squares of pasta and set aside in cling film until required.
5. Cook: Remove the goat's cheese mixture from the fridge and leave in a luke-warm place. Add a dash of olive oil and salt to a small bowl. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil and drop in the 24 pasta squares, stirring vigorously to avoid sticking.
6. Serve: Strain the pasta after 45 seconds and toss through the olive oil and salt. Carefully place 3 squares on each plate, followed by a teaspoon-sized ball of the warm goat's cheese. Top each ball with another sheet of pasta, chervil, chives, basil cress and cracked black pepper. Finish with a drizzle of good quality olive oil before serving.

## Notes

- This highly achievable goat's cheese ravioli recipe makes a great batch of homemade pasta, perfect for serving with tomato sauce, a simple butter-based sauce, sage and pine nuts, or a pear and walnut salad. The ravioli is unique as it's made with open square shaped pasta, known as fazzoletti or handkerchief pasta, so you can see the filling through the pasta.
- Chef's Tips: The "fazzoletti" style pasta is what makes this dish special - you can see the filling through the pasta. Serve immediately after assembling as the pasta will continue absorbing moisture from the cheese. This is one of the most accessible ravioli recipes - great for beginners.
