# King Prawn Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: GBC Kitchen
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Raw king prawns, peeled: 150 g
- Double cream: 60 ml
- Lemon zest: 1 tsp
- Chives, finely chopped: 1 tbsp
- Salt and pepper: to taste

## Method

1. Pulse the prawns in a food processor to a fine chop. Stop short of a puree.
2. Add the cream, lemon zest, salt and pepper and pulse a few more times to combine.
3. Scrape into a bowl and fold in the chives by hand.
4. Cover and chill 20 minutes.

## Notes

- Used in Pink Prawn Ravioli.
- No sub-components.
- Goes in raw and cooks through in the 2-3 minutes the ravioli needs.
- Piped or spooned in small mounds onto the bottom half of the sheet, folded over, and cut into squares with a fluted cutter.
- The source's prawn line reads "150 g raw king prawns, peeled (+ 6-12 more to serve)". Only the 150 g belongs to the filling; the extra prawns are pan-fried as a garnish.
- Extra lemon is squeezed over at serving, separate from the zest here.
