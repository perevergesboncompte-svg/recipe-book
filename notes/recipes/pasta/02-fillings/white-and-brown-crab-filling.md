# White and Brown Crab Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: Richard Corrigan
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- White crab meat: 300 g
- Brown crab meat: 60 g
- Salt and pepper: to taste

## Method

1. Mix the white and brown crab meat together.
2. Season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

- From the source's Crab Ravioli, recipe 17. There is no dish file for it in this collection, and the PDF's own contents list stops at 16 and omits it.
- No sub-components, and the shortest filling in the source: two grades of crab meat, seasoning, nothing else.
- Distinct from the Crab and Salmon Mousse Filling, which is the other Dominic Chapman crab recipe. No mousse, no herbs, no lemon, and a 5:1 white-to-brown ratio.
- 8 balls to a 50 cm sheet, egg-washed, cut with a 7 cm cutter, boiled 2-3 minutes.
- The source pairs it with saffron pasta, blanched samphire and lobster bisque, all outside this file, and says to use fresh crab.
