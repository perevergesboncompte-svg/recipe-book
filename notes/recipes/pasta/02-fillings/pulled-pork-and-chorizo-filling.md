# Pulled Pork and Chorizo Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: Chantelle Nicholson
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Braise — pork belly, skin removed: 500 g
- Braise — onion, carrot, leek, celery (chopped): 1/2 each
- Braise — garlic, halved: 1 bulb
- Braise — smoked paprika: 5 g
- Braise — chilli flakes (habanero): 2 g
- Braise — white peppercorns: 2 g
- Braise — tomato purée: 25 g
- Braise — red wine: 50 ml
- Braise — chicken stock mixed with veal stock in a 50:50 ratio: to cover
- Braise — bay leaf: 1
- Braise — thyme: 1 sprig
- Braise — coriander stalks: 3
- Braise — olive oil: quantity not given
- Final mix — chorizo, diced: 50 g
- Final mix — reduced braising liquid: quantity not given
- Final mix — coriander, leaves picked and chopped: 1 bunch

## Method

1. Braise the pork belly at 120 C for 4 hours with stock and the aromatics.
2. Shred the meat.
3. Reduce the braising liquid.
4. Fry the diced chorizo in a hot pan until it caramelises lightly and is cooked through.
5. Mix the shredded pork with the chorizo and the oil left in its pan, the reduced liquid and the chopped coriander leaves.
6. Roll into 50 g balls.

## Notes

- Used in Pulled Pork Raviolo, which is sealed between gyoza wrappers rather than fresh pasta sheets.
- Two sub-components: a long braise, then a cold mix that binds the shredded meat with its own reduced liquid.
- The source lists smoked paprika and habanero chilli flakes among the ingredients but never states at which step they go in. They are grouped with the braise here because that is the only stage the source describes taking aromatics.
- No quantity for the reduced braising liquid or the olive oil, and the stock is given only as enough to cover.
- After assembly the raviolo is steamed 6 minutes, dropped into an ice bath, then re-steamed 5 minutes to serve, so the filling is heated twice.
- The chilli, peanut and sesame sauce is outside this file.
