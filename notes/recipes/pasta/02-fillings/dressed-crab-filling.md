# Dressed Crab Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: Xavier Boyer
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Crab mix — crab: 200 g
- Crab mix — lemon juice: 1 tsp
- Crab mix — tarragon: 1 pinch, chopped
- Crab mix — cayenne: 1 pinch
- Crab mix — salt: 1 pinch
- Crab mix — honey dressing: 20 g
- Crab mix — cocktail sauce: 25 g
- Honey dressing — acacia honey: 50 g
- Honey dressing — sherry vinegar: 30 g
- Honey dressing — grapeseed oil: 75 ml
- Cocktail sauce — egg yolk: 1
- Cocktail sauce — Dijon mustard: 50 g
- Cocktail sauce — white wine vinegar: 10 g
- Cocktail sauce — grapeseed oil: 250 ml
- Cocktail sauce — ketchup: 80 g
- Cocktail sauce — cognac: 15 g
- Cocktail sauce — Worcestershire: 5 g
- Cocktail sauce — Tabasco: 10 drops
- Cocktail sauce — cayenne: 1 pinch
- Cocktail sauce — salt: 1 pinch

## Method

1. Whisk the honey with the vinegar, then whisk in the oil to make the dressing.
2. For the cocktail sauce, whisk the egg yolk with the mustard and vinegar, then whisk in the oil. Season it with ketchup, cognac, Worcestershire, Tabasco and cayenne.
3. Combine the crab with lemon juice, tarragon and cayenne.
4. Fold 20 g of the dressing and 25 g of the cocktail sauce through the crab.

## Notes

- Used in Crab with Turnip, where 0.5 mm turnip slices blanched and cut into 3 cm discs stand in for pasta. The mix is layered between two discs rather than sealed inside dough.
- Three of those turnip ravioli go on each plate, 15 g of crab mix on a disc with a second disc over it.
- Three sub-components: the crab mix and the two dressings that go into it. Only 20 g and 25 g of them are filling. The rest of the dressing seasons the turnip discs and is drizzled round the plate, which is plating, not filling.
- The source weighs out all three sub-components, once in its ingredient groups and again beside the steps. The cocktail sauce oil is the only place the two disagree, 250 ml in the list against 175 ml beside the step, and the list is what is recorded here.
- Nothing is cooked after assembly. This is a cold filling.
- The source's own tip: make extra cocktail sauce, it keeps a week.
- The two vinegars are not the same: sherry vinegar goes in the honey dressing, white wine vinegar in the cocktail sauce.
