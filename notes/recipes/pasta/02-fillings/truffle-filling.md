# Truffle Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: Steve Drake
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Ruby port: 100 ml
- Madeira: 50 ml
- Brandy: 25 ml
- Brown chicken stock: 400 ml
- Truffle juice: 400 ml
- Butter: 240 g
- Truffle oil (extra for garnish): 40 ml
- Salt: 10 g
- Gelatine, soaked: 16 g
- Agar agar: 6 g

## Method

1. Reduce the port, Madeira and brandy together to 15 ml.
2. Add the stock and truffle juice, bring to the boil and skim off fat and scum.
3. Off the heat, whisk in the butter, oil and salt.
4. Transfer to a thermomix with the soaked gelatine and agar agar, and run at full speed up to 90 C for 30 seconds.
5. Take the top off and leave to set in the fridge.
6. Blend the set mixture again, then pipe it into 2 cm round silicone or rubber moulds and let it set.

## Notes

- Used in Truffle Ravioli.
- A gel, not a paste. It has to be fully set and unmoulded before it meets the pasta, which is why the source says not to rush the chilling.
- No sub-components, but two setting agents doing different jobs: gelatine for the melt, agar agar for the hold at 90 C.
- Step 3 in the source says to whisk in olive oil, while the ingredient list gives truffle oil. The two do not agree and the source never resolves it.
- Vegetarian version per the source: vegetable stock in place of chicken, extra agar agar in place of the gelatine (amount not given), and no Parmesan garnish.
- Yields 8 canapé portions, laid in 2 rows of 4 and cut with a 3 cm cutter.
