# Aubergine and Ricotta Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: Emily Roux and Diego Ferrari
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Aubergine: 1
- Ricotta: 40 g
- Parmesan: 40 g
- Shallot, finely chopped: 1
- Basil leaves, freshly chopped: 4
- Rock salt, for roasting the aubergine: 1 handful

## Method

1. Wrap the aubergine in foil with rock salt and roast it for 1 hour at 180 C.
2. Dice the flesh and dry it out in a pan.
3. Mix the dried aubergine with the ricotta, Parmesan, shallot and basil.
4. Pipe onto the pasta.

## Notes

- Used in Aubergine Ravioli with Sicilian Red Prawns.
- No sub-components.
- Drying the aubergine out completely is the step the source flags. Wet flesh ruins the texture once the cheese goes in.
- The rock salt is a roasting bed rather than a measured seasoning, and the source puts a handful of it under the foil.
- The 150 g smoked scamorza is a siphoned sauce foam, the 12 red prawns a grilled garnish, the 50 ml reduced prawn bisque a sauce, and the marinated aubergine a garnish: a quarter aubergine sits overnight in 50 ml rice wine vinegar and 50 ml balsamic, then the drained cubes are fried and one goes on each raviolo. All four are outside this file.
