# Butternut Squash and Truffle Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: Andrew MacKenzie
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Butternut squash, peeled and diced: 1
- Black truffle, grated: 1
- Pecorino cheese: 50 g
- Salt: to taste
- Butter, for cooking the squash: a little

## Method

1. Cook the diced squash in a pan with a little butter until very soft.
2. Blend in a food processor and pass through a sieve.
3. Hang in muslin in the fridge overnight to drain.
4. Once thoroughly strained, mix in the grated truffle and pecorino and season with salt.

## Notes

- Used in Butternut Squash Ravioli.
- No sub-components.
- The overnight drain is the step that matters. A wet mix bursts the ravioli in the water.
- A heaped tablespoon per raviolo, between 20 cm circles pressed into a flying-saucer shape.
- The source calls truffle the star of the dish and says to buy the best you can afford.
- Butter is called for in the method but carries no quantity beyond "a little" and does not appear in the source's ingredient list.
