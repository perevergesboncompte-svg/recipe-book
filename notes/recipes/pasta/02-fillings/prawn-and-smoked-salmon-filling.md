# Prawn and Smoked Salmon Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: Georgina Auterac
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Smoked salmon: 100 g
- Double cream: 50 ml
- Cooked prawns: 180 g
- Dill, chopped: 1 tsp
- Salt and black pepper: to taste

## Method

1. Blend half the smoked salmon with the cream to a paste.
2. Roughly chop the other half of the salmon.
3. Put the chopped salmon in a bowl with the prawns and dill, add the paste, season and mix well.
4. Chill 20 minutes.

## Notes

- Used in Prawn and Smoked Salmon Ravioli.
- No sub-components, but the salmon splits in two: half becomes a cream paste that binds, half stays in rough pieces for texture. Do not blend all of it.
- The prawns go in already cooked, so the filling only needs the 4-5 minutes the pasta takes.
- About 1/2 teaspoon per raviolo. The source's warning is against overfilling, however good the mix tastes, because they burst.
- Water, not egg, is brushed round each mound to seal.
- The 1 tbsp dill and the lemon in the source's serving list dress the cooked ravioli and are not part of the filling.
