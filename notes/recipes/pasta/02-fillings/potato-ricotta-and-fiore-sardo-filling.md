# Potato, Ricotta, and Fiore Sardo Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Source: Missy Robbins, Pasta

## Ingredients

- Yukon gold potatoes: 456 g
- Ricotta: 232 g
- Pecorino romano, finely grated: 22 g
- Fiore sardo, finely grated: 30 g
- Salt: quantity not given

## Method

1. Cover the potatoes with cold water in a pot and set them over medium heat to come to a gentle simmer. Give them 30 to 45 minutes, until a fork slides through easily.
2. Lift them out with a slotted spoon onto a sheet pan and leave them about 15 minutes to cool and dry out.
3. Once you can handle them, peel the potatoes and pass them through a ricer or food mill into a bowl.
4. Set a tamis or fine-mesh strainer over that same bowl and push the ricotta through it.
5. Fold in the pecorino and fiore sardo until evenly worked through, then salt until it tastes well seasoned on its own.
6. Cover and refrigerate until you are ready to fill.

## Notes

- Boiling the potatoes whole and resting them 15 minutes before ricing drives off moisture, which keeps the filling from going slack.
- Push the ricotta through a tamis rather than stirring it in. That takes the grain out of it so the filling stays smooth against the riced potato.
- Season it until it tastes well seasoned by itself. It is diluted by the pasta and the sauce around it.
- Fiore sardo is the smoky note. Pecorino romano carries the salt and sharpness.
- No mint in the filling. Cooked mint loses its edge, so mint belongs on the plate instead.
