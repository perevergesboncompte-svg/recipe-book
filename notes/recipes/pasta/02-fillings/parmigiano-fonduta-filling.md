# Parmigiano Fonduta Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Source: Missy Robbins, Pasta

## Ingredients

- Heavy cream: 474 g
- Parmigiano-reggiano, finely grated: 225 g
- Egg yolks, stirred: 27 g
- Salt: quantity not given

## Method

1. Bring the cream to a bare simmer in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Drop the heat to low and hold it there until the cream has reduced by a third, 1 to 1½ hours. Watch the bottom of the pan, it scorches easily.
3. Let the cream stand 5 minutes.
4. Pour it into a high-speed blender. Run the blender on low to medium and rain the parmigiano in slowly, until the mixture emulsifies. Taste before you salt, because the cheese brings a lot of salinity already.
5. With the blender still turning, trickle the yolks in so they temper instead of splitting the mixture.
6. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour before piping.

## Notes

- No wine and no flour. This fonduta is cheese, reduced cream and enough yolk to bind.
- It is piped cold and melts in the pot, finishing with the texture of a runny egg yolk.
- The yolks go in as a thin drizzle with the blender running. Dumped in, they break the emulsion.
- Filling held several hours or overnight needs 30 minutes out of the refrigerator before it will pipe.
- Reducing the cream slowly over 1 to 1½ hours is deliberate. A scorched pan bottom carries straight into the filling.
