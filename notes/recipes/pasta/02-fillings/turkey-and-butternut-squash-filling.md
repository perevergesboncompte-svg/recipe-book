# Turkey and Butternut Squash Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: Dominic Chapman
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Filling — turkey leg: 1
- Filling — butternut squash: 1
- Filling — onion, finely chopped: 50 g
- Filling — garlic cloves, minced: 2
- Filling — Parmesan, grated: 50 g
- Filling — parsley, finely chopped: 5 g
- Filling — olive oil: 2 tbsp
- Filling — salt and pepper: to taste
- Poaching liquor — thyme: 10 g
- Poaching liquor — bay leaves: 2
- Poaching liquor — carrot, onion, celery stick, leek (large dice): 1 each
- Poaching liquor — garlic, halved: 1 bulb

## Method

1. Halve the squash, score the flesh, brush with olive oil. Sit it cut-side up in a tray, cover with foil and roast at 200 C (gas mark 6) until tender.
2. Cool, then mash the flesh smooth and set aside.
3. Cover the turkey leg with cold water and add the thyme, bay leaves, diced carrot, onion, celery, leek and the halved garlic bulb. Bring to the boil, drop the heat and simmer 10 minutes.
4. Turn the heat off and leave the leg to cool in the liquor.
5. Skin the cooled leg and shred the meat into bite-size pieces, pulling out all bone and sinew.
6. Soften the chopped onion and garlic in olive oil without letting them colour.
7. Off the heat, fold in the turkey, squash, Parmesan and parsley. Season and mix until combined.
8. Cool, then weigh into 50 g balls.

## Notes

- Used in Turkey and Squash Ravioli.
- Two sub-components: the filling proper and a poaching liquor for the turkey leg. The source never says to reuse or reduce the liquor, so it is a cooking medium only.
- The 2 tbsp olive oil covers both brushing the squash and frying the onion. The source does not split it.
- Leg, not breast, is deliberate — the source says it carries more flavour. Built for leftover roast turkey.
- Portioned at 50 g and cut with a 6-8 cm ravioli stamp.
- Dry the pasta well before sealing. Moisture at the seam stops it closing.
