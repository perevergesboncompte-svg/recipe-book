# Smoked Potato and Egg Yolk Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: Ben Waugh
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Potato nest — Désirée potatoes (for 400 g cooked): 500 g
- Potato nest — butter: 100 g
- Potato nest — smoked water: 15 g
- Potato nest — whole milk: 20 g
- Potato nest — salt: 4 g
- Egg yolk — egg yolks, St. Ewe's preferred: 4
- To finish — egg, beaten, for the egg wash: 1

## Method

1. Boil the potatoes until tender, drain, and pass through a ricer. You need 400 g.
2. Melt the butter, season it with salt, then add the smoked water and milk.
3. Beat that into the riced potato.
4. Pipe the mixture inside a ring set on 15 cm pasta squares, building it up as a nest, one per raviolo.
5. Set a raw yolk into the middle of each nest, then pipe more potato over the top of it.
6. Lift the ring away and brush the potato and the pasta with the beaten egg before the second square goes on.

## Notes

- Used in Smoked Potato Ravioli, as one 12 cm raviolo per person.
- Two sub-components: a piped potato nest and a raw yolk that sits in its hollow. Pipe the potato as a wall, not a mound, or there is nowhere for the yolk to sit.
- Get every trace of air out from around the yolk before sealing. The source is explicit that trapped air floats the yolk during cooking.
- The dish file's filling note credits truffle, but the source keeps truffle out of the filling entirely: 80 g truffle paste goes into the beurre blanc and fresh summer truffle is shaved over at the table.
- The 4 g of salt belongs to the potato alone, separate from the 8 g in the pasta dough and the 2 g in the beurre blanc.
- 500 g raw potatoes is sized to yield the 400 g cooked the recipe needs.
