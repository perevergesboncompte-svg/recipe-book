# Polenta Filling

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Chef: Luke Holder
- Source: Great British Chefs

## Ingredients

- Water: 830 ml
- Polenta: 100 g
- Parmesan: 100 g
- Butter: 30 g

## Method

1. Boil the water and rain in the polenta.
2. Cook for a full hour, stirring.
3. Blend with the Parmesan and butter.
4. Spread into a tray and leave to cool and set.
5. Cut circles from the set polenta for the pasta.

## Notes

- Used in Polenta Ravioli with Artichoke and Truffle.
- No sub-components.
- A solid filling, like the truffle gel: it is set and cut into discs before it ever meets the pasta, and the ravioli are then cut into triangles.
- The full hour of cooking is what the source insists on for texture.
- The dish name and the dish file's filling note both credit artichoke and truffle, but the source puts both in the serving sauce, not in the filling. 9 artichokes are steamed separately and 100 g truffle is listed "for serving", alongside stock, butter and tomato in the glazing sauce.
- Omit the Parmesan for a vegetarian version, per the source, and use vegetable stock in the sauce.
