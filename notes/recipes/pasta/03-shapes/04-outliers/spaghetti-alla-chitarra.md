## About

From In Abruzzo as maccheroni alla chitarra, spaghetti alla chitarra, and pasta alla chitarra; in Lazio as tonnarelli. It is an outlier that fits none of the other families. Made with egg dough.

Classic pairing: Its versatility is a calling card, so think of it as a handmade substitute for spaghetti.
