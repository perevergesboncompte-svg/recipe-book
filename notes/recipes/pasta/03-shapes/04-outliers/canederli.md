# Canederli

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Formed: dumpling
- Region: Italian Alps, particularly in Trentino–Alto Adige
- Source: Pasta, Missy Robbins

## Method

1. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. Lightly wet your hands and work in batches to form the canederli dough into 54g balls the size of golf balls. Transfer to the sheet pan.
3. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for up to 24 hours.
