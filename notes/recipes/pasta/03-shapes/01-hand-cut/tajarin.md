## About

From Piedmont, particularly in the Langhe hills south of Turin—aka home of Barolo and Barbaresco. It is cut from a rolled sheet. Made with egg dough.

Classic pairing: No one, at least not me, will argue against white truffles and tajarin, but I see the shape more broadly as a shortcut to refinement without much adornment.
