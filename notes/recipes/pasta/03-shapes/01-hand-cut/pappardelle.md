## About

From Throughout Italy, with its densest concentration in Tuscany. It is cut from a rolled sheet. Made with egg dough.

Classic pairing: Any ragù that suits, or with chickpeas and rosemary, a combination that has been burned in my memory since I tried it in Sansepolcro twenty years ago.
