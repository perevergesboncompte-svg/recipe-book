## About

From Emilia-Romagna, particularly Reggio Emilia. It is filled, so it needs both a sheet and a filling.

Classic pairing: A bold filling with a simple sauce, like butter and pepper or butter and pasta water with fennel pollen, which allows the shape to own the plate.
