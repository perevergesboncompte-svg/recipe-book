## About

From Piedmont, particularly the Langhe and Monferrato. It is filled, so it needs both a sheet and a filling.

Classic pairing: The classic preparation of agnolotti dal plin is one of my favorite recipes in this book, but I also use the shape for fillings that feature ingredients that do not need volume to impress, such as sunchoke or fonduta.
