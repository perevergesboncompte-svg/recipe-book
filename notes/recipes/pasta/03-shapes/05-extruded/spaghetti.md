# Spaghetti

- Section: pasta
- Status: drafted
- Formed: extruded
- Rest: 2-6 h until semi-hardened
- Source: Pasta, Missy Robbins

## Method

1. Line a sheet pan with parchment and dust it with semolina.
2. Mix the extruded dough and let the machine come up to temperature before feeding it.
3. Extrude and cut to 18 inches.
4. Draw the strands straight, then coil them into horseshoes.
5. Dry 2 to 6 hours, until semi-hardened.
6. Refrigerate up to 36 hours if not cooking the same day.

## Notes

- Needs a pasta extruder. The book gives no die specification for this shape, so match the die to the profile you want.
- Calls for the book's extruded dough, which is a separate formula not recorded in this collection.
- Solid strand, the same length as bucatini but without the bore.
