## About

From In Sicily, particularly the western side of the island, and in Sardinia as busa. It is shaped by hand, without a sheet. Made with semolina dough.

Classic pairing: While I love the way the shape squirrels away pesto alla trapanese, it’s also a great companion to classic red sauce or fresh tomato sauce and to a hearty ragù like pork sugo.
