## About

From Puglia, primarily, but also in Calabria, Basilicata, Molise, and as far north as Abruzzo. It is shaped by hand, without a sheet. Made with semolina dough.

Classic pairing: Red sauce and ricotta salata or with anchovy, garlic, and broccoli rabe.
