## About

From Tuscany, particularly in the areas surrounding Siena and Grosseto; around Arezzo as bringoli; in Umbria as umbrici, umbricelli, or umbrichelli; and in Lazio as umbrichelli or lombrichetti. It is shaped by hand, without a sheet. Made with egg dough.

Classic pairing: Pici are toothsome and flavorful enough, especially if you make them with egg dough, to be paired with minimalist sauces, but my preferred pairing is a hearty ragù.
