## About

From Sardinia, particularly in the south near Cagliari. It is shaped by hand, without a sheet. Made with egg dough or semolina dough.

Classic pairing: While I love the traditional malloreddus alla campidanese, I’ve got a soft spot for how this shape performs in a light seafood brodo (I have served it with clams, sea beans, and saffron at Misi) or a simple red sauce flavored with saffron.
