# Empanada de Zamburiñas

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Dough: leche-pablo-gallego
- Filling: zamburinas-pablo-gallego
- Yield: 1 large empanada, 6-8 servings
- Active time: 30 min
- Rest: 30 min
- Oven: 200 °C, 30-40 min, with a 15 min preheat
- Energy cost: $2.93 (55 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: El Comidista style — Pablo Gallego, A Coruña, elpais.com/gastronomia

## Ingredients

- Dough — wheat flour: 700 g
- Dough — milk: 200 ml
- Dough — sunflower oil: 200 ml
- Dough — salt: 1 pinch
- Egg, for glazing: 1
- Filling — zamburiñas, 20/30 count (or vieiras): 1 bag
- Filling — onions: 2-3
- Filling — green pepper: 1
- Filling — garlic cloves: 2
- Filling — large tomato: 1
- Filling — white wine: a splash
- Filling — parsley, salt, olive oil: no quantity given

## Method

1. Thaw the zamburiñas overnight if frozen, drain them well and halve each piece. Have the milk and oil lukewarm. Preheat the oven to 200 °C for 15 min and line the tray with baking paper.
2. For the zaragallada, chop the onion, garlic and pepper. Heat plenty of olive oil, colour the garlic, then add the onion for a few minutes.
3. Add the green pepper and sweat slowly until the onion is translucent.
4. Add the chopped tomato. Once it releases its water, add the white wine and cook off the alcohol, then reduce until compact.
5. Stir in the zamburiñas and parsley, season, turn off the heat and leave to cool.
6. For the dough, mix the flour with the milk, then work in the sunflower oil. Knead to a fine, smooth, workable dough — there is no yeast, so it does not ferment.
7. Divide the dough in two, one piece slightly larger for the base. Roll the base out thin on the lined tray.
8. Spread the filling over the base, draining off excess oil, without overloading it.
9. Roll the lid, cover, and seal the edges by pressing with your fingers or a fork.
10. Cut a central hole for a chimney plus a few pricks so steam and fat can escape.
11. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200 °C for 30-40 min until golden.
12. Let it cool before serving.

## Notes

- The dough and filling here are the same recipe as the two component pages, from the same El Comidista video.
- The El Comidista dough proportion, 700 g flour to 200 ml milk to 200 ml sunflower oil, gives a thin, crisp dough with no yeast, so it needs no fermentation.
- Drain the filling well before assembling. Excess liquid softens the base and can split the empanada.
- Fresh zamburiñas can be soaked in cold salted water for about 2 h to clean them before use.
- The central hole, the little volcano, is essential. It lets steam out and stops the dough puffing up or bursting open.
- The original video bakes at 180 °C for 40-45 min. Adjust to your oven and go by how well the dough has browned.
- Better warm or the next day. Keeps 2-3 days in the fridge and reheats gently in the oven to bring the crispness back.
