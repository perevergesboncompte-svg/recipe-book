# Empanada de Espinacas

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: Filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 15 min
- Rest: 20 min (chill before assembling)
- Oven: 190-200 °C, about 30 min
- Source: Traditional Mediterranean vegetarian spinach empanada — no chef or publication credited

## Ingredients

- Fresh spinach (or 400 g frozen): 800 g
- Onions: 2
- Garlic cloves: 3
- Raisins (soaked): 50 g
- Pine nuts: 40 g
- Hard-boiled eggs (optional): 2
- Olive oil, salt, pepper, nutmeg: to taste

## Method

1. Soak the raisins. Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan and set aside.
2. Sweat the chopped onion and garlic in oil until soft.
3. Add the spinach and cook until it collapses and gives up its water.
4. Drain the spinach very thoroughly, squeezing out every bit of water. Season and grate over a little nutmeg.
5. Off the heat, stir in the raisins, pine nuts and chopped hard-boiled egg. Cool.
6. Fill, seal with a repulgue and bake at 190-200 °C for about 30 min.

## Notes

- Stovetop cook time is 15 min.
- Squeezing the water out of the spinach is the critical step. Skip it and the dough is soaked beyond saving.
- Raisins and pine nuts are the classic Catalan/Mediterranean pairing with spinach.
- A little nutmeg lifts the flavour a lot.
- Cheese such as feta or ricotta gives a creamier version.
- Filling goes on cold and well drained.
