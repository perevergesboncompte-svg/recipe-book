# Empanada de Ternera con Pimientos

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: Filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Rest: 30 min (chill before assembling)
- Oven: 190-200 °C, about 35 min
- Source: Galician/Spanish classic meat filling — no chef or publication credited

## Ingredients

- Minced beef (or cut in small pieces): 500 g
- Large onions: 2
- Red pepper: 1
- Green pepper: 1
- Garlic cloves: 2
- Crushed tomato: 200 g
- Sweet pimentón: 1 tsp
- Olive oil, salt, pepper, white wine: to taste

## Method

1. Sweat the chopped onion, garlic and peppers in oil over low heat until very soft.
2. Add the beef and cook until it loses its raw colour.
3. Stir in the pimentón off the heat for a second, then the crushed tomato, and cook down.
4. Splash in white wine and let it evaporate. Season and cook until juicy and compact.
5. Cool completely and drain off excess liquid.
6. Fill, seal with a repulgue and bake at 190-200 °C for about 35 min.

## Notes

- Stovetop cook time is 30 min.
- The pepper-and-tomato sofrito is the classic base of a Galician meat empanada.
- Drain the filling well. Meat juice and tomato soak straight through the dough.
- Add the pimentón off the heat so it does not turn bitter.
- Mince is quicker; small pieces of beef come out juicier.
- Filling always goes on cold.
