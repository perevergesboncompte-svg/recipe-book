# Empanada de Calabaza y Queso de Cabra

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: Filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Rest: 20 min (chill before assembling)
- Oven: 180 °C, about 30 min
- Source: Spanish home cooking, based on andococinando.com

## Ingredients

- Squash, peeled and diced: 800 g
- Onion, for caramelizing: ½
- Goat cheese (log): 150 g
- Garlic cloves: 3
- Grated tomato (optional): 1
- Fresh thyme: 1 bunch (or 1 tsp dried)
- Oregano: no quantity given
- Ground cinnamon (or cumin): 1 pinch
- Brown sugar: 1 tbsp
- Extra virgin olive oil, salt, pepper: no quantity given

## Method

1. Roast or pan-cook the squash with oil, garlic, thyme, oregano and cinnamon until tender. Season and cool.
2. Separately caramelize the julienned onion over low heat with salt and brown sugar for 25-30 min.
3. Mix squash and onion, and cool completely.
4. Roll out the base, spread the filling and crumble the goat cheese over the top.
5. Cover, seal with a repulgo, cut a central hole and brush with egg.
6. Bake at 180 °C for about 30 min until golden. Let it cool to warm before cutting.

## Notes

- An autumnal sweet-savoury contrast: sweet squash and onion against the sharp, assertive goat cheese.
- The cinnamon or cumin is the differentiating touch. A pinch is enough.
- Roast the squash rather than boiling it, so it adds no water and concentrates in flavour.
- Add the goat cheese in pieces at assembly so it melts in veins.
- Works just as well cold as hot, which makes it good for taking out.
- Fully vegetarian. For a vegan version, drop the cheese and skip the egg glaze.
- The source flags these as additions not in the original: the quantity of goat cheese, the cinnamon/cumin, and the brown sugar.
