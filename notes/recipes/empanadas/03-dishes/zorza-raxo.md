# Empanada de Zorza / Raxo

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: Filling for 1 large empanada (tin of about 32 cm)
- Rest: 36-48 h (marinating the zorza)
- Oven: 195 °C, 45-50 min
- Energy cost: $2.75 (50 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: Traditional Galician matanza cooking — La Cocina de Frabisa, La Voz de Galicia

## Ingredients

- Lean pork (aguja or raxo), in small pieces: 500 g
- Garlic cloves: 2-3
- Sweet pimentón de la Vera: 5 g
- Hot pimentón: 3 g
- Dried oregano: 1 tsp
- Coarse salt: no quantity given
- Extra virgin olive oil for the majado: 2-3 tbsp
- Large onions: 2 (about 400 g), with 1 tsp sweet pimentón
- Piquillo or roasted peppers (optional): to taste

## Method

1. Make the zorza 36-48 h ahead: pound oil, garlic, salt, oregano and both pimentóns into a majado, mix it through the meat, cover and refrigerate, stirring every 8-10 h. It goes into the empanada RAW.
2. Sweat the onion with salt. Off the heat stir in 1 tsp sweet pimentón, then cool completely.
3. Spread the cold onion over the dough base.
4. Scatter the raw zorza and the peppers over the top.
5. Cover, seal, cut a chimney, prick the top and brush with egg.
6. Bake at 195 °C for 45-50 min, then cool on a rack before cutting.

## Notes

- The trick: the meat goes in raw but already marinated, and cooks inside the dough in its own juices, so it comes out extremely tender instead of drying out.
- Marinate well ahead, 36-48 h. That is what turns plain lean pork into real Galician zorza.
- Cut the meat into small, even pieces so it distributes properly.
- Add the pimentón to the onion off the heat so it does not turn bitter.
- The onion must be cold before assembling. Warm onion softens the dough.
- Cool the baked empanada on a rack before cutting so it sets.
