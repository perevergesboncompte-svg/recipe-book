# Empanada de Mejillones

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: Filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Rest: 30 min (chill before assembling)
- Oven: 190-200 °C, about 35 min
- Energy cost: $2.24 (35 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: Galician classic mussel/shellfish empanada — no chef or publication credited

## Ingredients

- Mussels (or about 300 g of cooked meat): 1 kg
- Large onions: 3
- Red pepper: 1
- Green pepper: 1
- Garlic cloves: 2
- Crushed tomato: 150 g
- White wine: a splash
- Olive oil, pimentón, salt, parsley: to taste

## Method

1. Steam the mussels open with a splash of wine. Shell them, chop the meat, and strain and keep the juice.
2. Sweat the onion, garlic and peppers in oil over low heat until meltingly soft.
3. Add pimentón and tomato and cook down. A little of the strained juice can go in here.
4. Stir in the chopped mussels and parsley, adjust salt, and cook 2 min.
5. Cool and drain off excess liquid.
6. Fill, seal with a repulgue and bake at 190-200 °C for about 35 min.

## Notes

- Stovetop cook time is 25 min.
- Fresh steamed mussels or tinned both work — plain brine, or escabeche for more flavour.
- The liquor from opening the mussels is gold. Strain it and add a little to the zaragallada.
- Drain well. Shellfish releases water and would soften the base.
- Add the pimentón off the heat so it does not turn bitter.
- Filling always goes on cold.
