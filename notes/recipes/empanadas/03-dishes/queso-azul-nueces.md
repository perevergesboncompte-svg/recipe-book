# Empanada de Queso Azul y Nueces

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: Filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 15 min
- Oven: Air fryer at 200 °C for 8-10 min, or oven at 180 °C for about 25 min
- Source: Nestlé Cocina (Buitoni) — a nod to the Argentine roquefort empanada and to cabrales

## Ingredients

- Blue cheese (cabrales or roquefort), crumbled: 175 g
- Caramelized onion: 175 g
- Cream cheese, Philadelphia type: 100 g
- Walnuts, chopped: 100 g
- Empanada dough (Buitoni): 1 packet
- Egg (for glazing): 1
- Pepper: no quantity given

## Method

1. Lightly toast the walnuts in a dry pan and set aside.
2. Caramelize the julienned onion over low heat with a pinch of sugar, 25-30 min.
3. Mix the caramelized onion, blue cheese, cream cheese, walnuts and pepper into a smooth paste.
4. Roll out the dough, spread the filling leaving a 2 cm border, and cover with the lid.
5. Seal with a repulgo and brush with egg.
6. Cook in the air fryer at 200 °C for 8-10 min, or in the oven at 180 °C for about 25 min. Let it cool to warm before cutting.

## Notes

- Onion cook time is 30 min.
- Not just cheese: the assertive blue is balanced by the sweetness of the onion, and the walnuts bring crunch and a bitter backnote.
- The cream cheese melts everything into a silky paste and tames the blue.
- Toast the walnuts. It lifts the flavour and stops them going soft.
- No salt needed. The blue cheese brings plenty.
- Let it cool to warm before cutting so the cheese sets and does not run out.
- A powerful filling. A thin layer keeps it from cloying.
- Uses a shop-bought packet of empanada dough (Buitoni).
