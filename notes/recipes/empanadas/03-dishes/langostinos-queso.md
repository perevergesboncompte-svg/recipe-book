# Empanada de Langostinos y Queso

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: Filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Oven: 180 °C, 25-30 min
- Energy cost: $2.06 (30 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: Langostino and leek empanada, based on Beatriz Córdoba (oipacyl)

## Ingredients

- Langostinos, peeled and cut up: 250 g
- Large leeks, julienned: 2
- Small onion, chopped: 1
- Garlic cloves: 2
- Red pepper, diced: 1
- Grated cheese: 100 g
- Puff pastry sheet: 1
- Eggs: 2
- Oil, salt, pepper: no quantity given

## Method

1. Sweat the onion, leeks and pepper in oil with salt until meltingly soft, without browning.
2. Add the garlic and, once it colours, the langostinos. Sauté briefly.
3. Adjust salt and pepper and cool completely.
4. Beat the eggs in a separate bowl, stir in the grated cheese, then fold the cooled langostino and leek mixture through.
5. Lay the puff pastry sheet in a tin, pour the mixture over it and fold the edges of the pastry inward over the filling.
6. Bake at 180 °C for about 25-30 min until the pastry is golden.

## Notes

- The source gives no times for the stovetop steps, only the 180 °C oven and its 25-30 min.
- Built on a bought sheet of hojaldre (puff pastry) rather than an empanada dough, with no lid: the edges fold inward over the filling.
- The sweet richness of sweated leek is the ideal base for shellfish.
- Do not overcook the langostinos. A moment in the pan is enough, and they finish in the oven.
- The beaten egg and grated cheese bind the filling and add creaminess as it bakes.
- Drain and cool well. Shellfish releases water and would soften the pastry.
- Puff pastry browns fast. Watch the last few minutes.
