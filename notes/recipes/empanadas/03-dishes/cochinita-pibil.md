# Empanada de Cochinita Pibil

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: Filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 15 min
- Oven: 180 °C, 20-25 min
- Energy cost: $1.89 (25 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: Yucatecan cooking (Mexico) — pineapple variant from Nati Recetas Caseras

## Ingredients

- Cochinita pibil, already made and shredded: 500 g
- Achiote powder: 1 tsp
- Red onion, chopped: 1
- Fresh pineapple, cubed and roasted: 200 g
- Red pepper, chopped: 1
- Oil: 2-3 tbsp
- Salt and pepper: no quantity given

## Method

1. If starting from raw meat, make the cochinita pibil first. This recipe assumes 500 g already shredded.
2. Roast the cubed pineapple until caramelized and set aside.
3. Fry the red onion and red pepper until tender.
4. Combine the cochinita, onion, pepper, pineapple and achiote powder, and cook 3-4 min.
5. Cool the filling before assembling.
6. Fill, cover, seal and bake at 180 °C for 20-25 min until golden.

## Notes

- Stovetop cook time is 10 min.
- A Yucatecan fusion: smoky pork plus caramelized pineapple to cut the fat, and a spectacular red-orange colour.
- The recipe starts from cochinita that is already made. Use leftovers or buy it ready.
- Drain the cochinita well. It should be juicy but not swimming, or it breaks the dough.
- The roasted pineapple carries the sweetness and acidity that define the dish. Do not leave it out.
- Achiote stains heavily. Watch your surfaces and clothes.
- A little habanero or fresh salsa at the table takes it up a level.
