# Empanada de Verduras (Pisto)

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: Filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Rest: 30 min (chill before assembling)
- Oven: 190-200 °C, about 35 min
- Source: Traditional vegetarian vegetable/pisto empanada — no chef or publication credited

## Ingredients

- Onions: 2
- Red pepper: 1
- Green pepper: 1
- Courgette: 1
- Aubergine: 1
- Crushed tomato: 300 g
- Hard-boiled eggs (optional): 2
- Olive oil, salt, pimentón, oregano: to taste

## Method

1. Cut all the vegetables into small, even dice.
2. Sweat the onion and peppers in oil, then add courgette and aubergine and cook until soft.
3. Add pimentón and crushed tomato, and cook over medium heat until well reduced into a pisto.
4. Season with salt and oregano. The pisto should be dry and unctuous, with no free liquid.
5. Cool, and add chopped hard-boiled egg when assembling if using.
6. Fill, seal with a repulgue and bake at 190-200 °C for about 35 min.

## Notes

- Stovetop cook time is 35 min.
- The pisto has to be properly reduced and dry. Vegetables release more water than any other filling.
- Dice small so the filling packs in and the empanada still closes.
- Tuna or egg turns it into a non-strictly-vegetarian version.
- A little grated cheese over the filling makes it richer.
- Filling goes on cold and drained.
