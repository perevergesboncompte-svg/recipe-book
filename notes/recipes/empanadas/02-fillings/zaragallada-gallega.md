# Relleno de Zaragallada

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 15 min
- Cook: 40 min, slow
- Rest: 30 min to cool
- Source: Pablo Gallego, El Comidista

## Ingredients

- Onion, julienned: ½
- Peppers (1 red + 1 green): 2
- Garlic clove (optional): 1
- Tuna, drained: 300 g
- Olive oil for sweating: as needed
- Salt: as needed
- Pimentón (goes in the MASA, not here): ½ tsp

## Method

1. Start the base: oil over medium-low heat, brown the garlic, then add the onion with a pinch of salt.
2. Sweat the onion 15 to 20 min until translucent and melting, without letting it take dark colour.
3. Add the peppers in strips and sweat another 20 to 25 min over low heat until reduced.
4. Drain off the excess oil, keeping it for brushing, and cool completely.
5. Assemble with the cold zaragallada and scatter the drained tuna over the top before covering.
6. The pimentón belongs in the dough, ½ tsp alongside the salt, not in the sofrito.

## Notes

- This is the shared Galician base — pochado onion and pepper — that most of the other fillings here build on. Five of the other ten fillings in this collection name a zaragallada as their base: zamburinas-pablo-gallego, atun-lata-comidista, lomo-berasategui, atun-olleros and berberechos-pepe-solla.
- Must be drained and cooled completely before assembly. Rest the baked empanada 15 to 20 min before cutting.
- Per the transcript of the recipe the filling is zaragallada plus tuna, with no zamburiñas.
- Generous oil and low heat. Zaragallada is a confit, so 40 gentle minutes beats 15 hard ones.
- Quantities are faithful to the transcript, so ½ onion and 2 peppers. For more zaragallada go up to 2 onions.
- Keep the zaragallada's oil to brush the dough — colour, flavour and shine, and the most traditional finish.
- Pimentón off the heat. It burns in seconds and turns the whole empanada bitter.
- Written for the masa de leche: leche-pablo-gallego.
