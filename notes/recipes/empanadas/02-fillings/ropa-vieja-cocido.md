# Relleno de Ropa Vieja

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Cook: 15 min
- Rest: 30 min to cool
- Source: Directo al Paladar (Pakus)

## Ingredients

- Cocido leftovers (garbanzos, meats, vegetables): 550-600 g
- Onions: 2
- Garlic cloves: 4
- Sweet pimentón: 2 tbsp
- Extra virgin olive oil: 5-6 tbsp
- Cocido broth (optional, to bind): 1-2 ladles
- Salt: to taste

## Method

1. Chop the garbanzos, meats and vegetables from the cocido very fine, nothing bigger than a chickpea.
2. Caramelise the finely chopped onion with a pinch of salt until well browned. Set it aside.
3. In the oil, colour the finely chopped garlic over gentle heat.
4. Off the heat, add the pimentón and stir so it does not burn.
5. Add the leftovers and the onion and cook over the lowest heat about 10 min. Add broth if you want it more meloso.
6. Reduce until firm with no loose liquid, then drain and cool completely before assembling.

## Notes

- Reduce it until firm and dry, then drain and cool completely. The source repeats this as a rule: cold and drained, always.
- The aprovechamiento dish par excellence, with a "pringá" texture, juicy but compact.
- Chop everything very fine so it binds and does not tear the dough.
- Pimentón off the heat so it does not scorch and turn bitter.
- The source builds it on hojaldre and bakes with the fan at 200 °C for about 20 min.
