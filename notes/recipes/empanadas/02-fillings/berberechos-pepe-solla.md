# Relleno de Berberechos, Pepe Solla

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Cook: 30 min
- Rest: 30 min to cool
- Source: Pepe Solla, Casa Solla, Poio

## Ingredients

- Fresh berberechos (or 2 tins in brine, drained): 500-600 g
- Large onions: 3
- Peppers (1 green + 1 red): 2
- Garlic cloves: 2
- Sweet pimentón: 1 tsp
- Olive oil for sweating: as needed
- Salt: as needed

## Method

1. Open the berberechos in a covered pan over high heat with a splash of water. Shell them, strain the liquor and keep it.
2. Finely chop the onion, pepper and garlic.
3. Sweat them gently in the oil for 25 to 35 min, until the onion is melting, almost confit.
4. Add the pimentón away from intense heat and stir a few seconds so it does not turn bitter.
5. Fold in the berberechos, mix and adjust the salt. Add a little of the liquor if it is dry.
6. Cool completely before assembling.

## Notes

- Must be cooled completely before assembly, since a warm filling softens Solla's thin dough.
- Drain the berberechos well and go easy on the liquor. It can be reduced separately and added concentrated instead.
- The filling quantities are estimates. The source details the dough, not the grams of filling.
- Solla picks berberechos because they "encajan a la perfección", and the same base takes carne, atún, pollo or pulpo.
- Take your time on the onion. A very well pochada zaragallada is the secret of a Galician empanada.
- Pimentón last and off the heat.
- Written for fina-pepe-solla.
- Pepe Solla, Casa Solla, Poio, 1 Michelin star.
