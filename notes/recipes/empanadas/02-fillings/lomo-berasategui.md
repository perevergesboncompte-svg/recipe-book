# Relleno de Lomo, Berasategui

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Cook: 30 min
- Rest: 30 min to cool
- Source: Martín Berasategui, El Diario Vasco

## Ingredients

- Pork cabezada de lomo, in small pieces: 500 g
- Onions, in strips: 700 g
- Small green pepper, in strips: 1
- Small red pepper, in strips: 1
- Garlic cloves: 2
- Fresh tomato, peeled and grated: 500 g
- Extra virgin olive oil: 125 ml
- Sweet pimentón de la Vera: 1 tsp
- Salt: quantity not given

## Method

1. Season the meat: mix the lomo with salt and the pimentón de la Vera and let it take up the seasoning.
2. In a pot with the oil, sweat the onion, peppers and garlic slowly over gentle heat until very soft.
3. Separately, brown the meat in batches over high heat so it sears rather than stews. Set it aside.
4. Add the grated tomato to the sofrito, cook it a few minutes, then drain it in a sieve — keep that oil for the dough.
5. Combine the seared meat with the drained vegetables into an even filling.
6. Cool completely before assembling.

## Notes

- Must be drained and cooled completely. A wet filling soaks and ruins the dough, and a warm one softens it.
- Cut the lomo into small, even pieces so every bite has meat in it.
- Sear the meat in batches over high heat. All at once it releases water and stews instead.
- 700 g of onion against 500 g of pork, so this is a sofrito-forward filling rather than a meat-heavy one.
- Sweat the vegetables slowly. That is what gives the sweet, juicy character.
- Keep the drained sofrito oil for the dough — the Berasategui dough calls for 100 ml of it.
