# Relleno de Atún con Azafrán, Olleros

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Cook: 35 min sofrito
- Oven: 160 °C, 40 to 50 min
- Energy cost: $2.75 (50 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: Javier Olleros, Culler de Pau, via RTVE

## Ingredients

- Atún or bonito, drained: 100 g (the RTVE figure; go up to 300 g for a fuller filling)
- Onions: 4
- Red pepper: 1
- Tomato: 1
- Tomato sauce: 200 g
- Garlic clove: 1
- Bay leaf: 1
- Extra virgin olive oil: 200 ml
- Albariño wine: 50 ml
- Saffron: 12 threads
- Sweet pimentón: quantity not given
- Salt: to taste
- Food colouring (optional): quantity not given

## Method

1. Start the base: into hot oil go the garlic, the onion in julienne, the red pepper in strips and the bay leaf.
2. Sweat briefly, add the pimentón, and the colouring if using, then cook 15 min over low heat.
3. Add the tomato in strips and the tomato sauce, then stir in the saffron.
4. Add the albariño and reduce until dry, roughly 20 min, until the moisture is gone.
5. Strain the sofrito and keep the aromatic oil for the dough or for brushing.
6. Cool it, and once cold mix in the flaked tuna before assembling.

## Notes

- The sofrito has to be reduced dry and then fully cold before the tuna goes in, so it stays juicy rather than drying out. Reducing the wine and tomato for a full 20 min is the critical step against a raw, soggy base.
- Use the sofrito oil, carrying saffron, pimentón and bay, for the corn dough or for brushing.
- The tuna quantity is faithful to RTVE at 100 g. Against 4 onions that is short on fish, so it can go up to around 300 g.
- Gentle, long bake at 160 °C for 40 to 50 min, then a 30 min rest before cutting.
- Saffron plus pimentón is the colour and aroma signature of this empanada.
- Written for the corn dough: maiz-olleros.
- Javier Olleros, Culler de Pau, 2 Michelin stars.
