# Relleno de Carrilleras

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: filling for 1 large empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Cook: 20 min in the pressure cooker from when it starts to whistle (total 1 h)
- Rest: overnight to cool
- Oven: 180 to 190 °C once assembled, until golden
- Source: Karlos Arguiñano's stew via Directo al Paladar, adapted to a filling

## Ingredients

- Pork carrilleras: 8
- Large onion: 1
- Shallots (or 2 more cebolletas): 12
- Carrots: 1
- Garlic cloves: 3
- Pimientos choriceros, the flesh only: 1
- Meat stock: 250 ml
- Red wine: 250 ml
- Corn flour: as needed
- AOVE: as needed
- Salt: as needed
- Pepper: as needed

## Method

1. Season and flour the carrilleras and sear them in oil in the pressure cooker. Set them aside.
2. Fry the garlic, shallots, onion and carrot, then add the choricero flesh. Pour in the red wine and reduce.
3. Return the carrilleras, cover with the stock, put the lid on and stew 20 min in the pressure cooker counted from when it starts to whistle, until very tender.
4. Lift out the meat, blend the vegetables with the cooking liquid and reduce the sauce until thick.
5. Shred the carrilleras and mix them with the reduced sauce, juicy rather than liquid.
6. Drain in a sieve and cool completely, better overnight, before assembling.

## Notes

- Reduce the sauce hard, then drain and cool completely, better overnight. For an empanada the sauce should glaze the meat, not flood it, and hot juice softens and splits the dough.
- The pimiento choricero is Arguiñano's trick: it boosts the sauce and gives colour.
- Pork carrilleras are sold already boned, so there is only the membrane to clean off.
- The corn flour is for dusting the cheeks before searing, not for the sauce.
