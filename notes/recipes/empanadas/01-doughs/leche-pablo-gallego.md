# Masa de Leche, Pablo Gallego

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 2 sheets (base + tapa), 1 large empanada
- Active time: 10 min
- Knead: 5 min
- Rest: none
- Source: Pablo Gallego, A Coruña, El Comidista video, elpais.com/gastronomia

## Ingredients

- Wheat flour: 700 g
- Milk: 200 ml
- Sunflower oil: 200 ml
- Salt: ½ tsp
- Pimentón, for colour: ½ tsp
- Egg, for brushing: 1

## Method

1. Warm the milk and the oil to lukewarm, not hot. The dough handles better that way.
2. Mix the flour with the salt and pimentón in a wide bowl.
3. Pour in the warm milk and work it into the flour.
4. Add the sunflower oil and mix until everything comes together.
5. Knead a few minutes on a lightly floured surface to a fine, smooth, workable dough that does not stick to your hands. Stop the moment it is smooth and even.
6. Divide in two, one piece slightly larger for the base.
7. Roll each piece thin, assemble the empanada, and brush with beaten egg before baking.

## Notes

- No yeast, so nothing ferments and there is no rest — roll it straight away. This is the thin, crisp style, not a masa gramada.
- The El Comidista video proportion is 700 g flour to 200 ml milk to 200 ml sunflower oil, so 3.5 : 1 : 1. Milk sits at 29% of flour weight and the oil at another 29%, giving 57% total liquid with over half of it fat.
- Sunflower oil keeps the dough neutral and crisp. The oil used to fry the relleno can replace it for more flavour.
- If it turns sticky, add flour a little at a time. If it is dry, another splash of milk.
- Do not overwork it.
- Roll the base slightly larger than the tray so it seals properly against the tapa.
