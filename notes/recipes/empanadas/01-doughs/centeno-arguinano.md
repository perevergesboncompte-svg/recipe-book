# Masa de Trigo y Centeno, Arguiñano

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 large empanada, base + tapa
- Active time: 15 min
- Rest: 45 to 60 min prove
- Oven: 180 °C, about 40 min
- Energy cost: $2.41 (40 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: Karlos Arguiñano, Hogarmania

## Ingredients

- Wheat flour: 500 g
- Rye flour (harina de centeno): 150 g
- Fresh yeast: 12 g
- Salt: 12 g
- Warm water: 150 ml
- Oil from the relleno sofrito: 150 ml
- Egg, for brushing: 1

## Method

1. Mix the wheat and rye flours with the salt.
2. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water and add it to the flours along with the sofrito oil.
3. Knead to a smooth, elastic dough.
4. Prove 45 to 60 min until doubled, somewhere warm and out of draughts.
5. Divide in two, roll base and tapa, fill with cold relleno and seal the edges. Cut a central chimney so steam escapes.
6. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 180 °C for about 40 min, until golden.

## Notes

- The rye is the distinguishing feature: 150 g of 650 g total flour, so 23%, which brings rusticity and flavour. It sits between the cereal empanada and a plain fermented home dough.
- Water hydration is 150 g against 650 g flour, 23%. Counting the sofrito oil as liquid it reaches 46%. Both figures match the source.
- Using the relleno's own oil as the fat is what gives the dough its Galician flavour.
- Comes out springy with a rustic rye edge.
- Karlos Arguiñano, 1 Michelin star, Zarautz.
