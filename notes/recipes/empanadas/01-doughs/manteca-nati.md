# Masa de Manteca, Nati

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: About 12-15 individual empanadas, cut as 12 cm rounds
- Active time: 15 min
- Rest: 30 min
- Source: Nati Recetas Caseras

## Ingredients

- Wheat flour: 500 g
- Lard: 100 g
- Egg: 1
- Warm water: 150 ml
- Salt: 1 tsp

## Method

1. Mix the flour and salt with the lard, the egg and the warm water, and knead to a smooth dough.
2. Leave the dough to rest 30 min.
3. Roll it out and cut 12 cm rounds.

## Notes

- Written for the cochinita pibil empanada, which is built as individual rounds rather than one large empanada.
- No yeast, so the 30 min rest only relaxes the dough before rolling.
