# Masa Casera, Frabisa

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 large empanada, base + tapa (tin of about 32 cm)
- Active time: 15 min
- Rest: 15 min under a cloth
- Oven: 195 °C, 45-50 min
- Energy cost: $2.75 (50 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: La Cocina de Frabisa

## Ingredients

- Flour (repostería or panadera): 500 g
- Flour for kneading: a little more, no quantity given
- Sunflower oil: 150 g
- Milk: 150 g
- Egg, lightly beaten: 1
- Salt: 8 g

## Method

1. Mix the flour with the salt, then pour in the oil, the milk and the beaten egg.
2. Knead to a smooth dough, dusting with a little more flour if it sticks.
3. Cover the bowl with a cloth and leave it 15 min.
4. Divide in two, roll base and tapa thin, fill with cold filling and seal.
5. Bake at 195 °C for 45-50 min, until golden.

## Notes

- No yeast of any kind, so nothing ferments. The 15 min under the cloth only lets the dough relax before rolling.
- Sunflower oil is what the source uses. Olive oil works instead if you prefer the flavour.
- Sized for the 32 cm tin the zorza empanada is built in.
