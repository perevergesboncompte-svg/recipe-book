# Masa de Trigo y Maíz, Olleros

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 large empanada, base + tapa
- Active time: not given by the source
- Rest: not given by the source
- Oven: 160 °C, 40 to 50 min
- Energy cost: $2.75 (50 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: Javier Olleros, Culler de Pau, via RTVE

## Ingredients

- Wheat flour: 350 g
- Corn flour: 225 g
- Sourdough starter (masa madre): 20 g
- Butter: 150 g
- Warm water: 60 ml
- Eggs: 2
- Salt: quantity not given
- Oil from the relleno sofrito: quantity not given

## Method

1. Strain the finished sofrito and keep the oil it gives up for the dough.
2. Mix the wheat flour and the corn flour with the eggs and the salt.
3. Melt the butter and work it in while it is still liquid.
4. Dilute the starter in the warm water and add that next.
5. Work in the reserved sofrito oil, then divide for base and tapa and fill with the cold relleno.
6. Bake at 160 °C for 40 to 50 min.

## Notes

- This is the dough printed on Olleros' RTVE page, wheat-led and enriched. It is not the corn and rye masa de maíz he presented at Madrid Fusión.
- 150 g of butter against 575 g of flour is 26% fat, so it bakes short and tender rather than bready.
- Water is only 60 ml against 575 g of flour. The eggs and the melted butter carry most of the moisture.
- 20 g of starter is a token amount, there for flavour rather than lift.
- The sofrito oil goes in during the mixing, carrying the saffron, pimentón and bay out of the relleno.
- Javier Olleros, Culler de Pau, 2 Michelin stars.
