# Masa de Maíz, Olleros

- Section: empanadas
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 large rustic cereal empanada
- Active time: 20 min
- Rest: 1 to 2 h ferment
- Oven: 230 °C, about 25 min
- Source: Javier Olleros, Culler de Pau, O Grove, presented at Madrid Fusión

## Ingredients

- Precooked corn flour (harina de maíz precocida, P.A.N.): 700 g
- Galician rye flour: 300 g
- Liquid sourdough starter (masa madre líquida): 200 g
- Water: 900 g
- Fresh yeast: 10 g
- Salt: 20 g

## Method

1. Mix the corn and rye flours with the salt in a wide bowl.
2. Dissolve the yeast and the starter in warm water.
3. Bring liquids and flours together and work to a dense, homogeneous dough. Corn and rye drink a lot of water and barely develop gluten.
4. Rest 1 to 2 h to ferment and come to temperature. It rises very little, which is expected — this is rustic, not a springy bread dough.
5. Spread it over the tray, fill, and cover. It will not roll as thin as a wheat dough.
6. Brush the surface with sofrito oil and bake at 230 °C for about 25 min.

## Notes

- The odd one out: a cereal dough, rustic and aromatic, closer to corn bread. Do not expect fineness or crispness. It is the empanada de millo.
- 900 g water against 1000 g flour is 90% before the 200 g of liquid starter is counted. The source calls the hydration ~95-100%. It only works because precooked corn and rye absorb that much water without going claggy.
- No fat inside the dough at all, the only one here like that. The oil is brushed on the outside purely to brown it.
- The corn flour has to be precooked, P.A.N. type. Not cornstarch, not raw polenta.
- Galician rye brings flavour and colour and can be swapped for wholegrain rye.
- Hot and short: 230 °C seals the surface and cooks the inside fast.
- Javier Olleros, Culler de Pau, O Grove, 2 Michelin stars.
