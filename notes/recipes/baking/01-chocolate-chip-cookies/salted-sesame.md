# Salted Sesame Chocolate Chip

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 12 cookies
- Oven: 375F, 10-12 min
- Energy cost: $1.45 (12 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Rest: 30 min minimum
- Source: Dear Saturdays, dearsaturdays.com

## Ingredients

- Salted butter, room temperature: 227 g
- Cane sugar: 100 g
- Light brown sugar, packed: 330 g
- Eggs, room temperature: 2
- Vanilla extract: 10 ml
- Bread flour: 330 g
- Baking powder: 7 g
- Baking soda: 4 g
- Kosher salt: 5 g
- Semisweet chocolate, chopped: 110 g
- Sesame seeds, toasted: 15 g
- Maldon flaked salt: 5 g, to top

## Method

1. Toast the sesame seeds in a dry pan until golden. They burn fast.
2. Beat butter with both sugars 3 to 4 minutes until pale and fluffy.
3. Add the eggs one at a time, about 15 seconds apart, then the vanilla.
4. Fold in the dry mix in three additions, gently, to keep gluten down.
5. Fold in the chocolate chunks and sesame by hand.
6. Chill at least 30 minutes.
7. Scoop about 1.5 tbsp balls, top with Maldon, bake at 375F for 10 to 12 minutes.

## Notes

- Bread flour, not all-purpose, which is what gives the tall chunky build.
- Salted butter is already in the formula, so the kosher salt is on top of that.
