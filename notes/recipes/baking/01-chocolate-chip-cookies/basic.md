# Basic Chocolate Chip

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 12 cookies
- Oven: 350F, 12-15 min
- Rest: 30 min, better overnight
- Price: $3
- Tags: vegetarian
- Source: mine

## Ingredients

- Granulated sugar: 100 g
- Brown sugar, packed: 165 g
- Salt: 6 g
- Butter, melted: 115 g
- Egg: 1
- Vanilla extract: 5 ml
- All-purpose flour: 155 g
- Baking soda: 3 g
- Chocolate chunks: 110 g
- Dark chocolate chunks: 110 g

## Method

1. Whisk both sugars, salt and the melted butter to a smooth lump-free paste.
2. Add egg and vanilla, whisk hard until ribbons fall from the whisk and sit briefly on the surface.
3. Sift in flour and baking soda, fold until no dry flour shows.
4. Fold in both chocolates.
5. Chill 30 minutes, or overnight for better flavour.
6. Scoop with 10 cm between, bake at 350F for 12 to 15 minutes. Add 2 to 3 minutes for crisper edges.
7. Cool completely on the sheet.

## Notes

- Melted butter, so there is no creaming stage and no mixer needed.
- The simplest of the ten and the baseline the others get compared against.
