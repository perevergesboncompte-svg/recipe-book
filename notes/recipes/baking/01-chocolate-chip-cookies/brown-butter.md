# Brown Butter Chocolate Chip

- Section: baking
- Status: tested
- Yield: 26-28 cookies
- Unit weight: 45 g
- Oven: 375F, 12-14 min
- Rest: 90 min to resolidify, then 2 h minimum chill
- Appearance: 4.5
- Texture: 4
- Flavor: 4
- Technique: medium
- Overall: 4
- Source: Sally's Baking, sallysbakingaddiction.com

## Ingredients

- Unsalted butter: 226 g, browned
- Granulated sugar: 100 g
- Brown sugar, packed: 200 g
- Egg: 1, room temperature
- Egg yolk: 1, room temperature
- Vanilla extract: 10 ml
- All-purpose flour: 313 g
- Cornstarch: 3 g
- Baking soda: 5 g
- Salt: 3 g
- Milk: 30 ml
- Semi-sweet chips: 270 g
- Flaky salt: to finish, optional

## Method

1. Brown the sliced butter in a light-coloured pan, 5 to 7 minutes, until golden specks form. Scrape every browned bit into a bowl.
2. Cool 10 minutes, then refrigerate about 90 minutes until fully solid.
3. Beat the solid brown butter 1 to 2 minutes until creamy. It looks grainy before it comes together.
4. Add both sugars, beat about 2 minutes until lighter.
5. Beat in the egg, yolk and vanilla.
6. Add the dry mix (flour, cornstarch, baking soda, salt), mix low until just combined.
7. Drizzle in the milk with the mixer running, replacing moisture lost to browning.
8. Fold in the chips by hand.
9. Scoop 45 g balls, shaped tall rather than wide.
10. Chill at least 2 hours, up to 3 days.
11. Bake at 375F for 12 to 14 minutes, 8 per sheet, 7.5 cm apart. Golden edges, soft centres.
12. Cool 5 minutes on the sheet, then move to a rack.

## Notes

- Cornstarch is what makes these tender.
- The added milk compensates for browning losses.
- Resolidifying the butter is what lets you cream it, which is what makes them thick.
- The 2 hour chill is not optional.

## Learnings

Best looking of the five made at 4.5 appearance, but texture and flavor both 4, so 4 overall. Sits just below Broma and Levain. Longest total process of the five given the double chill.
