# NYC Chocolate Chip

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 12 large cookies
- Oven: 350F, 12-15 min
- Energy cost: $1.55 (15 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: Cupcake Jemma

## Ingredients

- Walnuts, skinned: 100 g
- Milk chocolate chips: 400 g
- Cold butter, cubed: 230 g
- Caster sugar: 160 g
- Soft light brown sugar: 160 g
- Self-raising flour: 200 g
- Plain flour: 300 g
- Salt: 2 g
- Bicarbonate of soda: 2 g
- Baking powder: 8 g
- Eggs: 2

## Method

1. Combine the cold cubed butter with both sugars.
2. Add both flours, salt, bicarbonate and baking powder. Mix until it starts to come together.
3. Add the eggs, mix to a cohesive, thick, slightly sticky dough.
4. Fold in walnuts and chocolate chips.
5. Scoop tennis-ball sized portions, spaced well apart since they spread.
6. Bake at 350F for 12 to 15 minutes, golden edges and soft centres.

## Notes

- Cold butter and no chill at all, the only one in the set that skips resting.
- Self-raising plus plain flour together do the lifting.
- 400 g of chocolate to 500 g of flour is the highest chocolate ratio in the set.
