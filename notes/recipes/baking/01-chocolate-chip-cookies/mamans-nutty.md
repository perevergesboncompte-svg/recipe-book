# Maman's Nutty Chocolate Chip

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 14 cookies
- Unit weight: 100 g
- Oven: 350F, 12-13 min
- Rest: 1 h minimum, up to 7 days
- Source: Vogue / Maman Bakery, vogue.com

## Ingredients

- Dark chocolate baking wafers: 310 g
- Unsalted butter, room temperature, cubed: 225 g
- Macadamia nuts, chopped: 135 g
- Sliced almonds: 56 g
- Walnut halves: 50 g
- Light brown sugar, packed: 298 g
- All-purpose flour: 326 g
- Fine sea salt: 12 g
- Eggs: 2

## Method

1. Mix the chocolate wafers, cubed butter and all three nuts on low about 30 seconds, which breaks the nuts down and spreads the fat.
2. Add the brown sugar, mix low until cohesive.
3. Add flour and salt gradually, mixing only until incorporated.
4. Add the eggs one at a time, scraping down, until the dough pulls from the bowl.
5. Flatten into a 2.5 cm square in parchment, bag it, chill at least 1 hour.
6. Divide into 14 balls of about 100 g, 7 per pan, 7.5 cm apart.
7. Bake at 350F for 12 to 13 minutes.

## Notes

- 12 g of salt is high, which is deliberate against 298 g of sugar.
- The dough keeps up to 7 days chilled, so it splits over two sessions well.
