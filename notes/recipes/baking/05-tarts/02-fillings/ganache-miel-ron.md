# Ganache de Miel y Ron

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: fills 1 x 18 cm tart
- Source: Chocolate Cacao, youtube.com/@chocolate_cacao

## Ingredients

- Milk chocolate: 180 g
- Heavy cream, about 45% fat: 125 ml
- Honey: 15 g
- Rum: 10 ml
- Unsalted butter: 20 g

## Method

1. Chop the chocolate.
2. Add the honey and rum to the cream and bring to a boil.
3. Pour over the chocolate and emulsify.
4. Let the temperature drop, then add the butter.
5. Once the butter has melted in, pour into the tart shell.

## Notes

- Milk chocolate, not dark, so the honey and rum carry the flavour rather than cocoa bitterness.
- 180 g chocolate to 125 ml cream is a firm ratio, roughly 1.44:1, which is what lets it slice cleanly.
- Cream at 45% fat. Lower fat gives a looser set.
- Butter goes in off the heat. Added hot it splits the emulsion.
