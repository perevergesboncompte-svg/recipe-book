# Basque Cheesecake Filling

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: fills 1 x 20 cm cake, 8 servings
- Active time: 20 min
- Oven: 320F, 40 min
- Rest: 8 h chill
- Source: Alex Cordobes, Madrid, alexcordobes.es

## Ingredients

- Cream cheese, Philadelphia style, room temperature: 400 g
- Heavy cream, 35% fat: 200 ml
- Eggs: 4 large
- Sugar: 150 g
- All-purpose flour: 8 g
- Salt: a pinch

## Method

1. Bring the cream cheese to room temperature, at least an hour out of the fridge, or it will stay lumpy.
2. Whisk the eggs and cream smooth. Do not overbeat.
3. Add the cream cheese and bring it together with a spatula, stirring rather than whisking. Air bubbles crack the top.
4. Sift the flour over and fold until no streaks remain.
5. Fold in the sugar and salt with the spatula.
6. Pour into the prepared crust and bake at 320F for 40 minutes.
7. Chill 8 hours before cutting.

## Notes

- Only 8 g of flour across the whole filling, which is what keeps it custardy rather than cakey.
- Line the tin with crumpled parchment standing well above the rim, since it climbs a lot during the bake.
- Stir, never whisk, once the cheese is in.
- The 8 hour chill is not optional if you want clean slices.
