# Cheesecake Shortbread Crust

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 x 20 cm base, 4-5 mm thick
- Active time: 15 min
- Oven: 320F for 10-12 min, blind
- Energy cost: $1.45 (12 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: classic shortbread, as paired with the Alex Cordobes filling

## Ingredients

- All-purpose flour: 150 g
- Cold butter, cubed: 75 g
- Sugar: 30 g
- Salt: a pinch

## Method

1. Combine flour, sugar and salt, then add the cold cubed butter.
2. Rub in with your fingertips to coarse sand with some pea-sized butter left. Work fast to keep it cold.
3. Press firmly and evenly into the lined tin, about 4 to 5 mm thick, compacting with a glass.
4. Blind bake 10 to 12 minutes at 320F until lightly golden at the edges.
5. Cool slightly while you make the filling.

## Notes

- The alternative to the Maria cookie base, and the reason to pick it is a real pastry crust rather than a crumb one.
- Unlike the cookie base, this one gets a blind bake, which is what stops it going soggy under a wet filling.
- Leaving pea-sized pieces of butter in is deliberate, so stop short of a uniform powder.
