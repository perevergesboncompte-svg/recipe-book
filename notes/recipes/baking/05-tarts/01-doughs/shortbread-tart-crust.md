# Shortbread Tart Crust

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 x 20cm base, 4-5mm thick
- Active time: 15 min
- Oven: 320F, 10-12 min blind
- Energy cost: $1.45 (12 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: Classic shortbread, as paired with the Alex Cordobés cheesecake

## Ingredients

- All-purpose flour: 150 g
- Cold butter, cubed: 75 g
- Sugar: 30 g
- Salt: a pinch

## Method

1. Combine flour, sugar and salt, then add the cold cubed butter.
2. Rub in with your fingertips to coarse sand, leaving some pea-sized butter pieces. Work quickly so the butter stays cold.
3. Press firmly and evenly into the lined tin, compacting with the bottom of a glass, about 4 to 5 mm thick.
4. Blind bake 10 to 12 minutes at 320F until lightly golden at the edges.
5. Cool slightly while you make the filling.

## Notes

- Unlike the Maria cookie base, this one gets pre-baked, which is what keeps it from going soggy under a wet filling.
- Pea-sized butter pieces left in are deliberate, so do not rub it to a uniform powder.
