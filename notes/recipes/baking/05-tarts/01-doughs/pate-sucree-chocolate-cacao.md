# Pate Sucree, Chocolate Cacao Method

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 x 18 cm tart shell
- Rest: 8 h wrapped, then 1 h in the tin
- Oven: preheat 170C, bake 160C for 35 min, then 140C for 5 min after the yolk wash
- Source: Chocolate Cacao, youtube.com/@chocolate_cacao

## Ingredients

- Unsalted butter: 75 g
- Almond flour: 25 g
- Sugar: 50 g
- Salt: 1 g
- Whole egg: 30 g
- Wheat flour: 125 g
- Egg yolk, to seal the baked shell: as needed

## Method

1. Sift the almond flour, sugar and salt together.
2. Melt the butter and whisk it.
3. Add the sifted dry mix in several additions, mixing well after each.
4. Beat the egg and add it in 3 to 5 goes, a little at a time.
5. Mix until it cuts cleanly with a rubber spatula.
6. Add the sifted wheat flour in 2 or 3 additions.
7. Wrap with a little flour and rest 8 hours.
8. Roll out with a dusting of flour. No kneading.
9. Line the tin, prick with a fork, rest 1 hour.
10. Lay foil in and fill with baking stones. Foil holds the shape better than parchment here.
11. Preheat to 170C, bake at 160C for 35 minutes. Give it longer if it is pale.
12. Lift out the stones and foil, then brush egg yolk over the inside.
13. Bake 5 minutes at 140C to set that seal, then cool.

## Notes

- Melted butter rather than cold, which is why there is no rubbing-in stage and no kneading.
- 25 g almond flour against 125 g wheat flour, so a fifth of the dry weight, giving a shorter and more fragrant shell than plain sucree.
- The yolk wash after blind baking is the waterproofing step. Skip it and a wet ganache will soften the base.
- Drop the oven below the preheat temperature on loading, 170C down to 160C.
- Two rests, 8 hours wrapped and 1 hour once lined, and both matter for a shell that holds its walls.
