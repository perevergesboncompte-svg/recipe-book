# Pâte Brisée

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 x 23cm tart base, serves 6
- Active time: 10 min
- Rest: 30 min to 1 h
- Source: French Pâtisserie, Ferrandi Paris (Flammarion, 2017)

## Ingredients

- All-purpose flour, sifted: 125 g
- Unsalted butter, diced and well chilled: 75 g
- Egg, lightly beaten: 40 g
- Sugar: 6 g
- Salt: 2.5 g

## Method

1. Paddle the sifted flour, diced butter, sugar and salt on low 30 to 45 seconds, to the texture of coarse sand with the butter evenly coated.
2. Scrape the bowl down.
3. Add the beaten egg in a steady stream on low, mixing only until it gathers into a smooth mass. This takes 15 to 20 seconds.
4. Stop the moment it is smooth. Overworking makes it elastic and tough.
5. Shape into a disk by hand, handling it as little as possible.
6. Wrap tight and chill at least 30 minutes.
7. Let it sit 2 to 3 minutes before rolling so it does not crack. Roll to an even 3 mm, rotating and dusting as needed.
8. Line the ring, pressing gently into the flutes.

## Notes

- Only 6 g of sugar, so this is the savoury-capable one. Use it where the filling carries the sweetness.
- The butter wants to be well chilled but not frozen, unlike pâte sablée.
- Keeps 3 days refrigerated, well covered.
