# Whole Wheat Galette Crust

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 galette, serves 6
- Source: Claire Saffitz, Dessert Person

## Ingredients

- Whole wheat flour: 1 cup
- All-purpose flour: 1/2 cup
- Sugar: 2 tsp
- Diamond Crystal kosher salt: 1 tsp
- Unsalted butter, chilled, in 1/2 inch pieces: 10 tbsp
- Ice water: 1/2 cup

## Notes

- Two thirds whole wheat to one third all-purpose, which is where the nutty flavour comes from.
- 10 tbsp of butter to 1.5 cups of flour is a high fat ratio, and the 1/2 inch pieces are meant to stay visible. Those sheets of butter are what make it flaky rather than short.
- Ice water, and chilled butter, both non-negotiable for flakiness.
- The source is a video. Its description lists these quantities but not the mixing steps, so the method below is the standard sequence for a flaky pastry of this type rather than a transcript.

## Method

1. Whisk the flours, sugar and salt together.
2. Toss the chilled butter through the flour and work it in until the pieces are flattened but still visible. Do not rub it to sand.
3. Add ice water a little at a time, tossing, until it just holds together.
4. Shape to a disc, wrap, and chill before rolling.
