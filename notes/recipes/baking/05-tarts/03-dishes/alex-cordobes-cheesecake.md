# Alex Cordobes Cheesecake

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Dough: maria-cookie-base, cheesecake-shortbread-crust
- Filling: basque-cheesecake
- Yield: 1 x 20 cm cake, 8 servings
- Active time: 20-30 min depending on the crust
- Oven: 320F, 40 min
- Rest: 8 h chill
- Source: Alex Cordobes, Madrid, alexcordobes.es

## Method

1. Pick a base. The Maria cookie base is faster, the shortbread crust is a real pastry and needs a 10 to 12 minute blind bake first.
2. Line a 20 cm springform with crumpled parchment standing well above the rim.
3. Press the base in and compact it.
4. Make the filling, pour it over the base.
5. Bake at 320F for 40 minutes.
6. Chill 8 hours before slicing.

## Notes

- Basque style, so a dark scorched top is the goal rather than a fault.
- Total elapsed time is dominated by the 8 hour chill, so this is a day-ahead cake.
- Filed under tarts because it is a crust plus a filling, which is the same structure, even though it is a cake by name.
