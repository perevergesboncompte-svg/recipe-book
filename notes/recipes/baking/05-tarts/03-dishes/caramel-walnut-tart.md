# Caramel Walnut Tart

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 x 23 cm tart
- Unit weight: 150 g
- Active time: 30 min
- Rest: 30 min chilled, plus cooling
- Oven: 350F to blind bake, then 325F for the filling, both my own estimates
- Price: $6
- Tags: vegetarian
- Source: bakery site recipes.html

## Ingredients

- Crust — flour: 200 g
- Crust — butter, cold and cubed: 150 g
- Filling — walnuts: 200 g
- Filling — caramel: 200 g
- Filling — cream: 100 ml
- Filling — eggs: 3

## Method

1. Rub the cold butter into the flour with your fingertips until it looks like coarse crumbs. Bring it together with a splash of cold water, no more than it needs.
2. Flatten into a disc, wrap and chill 30 minutes.
3. Roll out and line a 23 cm tin. Chill again while the oven comes up to 350F, then prick the base.
4. Line with paper and weights and blind bake 15 to 20 minutes. Lift the paper away and give it another 5 to 10 minutes, until the base is dry and pale gold.
5. Drop the oven to 325F.
6. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan until they smell nutty, then chop them roughly. Leave some pieces whole for texture.
7. Warm the caramel with the cream, stirring until it is smooth and pours easily. Take it off the heat and let it cool to barely warm, or it will scramble the eggs.
8. Whisk the eggs into the cooled caramel one at a time.
9. Scatter the walnuts over the base of the shell and pour the caramel custard over them.
10. Bake 25 to 35 minutes, until set at the edge with a slight wobble at the centre.
11. Cool completely before slicing.

## Notes

- The source list gives no crust and filling split. All the butter goes to the crust here, which works because the caramel already carries its own fat, so the filling needs none.
- At 150 g butter to 200 g flour the crust is very rich and will handle softly. Keep it cold and work fast.
- Caramel is listed as a finished 200 g rather than as sugar to cook, so this assumes a jar or a batch made ahead.
- Let the caramel cool before the eggs go in. Straight off the heat it will curdle them.
- Both oven temperatures are mine. The source records none.
- Toasting the walnuts is worth the five minutes, and it stops them going soft under the custard.
