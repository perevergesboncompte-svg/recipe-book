## Ingredients

- Peaches, washed and cut: quantity not recorded
- Sugar, for macerating: quantity not recorded

## Method

1. Make and chill the galette crust.
2. Wash and cut the peaches, toss them with some sugar, and leave them to macerate in the fridge before using.
3. Drain them before they go on the pastry.
4. Arrange over the rolled-out crust, fold the border over, and bake.

## Notes

- Macerating first is the point. Sugar pulls water out of the peaches in the fridge, so it ends up in the bowl rather than in the pastry.
- Drain what the maceration releases. Tipping it onto the crust undoes the whole step.
- Quantities were not recorded. The peach weight, the sugar, the oven temperature and the bake time all still need filling in.
- Crust is assumed to be the same whole wheat galette dough as the zucchini one, since that is the galette dough in this collection. Only the peach treatment was specified.
