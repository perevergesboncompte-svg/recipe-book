# Millionaire's Chocolate Caramel Tart

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Dough: chocolate-buckwheat-tart-dough
- Makes: 1 tart at 23 cm or 6 to 8 tartlets at 10 cm, 8 to 12 servings
- Active time: 40 min
- Rest: 2 h for the caramel to set, then 2 h for the chocolate
- Total time: about 5 h, not counting the shell
- Source: Tartine, A Classic Revisited

## Ingredients

- Chocolate buckwheat tart dough, baked and cooled: 1 batch
- Heavy cream, for the caramel: 160 ml
- Water, for the caramel: 2 tbsp
- Corn syrup, for the caramel: 85 ml
- Sugar, for the caramel: 150 g
- Unsalted butter, cold, for the caramel: 60 g
- Flaky sea salt, such as Maldon: 1 tsp
- Bittersweet chocolate, in chunks, for the ganache: 180 g
- Heavy cream, for the ganache: 225 ml
- Corn syrup, for the ganache: 2 tbsp
- Unsalted butter, at room temperature, for the ganache: 2 tbsp

## Method

1. Make the chocolate buckwheat tart dough, line a 23 cm tart pan or six to eight 10 cm tartlet pans, bake the shells through and cool them. See the dough.
2. Stand the cooled shell on a baking sheet, ready to fill.
3. For the caramel, put the 160 ml of cream in a small heavy pan over medium heat and take it to just short of a boil. Turn the burner to its lowest setting and hold it there warm.
4. Put the water, the 85 ml of corn syrup and the sugar in a medium heavy pan. Bring it to a boil over medium heat, swirling the pan gently until no sugar grain is left.
5. Now leave it alone, no stirring, 7 to 8 minutes, until the syrup is a deep amber. Once the color starts to come it will scorch in seconds, so stay with the pan.
6. Off the heat, drop in half the cold butter and swirl it through, then pour the warm cream in slowly. Expect it to boil up hard.
7. Wait for the bubbling to drop back, whisk the caramel smooth, then whisk in the rest of the butter.
8. Return the pan to medium-low heat and keep the caramel at a gentle boil for 30 seconds to thicken it. Take it off the heat.
9. Pour the caramel into the shell and give it about 2 hours to cool and set.
10. Scatter the flaky salt evenly across the surface of the caramel.
11. For the ganache, bring 5 cm of water to a bare simmer in a medium pan over medium-low heat.
12. Pick a medium heatproof bowl that sits firmly on the pan rim and melt the chocolate in it, held clear of the water, stirring it smooth with a rubber spatula. Lift the bowl off.
13. Bring the 225 ml of cream and the remaining 2 tbsp of corn syrup to a simmer in a small pan over medium heat.
14. Pour the hot cream onto the chocolate and stir gently with the spatula, working as little air in as you can, until the ganache is smooth. Stir the soft butter through.
15. Pour the ganache over the salted caramel. Turn, tilt and tap the pan as needed to settle it into an even layer.
16. Leave the tart at room temperature about 2 hours, until the chocolate has set.
17. Cut with a knife warmed in hot water and wiped dry before every cut, and serve the tart at room temperature.

## Notes

- Take the caramel to a properly dark amber. A pale caramel has not cooked the sweetness out of the sugar and the finished tart turns cloying.
- The caramel's 60 g of butter goes in cold and in two halves, one before the cream and one after. The ganache butter is a separate 2 tbsp and wants to be soft.
- The caramel gets its full 2 hours of setting before the salt and the ganache go on.
- Keeps wrapped at room temperature up to 3 days.
- The same quantities fill six to eight 10 cm tartlet shells. A 23 cm tart is rich enough to cut into slivers.
- The caramel's corn syrup is printed as both 1/4 cup and 85 ml, which cannot both be right, since a quarter cup is 60 ml. Corn syrup is dense enough that a quarter cup weighs about 85 g, so the second figure is almost certainly a weight printed under a volume heading. Weigh out 85 g and the two agree.
