# Lemon Tart

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 1 x 23 cm tart
- Unit weight: 150 g
- Active time: 30 min
- Rest: 30 min chilled, plus cooling
- Oven: 350F to blind bake, then 300F for the filling, both my own estimates
- Price: $5
- Tags: vegetarian
- Source: bakery site recipes.html

## Ingredients

- Crust — flour: 200 g
- Crust — butter, cold and cubed: 150 g
- Filling — lemons: 4
- Filling — eggs: 4
- Filling — sugar: 200 g

## Method

1. Rub the cold butter into the flour with your fingertips until it looks like coarse crumbs. Bring it together with a splash of cold water, no more than it needs.
2. Flatten into a disc, wrap and chill 30 minutes.
3. Roll out and line a 23 cm tin. Chill again while the oven comes up to 350F, then prick the base.
4. Line with paper and weights and blind bake 15 to 20 minutes. Lift the paper away and give it another 5 to 10 minutes, until the base is dry and pale gold.
5. Drop the oven to 300F.
6. Zest two of the lemons and juice all four. Whisk the eggs with the sugar, then stir in the zest and juice. Let it stand a few minutes and skim the foam off the top.
7. Pour into the shell while it is still warm and bake 25 to 35 minutes. It is done when the edge is set and the centre still has a slight wobble.
8. Cool completely before slicing, or it will not cut cleanly.

## Notes

- The source list gives no crust and filling split, so this reading puts all the butter in the crust and all the sugar in the filling. That makes a plain unsweetened shortcrust and a sharp baked lemon custard with no butter in it.
- At 150 g butter to 200 g flour the crust is very rich and will handle softly. Keep it cold and work fast.
- If it comes out too sharp for you, move 50 g of the butter into the filling and 30 g of the sugar into the crust next time. That shifts it towards a sweet crust and a mellower curd.
- Both oven temperatures are mine. The source records none, and neither does the file it came from.
- Skimming the foam is what keeps the surface clear rather than blotchy.
