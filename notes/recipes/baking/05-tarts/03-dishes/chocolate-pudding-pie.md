# Chocolate Pudding Pie

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Base: gluten-free-crumb-crust
- Filling: chocolate-pudding
- Makes: 1 pie at 23 cm, 8 to 10 servings
- Active time: 20 min for the assembly
- Rest: 1 1/2 h chilled to set the pudding, then 1 h chilled to set the cream
- Total time: about 3 h, not counting the shell
- Source: Tartine, A Classic Revisited

## Ingredients

- Gluten-free crumb crust, 23 cm, baked and cooled: 1 batch
- Chocolate pudding: 800 g
- Heavy cream, very cold: 360 ml
- Vanilla extract: 2 tsp
- Confectioners' sugar: 1 tbsp
- Bittersweet chocolate, chopped or shaved: 60 g

## Method

1. Make the gluten-free crumb crust in a 23 cm pie pan, bake it through and let it cool. See the base.
2. Set the cooled shell where you can reach it before starting the pudding.
3. Make the chocolate pudding to its own recipe and pour it into the shell straight away, as soon as it comes off the heat.
4. Chill the pie until the surface has gone cold and firm, about 1 1/2 hours.
5. Freeze the mixer bowl and whisk — or a medium bowl, if you are whipping by hand — for 5 minutes.
6. Add the cold cream, the vanilla and the confectioners' sugar to the chilled bowl.
7. Whip on medium speed, or hard by hand, until the cream holds peaks somewhere between medium and firm.
8. Spread the cream over the set pudding with an offset or rubber spatula, taking it right out to the crust.
9. Scatter the chopped chocolate or the shavings evenly across the cream.
10. Chill about 1 hour, until the cream has set. Serve the pie cool.

## Notes

- The pie takes 800 g of pudding and a full batch comes to 1.02 kg, so about 220 g is spare.
- The pudding has to go in while it is warm, so the crust must be baked and cooled before you start it.
- Cold, this pudding sets to roughly the texture of pot de crème and holds a spoon upright, which is what makes the pie sliceable rather than spoonable.
- Keeps in the refrigerator up to 4 days.
