# Danish Style Butter Cookies

- Section: baking
- Status: tested
- Yield: 34 cookies
- Oven: 340F, 12-15 min
- Rest: 20-30 min chill after piping
- Appearance: 3.5
- Texture: 3.5
- Flavor: 3.5
- Technique: easy
- Overall: 3.5
- Source: The Cooking Foodie, thecookingfoodie.com

## Ingredients

- Unsalted butter, room temperature: 230 g
- Powdered sugar: 85 g
- All-purpose flour: 200 g
- Cornstarch: 45 g
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Vanilla extract: 5 ml

## Method

1. Beat the butter and vanilla 2 to 3 minutes until light and fluffy.
2. Add the powdered sugar, beat smooth.
3. Add the sifted flour, cornstarch and salt in two additions, low speed, just until combined. The dough should be soft but not sticky.
4. Pipe 5 cm rosettes through a large open star tip.
5. Chill the piped cookies 20 to 30 minutes so they hold the ridges.
6. Bake one sheet at a time at 340F for 12 to 15 minutes, edges just lightly golden.
7. Cool completely on the pan, they are delicate warm.

## Notes

- Cornstarch again for the melt-in-the-mouth texture, here at 45 g against 200 g flour.
- Chilling after piping is what preserves the star ridges.
- Wants 82 to 85 percent butterfat European butter.
- Keeps a week airtight, or two months frozen.

## Learnings

Flat 3.5 across appearance, texture and flavor, the lowest of the five butter cookies made. Easy, but the Milk Powder version is the same effort for a full point more.
