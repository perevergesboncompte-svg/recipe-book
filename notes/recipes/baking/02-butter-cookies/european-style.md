# European Style Butter Cookies

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 60 cookies
- Oven: 325F, 18 min
- Rest: 1 h minimum as chilled logs
- Source: Pinch and Swirl, pinchandswirl.com

## Ingredients

- All-purpose flour: 250 g
- Baking powder: 3 g
- Fine sea salt: 1.5 g
- Salted European style butter, about 85% fat: 226 g
- Granulated sugar: 130 g
- Egg yolk: 1
- Demerara sugar, for coating: 55 g

## Method

1. Cream the butter and sugar about 3 minutes until light.
2. Mix in the egg yolk.
3. Add the sifted flour, baking powder and salt, mix to just combined.
4. Divide in half and roll each into a log about 1.5 inches across. Brush off loose flour.
5. Roll each log through demerara sugar spread on parchment.
6. Wrap tight and chill at least 1 hour.
7. Slice into 1/4 inch rounds, 1 inch apart.
8. Bake at 325F for 18 minutes until golden underneath and at the edges.
9. Cool 5 minutes, then move to a rack.

## Notes

- A slice-and-bake log rather than piped, the only one of its kind in the group.
- Do not flour the work surface when rolling the logs, or the sugar will not stick.
- Logs freeze well. Slice from frozen and add a minute.
- With unsalted butter, raise the salt to about 1/2 tsp.
- Keeps 2 weeks airtight.
