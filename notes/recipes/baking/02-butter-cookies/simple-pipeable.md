# Simple Pipeable Butter Cookies

- Section: baking
- Status: tested
- Yield: 26 cookies
- Oven: 375F, 10-12 min
- Appearance: 4
- Texture: 4
- Flavor: 4.25
- Technique: easy
- Overall: 4.25
- Source: Sugar Spun Run, sugarspunrun.com

## Ingredients

- Unsalted butter, softened: 226 g
- Granulated sugar: 150 g
- Egg yolk: 1
- Vanilla extract: 7.5 ml
- Salt: 2 g
- All-purpose flour: 265 g

## Method

1. Beat the butter and sugar on high several minutes until creamy.
2. Add the yolk, vanilla and salt, beat well, then scrape the bowl down.
3. Add the flour gradually on low until combined.
4. Raise to high and keep mixing several minutes more. Thoroughly mixed dough is what makes it pipeable.
5. Pipe swirls through an Ateco 827 onto an unlined, ungreased, cold sheet.
6. Bake at 375F for 10 to 12 minutes, until the edges just start to colour.
7. Cool 10 minutes on the sheet before moving.

## Notes

- Only six ingredients and no chill at all, the fastest butter cookie in the group.
- A large tip is not optional with this stiff dough. Warming the bag in your hands helps.
- Without a tip, drop spoonfuls and fork criss-cross them instead.
- Never pipe onto a warm sheet.
- Keeps 2 to 3 weeks airtight.

## Learnings

4.25 overall, second best of the butter cookies. Flavor 4.25 edges out appearance and texture at 4. Easy, and with no chill step it is the one to reach for when there is no time.
