# Pan Shortbread

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 16 pieces
- Oven: 325F, 35-40 min
- Energy cost: $2.41 (40 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: King Arthur Baking, kingarthurbaking.com

## Ingredients

- Salted butter, cool room temperature: 227 g
- Powdered sugar: 113 g
- Vanilla extract: 7.5 ml
- Table salt: 1 g
- All-purpose flour: 240 g

## Method

1. Beat the butter, sugar, vanilla and salt together.
2. Beat in the flour. It looks dry at first, so keep going until a stiff dough forms. Up to a tablespoon of water will bring it together if needed.
3. Press into a greased and parchment-lined 9 inch round or 8 inch square pan, smoothing the top.
4. Dock all over with a fork so steam escapes and it does not bubble.
5. Bake at 325F for 35 to 40 minutes, light golden on top and deeper at the edges.
6. Cool in the pan 5 to 10 minutes, cut while still slightly warm, then cool on a rack.

## Notes

- Pressed into a pan, so no rolling, cutting or chilling. The simplest route to shortbread here.
- Salted butter plus added salt is deliberate. With unsalted, raise salt to about 1/2 tsp.
- Flavour improves with age.
- Takes additions well: crystallized ginger, or dip half in chocolate.
- Keeps a week wrapped, freezes longer.
