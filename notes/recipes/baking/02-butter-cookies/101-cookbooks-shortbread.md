# 101 Cookbooks Shortbread

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 30 cookies
- Oven: 350F, 20-30 min
- Rest: 2 h minimum, plus 15-30 min after cutting
- Source: 101 Cookbooks, 101cookbooks.com

## Ingredients

- Cold unsalted butter, sliced: 454 g
- Granulated sugar: 100 g
- Powdered sugar: 65 g
- Fine grain sea salt: 5 g
- Unbleached all-purpose flour: 500 g

## Method

1. Cream the cold sliced butter until soft.
2. Add both sugars and the salt, mix until billowy and fluffy, scraping down once or twice.
3. Add the flour in two additions on low, just until combined.
4. Form a ball, press into a thick disc, wrap and chill at least a couple of hours.
5. Roll to 3/4 inch thick and cut shapes. Re-roll the scraps.
6. Dock the larger ones with a fork or skewer.
7. Arrange an inch apart, then chill another 15 to 30 minutes to stop spread.
8. Bake 20 minutes, rotate, then 10 to 15 more until deeply golden where they meet the pan.
9. Cool 5 minutes, then move to a rack.

## Notes

- Two sugars, granulated and powdered, unlike the other shortbreads.
- Roll it thick. Thin means dry.
- Take the colour further than feels right, since that toasted butter flavour is the point.
- Flavour keeps developing for several days, so make it ahead.
- Dough freezes for months, double wrapped.
- The file this came from was named "classic shortbread", but the recipe is the 101 Cookbooks one. Preppy Kitchen's Classic Shortbread is the one missing from the download.
