# Scottish Shortbread

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Yield: 12-16 pieces
- Oven: 375F, 25-30 min
- Energy cost: $2.06 (30 min bake plus 30 min, 3.5 kW at 59 cents/kWh)
- Source: Christina's Cucina, christinascucina.com

## Ingredients

- Salted butter, softened: 225 g
- Granulated sugar: 100 g
- All-purpose flour: 310 g

## Method

1. Mix the butter and sugar together but do NOT cream them. Amalgamate only, a few minutes, until it looks like sandy crumbs.
2. Add the flour and mix to a stiff consistency. Stop as soon as it comes together.
3. Roll out on a lightly floured surface to the shape of the pan.
4. Transfer to a lightly greased 8x8 pan or a sheet, trim the edges. For fingers, cut 3/4 inch strips.
5. Prick about halfway through with a fork.
6. Bake at 375F for 25 to 30 minutes, only very slightly browned at the edges. It should not go dark.
7. Cool in the pan before moving.

## Notes

- Three ingredients, and the refusal to cream is the entire technique. Creaming aerates, which is wrong for this texture.
- Highest oven of the shortbreads at 375F, yet the target is pale, so watch it rather than trusting the clock.
- Nothing to hide behind, so butter quality decides the result.
- Weigh rather than measure by cup.
- Keeps for weeks in a sealed tin and freezes well.
