# Cake aux Olives

- Section: baking
- Status: drafted
- Makes: 1 loaf, 23 by 12 cm, 6 to 8 servings
- Active time: 20 min
- Total time: about 2 h 30 min
- Oven: 325F for about 1 h
- Source: Tartine, A Classic Revisited

## Ingredients

- Bread flour: 275 g
- Baking powder: 2 1/2 tsp
- White wine: 120 ml
- Dry white vermouth: 120 ml
- Eggs, large: 4
- Olive oil: 210 ml
- Ham, chopped: 200 g
- Gruyère cheese, grated: 140 g
- Olives, pitted and coarsely chopped: 115 g
- Thyme leaves, fresh: 1 1/2 tbsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper, freshly ground: 1/2 tsp

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 325F. Butter the base and sides of a 23 by 12 cm loaf pan lightly, then line it with parchment and leave several centimetres standing above the rim.
2. Tip in the baking powder, flour, wine, vermouth and eggs. A stand mixer on the paddle handles this, and so does a bowl and a wooden spoon. Work on medium until the mixture pulls together.
3. Keep the mixer on medium and pour the olive oil in slowly, as a thin steady trickle, until it has all been taken up and the dough looks elastic.
4. Toss the olives, ham, Gruyère, thyme, salt and pepper together in a second bowl.
5. Fold that mixture through the batter with a spatula or a wooden spoon, stopping the moment it is evenly distributed.
6. Scrape the batter into the lined pan and level the top with an offset spatula.
7. Bake about 1 hour, until a cake tester comes out of the centre clean.
8. Leave the loaf in the pan on a wire rack about 20 minutes, then turn it out, set it the right way up and let it go completely cold.
9. Serve at room temperature.

## Notes

- This is the rare batter you want to work rather than protect. Mix until the dough shows elasticity and strings off the paddle as you lift it.
- The 210 ml of olive oil is what stops all that gluten development turning the crumb tough.
- The parchment overhang gives you handles, which a heavy oil-rich loaf needs to come out of the pan in one piece.
- Wrapped well, it holds 2 days at room temperature or about a week in the refrigerator.
