# Whole Grain Sourdough

- Status: stub

- Makes: 1 loaf

- Oven: 475F, 55-60 min total

## Ingredients

- Bread flour: 300 g
- Whole wheat flour: 200 g
- Water: 350 g
- Salt: 10 g
- Active starter: quantity not recorded yet

## Method

1. Roll the dough up, giving it tension as you go.
2. Leave it seam side up and rest 2 hours at room temperature.
3. Preheat to 475F.
4. Bake 20 minutes with the lid on.
5. Remove the lid and bake 30 minutes more.
6. Finish 5 to 10 minutes out of the dutch oven.

## Notes

- 475F, hotter than the high hydration sourdough at 450F, and the final stint comes out of the pot entirely.
- 200 g of whole wheat against 300 g bread flour is 40% wholegrain.
- 350 g water to 500 g total flour is 70% hydration before the starter, stiffer than the 75% high hydration loaf.
